# How to identify the Red-whiskered Bulbul (*Pycnonotus jocosus*)

Look for a medium-sized brown bulbul with a pointed black crest, a red cheek mark and a dark patch reaching onto the breast. The tail has white ends, while the area beneath it is red. It often turns up in pairs or small parties in gardens, orchards and open woodland, where its clear, varied calls may reveal it before it is seen.
