# How to identify the Splendid Fairywren (*Malurus splendens*)

Look for a small bird with a slender body and a long blue tail usually carried upright. Breeding males are vivid blue with bold black markings on the face and chest; females are brownish with a rusty bill. Splendid Fairywrens live in dry, shrubby country across much of Australia, including the southwest, where they forage close to cover.
