# How to identify the Garden Warbler (*Sylvia borin*)

Look for a small, plain warbler with brown upperparts, pale underparts and a subtle light ring around the eye. It can be hard to pick out among leaves and shrubs, where it often stays quiet and still. Garden Warblers breed across much of Europe and temperate Asia, then migrate to sub-Saharan Africa for winter. Despite its name, it is not especially associated with gardens.
