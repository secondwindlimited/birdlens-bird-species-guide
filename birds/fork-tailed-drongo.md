# How to identify the Fork-tailed Drongo (*Dicrurus adsimilis*)

Look for a glossy, all-black bird with bright red eyes, a sturdy bill and a long tail that ends in two narrow points. It often perches upright in open woodland or savanna, including near grazing animals, then darts out to catch insects. Fork-tailed Drongos are widespread south of the Sahara and stay in their range throughout the year.
