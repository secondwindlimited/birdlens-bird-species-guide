# How to identify the Great Horned Owl (*Bubo virginianus*)

A bulky owl with upright feather tufts, a rounded face and mottled brown plumage. Look for it perched in a tree by woodland, open country or even a town; it is active after dark and may be heard calling. Its deep hoots can answer one another in a duet, with a male and female calling back and forth.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Great Horned Owl |
| Scientific name | *Bubo virginianus* |
| Order | Strigiformes |
| Family | Strigidae |
| Genus | *Bubo* |
