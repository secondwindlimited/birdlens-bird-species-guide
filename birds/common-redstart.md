# How to identify the Common Redstart (*Phoenicurus phoenicurus*)

The Common Redstart is easiest to pick out by its orange-red tail, which it often quivers while perched. Breeding males have a black face, white forehead and orange breast; females are plainer, with a warm buff underside. It breeds in open woodland and wooded heath across much of Europe, then travels to eastern and northeastern Africa for the winter.
