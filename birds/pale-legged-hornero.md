# How to identify the Pale-legged Hornero (*Furnarius leucopus*)

Look for a plump, thrush-sized hornero with rusty upperparts, pale buff underparts, and a conspicuous light eyebrow. It usually walks boldly on the ground in open country, often beside rivers, though it also turns up in clearings and around settlements. A loud, descending run of coarse notes from a high perch is a useful clue. Its range spans lowland parts of much of South America.
