# How to identify the White-throated Flowerpecker (*Pachyglossa vincens*)

Look for a tiny, compact bird with a blunt, heavy bill, a very short tail and a pale throat above a yellow belly. Adult males show glossy blue-black upperparts; females are browner and less sharply marked. White-throated Flowerpeckers are most often found in pairs in Sri Lanka’s humid forests, especially among the canopy and nearby wooded growth.
