# How to identify the Arctic Loon (*Gavia arctica*)

A medium-sized, streamlined loon with a straight, pointed bill, the Arctic Loon is easiest to pick out by the white patch near its rear flank, visible even when it swims. In summer, its gray head, dark throat and fine white neck stripes are distinctive. It breeds across northern Eurasia and far western Alaska, then spends winter mainly in sheltered coastal waters.
