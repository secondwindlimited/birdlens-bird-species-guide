# How to identify the Western Rosella (*Platycercus icterotis*)

The Western Rosella is a small parrot with a red head and body, blue wings and rump, and a yellow cheek marking in adults. Males are brighter than females; young birds are greener and lack the yellow. It lives only in southwestern Western Australia, where it is often seen feeding on the ground in woodland, farmland, and parks.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Western Rosella |
| Scientific name | *Platycercus icterotis* |

| Family | Psittaculidae |
| Genus | *Platycercus* |
