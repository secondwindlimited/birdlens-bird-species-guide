# How to identify the Andean Cock-of-the-rock (*Rupicola peruvianus*)

This large, robust cotinga is easy to pick out when a male is showing: look for vivid scarlet or orange plumage, dark wings and a rounded crest. Females are brown and less conspicuous. The species lives in Andean cloud forest, where males gather at dawn to call and display. It may also appear briefly at fruiting trees or as it flies through a valley.
