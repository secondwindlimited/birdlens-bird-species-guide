# How to identify the Great Egret (*Ardea alba*)

A tall, slim white heron with a long neck and dark legs, the Great Egret is often recognised by its size among other white wading birds. Its bill is usually yellow in the Americas; elsewhere it darkens during the breeding season. Look for it at wetlands around the world, standing or moving through shallow water as it searches for food.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Great Egret |
| Scientific name | *Ardea alba* |
| Order | Pelecaniformes |
| Family | Ardeidae |
| Genus | *Ardea* |
