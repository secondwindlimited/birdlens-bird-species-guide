# How to identify the Long-toed Lapwing (*Vanellus crassirostris*)

A large, long-legged lapwing with a white face and throat, dark breast and nape, red bill, and strikingly long toes. Look for it around lakes, marshes, floodplains, and other wetlands across central and eastern Africa. It is usually seen alone or with one other bird, often standing on floating plants or near the edge of a pool.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Long-toed Lapwing |
