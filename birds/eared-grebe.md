# How to identify the Eared Grebe (*Podiceps nigricollis*)

The Eared Grebe is a small, compact waterbird with a narrow, pointed bill and a steeply peaked head. In breeding plumage, black feathers, chestnut sides and golden tufts behind the eyes make it distinctive. In winter it is greyer, with a dark cap and cheek. Look for it on marshy ponds and lakes in the breeding season, then on larger lakes, sheltered bays or open water during migration and winter.
