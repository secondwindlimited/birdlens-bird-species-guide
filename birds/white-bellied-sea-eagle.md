# How to identify the White-bellied Sea-Eagle (*Icthyophaga leucogaster*)

A very large eagle with a white head and underside, dark grey upperparts, and a short tail. It often circles with its wings raised in a shallow V; dark flight feathers show clearly from below. Adults are distinctive, while brown young birds may be mistaken for Wedge-tailed Eagles. Look for it around coasts, estuaries and waterways, and sometimes far inland.
