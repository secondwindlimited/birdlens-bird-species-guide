# How to identify the Taveuni Silktail (*Lamprolia victoriae*)

Look for a small, dark bird with a very short, rounded tail and a startling white patch across the rump and upper tail. Blue flecks on the head, back and chest can flash brightly in sunlight. The species occurs only on Taveuni, Fiji, where it uses forest. Listen for a long, plaintive whistle with a squeaky-wheel quality.
