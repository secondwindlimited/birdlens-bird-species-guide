# How to identify the Bicknell's Thrush (*Catharus bicknelli*)

Bicknell’s Thrush is a small, shy brown thrush with a spotted breast, a faint eye-ring and a subtle rusty tone in the tail and wings. It breeds in remote, high-elevation spruce forests in northeastern Canada and the northeastern United States. During migration it can be hard to find in dense woodland; it spends the winter mainly on Hispaniola.
