# How to identify the Great Spotted Kiwi (*Apteryx maxima*)

The Great Spotted Kiwi is a large, round-bodied, flightless bird with shaggy grey-brown feathers marked by pale bars and flecks. Its long, narrow bill and lack of a visible tail help separate it from the Weka. It lives in the northwestern South Island of New Zealand and is more often heard than spotted, especially as it searches for food after dark.
