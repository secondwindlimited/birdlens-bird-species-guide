# How to identify the Bare-headed Laughingthrush (*Melanocichla calva*)

A stocky, dark brown-gray bird with a conspicuously bare yellowish head, blue marks below the jaw and a vivid red-orange bill. It occurs only in Borneo’s north-central highlands, where it moves through forest tangles, often with other birds. Listen for a run of deep, hollow hoots, sometimes answered by higher, rising calls.
