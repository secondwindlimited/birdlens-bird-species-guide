# How to identify the Australasian Gannet (*Morus serrator*)

A large seabird with a white body, dark wing tips and a pale yellow wash on its head. It often gives itself away by folding its wings and plunging into the sea for fish. Look for it around southern Australian coasts and New Zealand, especially near offshore breeding colonies; birds may also appear in bays and harbours.
