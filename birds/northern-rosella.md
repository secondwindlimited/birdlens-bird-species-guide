# How to identify the Northern Rosella (*Platycercus venustus*)

The Northern Rosella is a small, pale-yellow parrot with a black head, white cheek patches, blue wings and tail, and red beneath the tail. It occurs in northern Australia, often in open woodland close to water. Look for it in pairs or small groups, or listen for its clear, repeated two-note call when birds are hidden among the trees.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Northern Rosella |
| Scientific name | *Platycercus venustus* |
| Order | Psittaciformes |
| Family | Psittaculidae |
| Genus | *Platycercus* |
