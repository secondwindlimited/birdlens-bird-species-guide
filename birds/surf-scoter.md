# How to identify the Surf Scoter (*Melanitta perspicillata*)

Look for a stocky, dark sea duck with a broad, deep-based bill. Adult males are black, with white marks on the forehead and rear of the head, and a strikingly patterned, swollen bill. Females and young birds are brown with pale facial marks. Most sightings are on coastal water in winter; inland encounters are uncommon.
