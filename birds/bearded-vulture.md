# How to identify the Bearded Vulture (*Gypaetus barbatus*)

A huge mountain raptor, the Bearded Vulture is easiest to pick out in flight by its long, slim wings and extended, pointed tail. Adults show grey upperparts and rusty orange colouring beneath, with a dark face and beard; young birds are much darker. Look for it gliding beside ridges or over cliffs in remote highlands across parts of Europe, Asia and Africa.

| Scientific name | *Gypaetus barbatus* |
