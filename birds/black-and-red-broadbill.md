# How to identify the Black-and-red Broadbill (*Cymbirhynchus macrorhynchos*)

Look for a stocky, dark broadbill beside lowland rivers: its vivid wine-red belly and rump stand out against a black head and back, while white marks stripe the folded wings. The oversized blue-and-yellow bill is especially distinctive. It occurs in river-fringing forest, mangroves and some waterside plantations across Southeast Asia, where it is resident.
