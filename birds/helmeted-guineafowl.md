# How to identify the Helmeted Guineafowl (*Numida meleagris*)

Look for a round, slate-grey bird covered in neat white spots, with a bare, oddly shaped head topped by a hard-looking knob. Helmeted Guineafowl often travel in noisy groups across open country, including savanna and farmland. Their harsh, repeated alarm calls can draw attention before the flock comes into view. They are resident across much of sub-Saharan Africa.
