# How to identify the European Starling (*Sturnus vulgaris*)

Look for a compact, dark bird with a short tail, pointed wings and a fine, sharp bill. In fresh winter plumage, pale flecks cover much of its body; as the breeding season approaches, these wear away and the feathers shine green and purple, while the bill turns yellow. It is often seen in noisy groups in towns and on farmland, and may visit garden feeders.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
