# How to identify the Eurasian Goshawk (*Astur gentilis*)

A large forest hawk, the Eurasian Goshawk has broad wings, a long tail and a pale face stripe. Adults are grey above, finely barred underneath and usually show orange eyes. Young birds are browner and streaked. Look for it over or within extensive woodland across much of Europe and Asia; in some parts of central Europe it also occurs among small woods and wooded urban areas.
