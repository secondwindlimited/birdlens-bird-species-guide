# How to identify the Black-chinned Sparrow (*Spizella atrogularis*)

A slim, long-tailed sparrow with a gray head and body, a streaked brown back, and a small pink bill. In breeding plumage, the male’s chin and throat look black. Listen for a high, whistled song that speeds into a trill. It lives in brushy, often rocky country across the southwestern United States and much of Mexico.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Black-chinned Sparrow |
| Scientific name | *Spizella atrogularis* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Passerellidae |
| Genus | *Spizella* |
