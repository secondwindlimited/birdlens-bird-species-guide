# How to identify the Coscoroba Swan (*Coscoroba coscoroba*)

The Coscoroba Swan is a white, medium-large waterbird with a vivid red bill that looks more like a duck’s than a typical swan’s. Black wing ends are hard to see when the bird is resting on water, but show in flight. Look for it on open lakes, wetlands, rivers and coastal waters in southern South America, often in flocks or among Black-necked Swans.
