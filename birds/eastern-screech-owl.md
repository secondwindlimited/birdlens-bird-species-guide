# How to identify the Eastern Screech-Owl (*Megascops asio*)

A compact owl with a broad head, short tail and raised ear tufts. Its finely marked feathers may be gray or rusty red, and its eyes are yellow. Look for it after dark in wooded neighborhoods, parks and forests across eastern North America. It often stays hidden in a tree cavity, but its descending call and wavering trill may reveal its presence.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Eastern Screech-Owl |
| Scientific name | *Megascops asio* |
| Order | Strigiformes |
| Family | Strigidae |
| Genus | *Megascops* |
