# How to identify the Common Chiffchaff (*Phylloscopus collybita*)

A compact, rather plain leaf warbler, the Common Chiffchaff is usually easiest to identify by its repeated, slightly uneven “chiff-chaff” song. It has brownish-green upperparts, pale underparts, a faint eyebrow and dark legs. Watch for its regular tail dip and sideways flick. In the breeding season it uses open woodland and scrub, often near water.
