# How to identify the Fieldfare (*Turdus pilaris*)

The Fieldfare is a large thrush with a slate-colored head and rump, a deep brown back, and a pale breast marked with dark spots. Its sharp, dry “chakchak” call is often heard as flocks move between fields and trees. It breeds in northern and central Europe and across northern Asia, then moves south for winter, when it may visit farmland, orchards, and parks.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
