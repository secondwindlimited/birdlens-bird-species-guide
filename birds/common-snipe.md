# How to identify the Common Snipe (*Gallinago gallinago*)

A compact, streaked wader with a straight bill much longer than its head is often first noticed when it bursts from cover. Look for buff lines along its brown back and a pale edge to the trailing part of the wing. It feeds in soft ground at wetlands, often close to reeds or grass, across its breeding range and on wintering grounds farther south.

|---|---|
