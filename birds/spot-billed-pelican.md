# How to identify the Spot-billed Pelican (*Pelecanus philippensis*)

Look for a large, mostly pale pelican with a bulky pinkish bill marked by dark spots along the upper surface. Grey, untidy feathers crown the head and nape, and bare skin circles the eyes. It is often seen fishing with other pelicans on lakes or estuaries. Breeding birds develop pink tones on the body and a warm ochre breast; nesting groups gather in trees near wetlands.
