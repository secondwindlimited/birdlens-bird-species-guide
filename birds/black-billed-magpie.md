# How to identify the Black-billed Magpie (*Pica hudsonia*)

The Black-billed Magpie is a large, boldly patterned bird with a very long tail and flashes of blue-green on its wings. Its black bill helps separate it from the yellow-billed magpie. Look for it in open country, beside roads, or in towns across much of western and central North America. It often searches the ground in small groups.
