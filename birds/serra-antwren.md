# How to identify the Serra Antwren (*Formicivora serrana*)

The Serra Antwren is a small, long-tailed bird of Brazil’s southeastern forests and scrub. Males have dark underparts and show striking differences in colour across their range: some are rufous-backed, while coastal birds can be almost entirely black. Females are brown above and pale below, with a strong pale stripe over the eye. Look for it moving actively through low vegetation.
