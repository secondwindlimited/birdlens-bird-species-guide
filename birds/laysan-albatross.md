# How to identify the Laysan Albatross (*Phoebastria immutabilis*)

Look for a mostly white albatross with long, slim wings, charcoal-gray upperparts and dark edging beneath the wings. Its pink, dark-tipped bill and blackish patch beside each eye help confirm it. Laysan Albatrosses are most often encountered over the North Pacific, from tropical waters to the Bering Sea; they may also pass close to land, especially around breeding islands.
