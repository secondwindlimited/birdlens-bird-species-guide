# How to identify the Wrentit (*Chamaea fasciata*)

A small, rounded brownish bird with a pale eye, short bill and remarkably long tail, often carried at an angle. Its plain plumage can make it hard to spot in thick scrub, but a quick series of sharp, speeding notes often gives it away. Look for it in coastal chaparral and scrubby woodland from Oregon through California to northern Baja California.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|

| Genus | *Chamaea* |
