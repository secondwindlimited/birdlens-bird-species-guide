# How to identify the Eurasian Blackcap (*Sylvia atricapilla*)

The Eurasian Blackcap is a compact, grey warbler best recognised by the small cap on its head: black in males and warm brown in females and young birds. It occurs in wooded places, dense scrub and gardens, where it may visit feeding stations in winter. Most birds leave their breeding grounds for Africa or the Mediterranean, though some stay in western Europe.
