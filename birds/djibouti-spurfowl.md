# How to identify the Djibouti Spurfowl (*Pternistis ochropectus*)

The Djibouti Spurfowl is a bulky, short-tailed ground bird with a grey-brown back, a rusty crown and a dark mask across its face. Fine pale streaks cover its neck and breast. Listen for a rapid, lengthening rattle of “krek” notes. It occurs only in Djibouti, where it is most likely to be found in upland woodland in the Goda Mountains.
