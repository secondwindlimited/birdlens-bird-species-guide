# How to identify the Cedar Waxwing (*Bombycilla cedrorum*)

A plump, smooth-looking bird with a small crest, black face mask and bright yellow tail tip. Listen for its thin, high whistle, often given as it moves with a flock. Cedar Waxwings are widespread across North America and are especially likely around berry-bearing trees in woods, orchards, gardens and parks.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Cedar Waxwing |
| Scientific name | *Bombycilla cedrorum* |
