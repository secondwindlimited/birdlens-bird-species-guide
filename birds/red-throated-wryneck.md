# How to identify the Red-throated Wryneck (*Jynx ruficollis*)

A slim, brown-patterned woodpecker with a small head, fine bill and long tail, the Red-throated Wryneck is most readily recognised by its reddish throat. It occurs across parts of sub-Saharan Africa, often in open country where trees are scattered. Look for it in woodland edges, farmland and gardens, usually with another bird nearby; its clear, ringing calls can reveal it first.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Red-throated Wryneck |
