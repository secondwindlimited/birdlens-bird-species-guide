# How to identify the Wilson's Warbler (*Cardellina pusilla*)

A small, lively warbler with yellow underparts, an olive-green to brown back and a narrow tail that it often flicks while feeding. Most adults and young males show a small black crown patch; young females usually lack it. Look for Wilson’s Warbler in dense shrubs and wet thickets during the breeding season, or in brushy woodland on migration and in winter.
