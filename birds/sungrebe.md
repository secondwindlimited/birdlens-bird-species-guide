# How to identify the Sungrebe (*Heliornis fulica*)

Look for a small, long-tailed bird with a striped black-and-white head and reddish-brown body, moving quietly along shaded water. Its lobed toes and yellow-and-black-banded legs may show as it swims. Sungrebes live on tropical lowland waterways in Central and South America, where they can slip into waterside vegetation or fly close above the surface when alarmed.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Sungrebe |
| Scientific name | *Heliornis fulica* |
| Order | Gruiformes |
| Family | Heliornithidae |
| Genus | *Heliornis* |
