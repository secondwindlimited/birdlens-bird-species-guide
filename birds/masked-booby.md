# How to identify the Masked Booby (*Sula dactylatra*)

Look for a large, white seabird with black wing edges and tail, a yellow bill, and dark skin around its face. It patrols tropical seas far from land and is most often seen near remote islands or with mixed flocks feeding offshore. Young birds are browner above and have mostly pale underwings.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Masked Booby |
| Scientific name | *Sula dactylatra* |
| Order | Suliformes |
| Family | Sulidae |
| Genus | *Sula* |
