# How to identify the White-bellied Spinetail (*Mazaria propinqua*)

Look for a small, brown spinetail with rusty flight feathers and a long, tapered tail, usually in low, tangled growth on Amazonian river islands. The adult’s dark lower throat stands out above a pale belly and helps separate it from similar spinetails. It stays in its range year-round; its song is a drawn-out, nasal, mechanical-sounding note.
