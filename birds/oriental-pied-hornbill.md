# How to identify the Oriental Pied-Hornbill (*Anthracoceros albirostris*)

The Oriental Pied-Hornbill is a medium-sized, mostly black hornbill with a white belly, pale facial markings and white along the wings and tail. Its yellowish bill carries a casque, which is larger in males. Look for it in forest, woodland and sometimes large city parks, where its loud calls may reveal a social group before the birds are seen.
