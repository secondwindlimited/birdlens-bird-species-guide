# How to identify the Crab-Plover (*Dromas ardeola*)

The Crab-Plover stands tall on long grey legs, with a thick dark bill and a mostly white body set off by a black back and dark wing tips. This bold pattern makes it easy to pick out among shorebirds. Look for it in groups along warm Indian Ocean shores, especially on sandy beaches and coral islands; it also uses rocky coasts and mangroves.
