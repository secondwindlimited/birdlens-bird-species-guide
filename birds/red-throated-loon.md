# How to identify the Red-throated Loon (*Gavia stellata*)

The Red-throated Loon is the smallest member of the loon family, with a fine, straight bill that it often tips slightly upward. In summer, its red throat patch and dark back are distinctive; in winter, look for a pale face beneath a darker crown and rear neck. It breeds on northern tundra ponds and spends much of the colder season along marine coasts.
