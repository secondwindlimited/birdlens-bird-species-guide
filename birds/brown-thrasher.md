# How to identify the Brown Thrasher (*Toxostoma rufum*)

Look for a fairly large, long-tailed bird with rich reddish-brown back and wings, a pale chest marked with dark streaks, and bright yellow eyes. It often stays low and hidden in dense shrubs, woodland margins, and young woods in eastern North America. Its clear, ringing song can give it away: the bird strings together paired phrases and may imitate other species.
