# How to identify the Ringed Woodpecker (*Celeus torquatus*)

The Ringed Woodpecker is a large, mainly rufous woodpecker with a pale cinnamon head, a tousled crest and a solid black breast. Males have a red mark on the side of the face; females do not. Listen for repeated, sharp whistles in mature forest or along its edges in northern South America and a separate area of southeastern Brazil.
