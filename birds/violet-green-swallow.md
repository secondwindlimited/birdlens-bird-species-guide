# How to identify the Violet-green Swallow (*Tachycineta thalassina*)

The Violet-green Swallow is a small, compact bird of western North America. Look for a green sheen above, a white face and broad white patches on the sides of the rump, especially clear in flight. It often catches insects over meadows and ponds, alone or among other swallows. Its nesting range reaches Alaska, and it winters farther south.
