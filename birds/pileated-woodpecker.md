# How to identify the Pileated Woodpecker (*Dryocopus pileatus*)

Look for a large, mostly black woodpecker with a scarlet crest and white markings along its face and neck. In flight, pale patches show on the upper and lower wings, and its broad, uneven wingbeats make its path dip. It is most likely to be seen in wooded places with tall, mature trees, but may visit a suet feeder nearby.
