# How to identify the Eurasian Nightjar (*Caprimulgus europaeus*)

A mottled brown-and-grey nightbird, the Eurasian Nightjar is difficult to spot when resting among leaves or bare ground. At dusk and after dark, listen for the male’s long, vibrating churr. In flight, males show clear white marks on the wings and tail; females lack these. It breeds in open, lightly wooded country across Europe and Asia, then migrates to Africa for winter.
