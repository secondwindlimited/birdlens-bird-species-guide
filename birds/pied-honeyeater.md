# How to identify the Pied Honeyeater (*Certhionyx variegatus*)

A male Pied Honeyeater is strikingly black and white, with a small blue patch of bare skin below the eye and a long, downcurved bill. Females and young birds are brown and more subdued. Look for them in inland Australia, where they roam through dry shrublands and woodlands in search of flowering plants; their numbers can vary greatly from place to place and year to year.
