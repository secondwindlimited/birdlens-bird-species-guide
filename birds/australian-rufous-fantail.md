# How to identify the Australian Rufous Fantail (*Rhipidura rufifrons*)

Look for a small, restless fantail with rusty-orange colouring on its forehead, rump and tail base, set against a grey-brown back and pale, spotted underside. It often spreads and flicks its tail as it moves through low foliage. The species occurs in northern and eastern coastal Australia and across parts of Southeast Asia and the western Pacific, usually in damp woodland.
