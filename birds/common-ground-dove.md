# How to identify the Common Ground Dove (*Columbina passerina*)

A very small, compact dove, best recognised by its short, blunt-ended tail, fine scale-like marks on the throat and upper chest, and dark flecks across brown wings. Its bill has a dark tip. It often feeds on the ground in open, brushy places, from the southern United States through the Caribbean and into South America.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Common Ground Dove |
| Scientific name | *Columbina passerina* |
| Order | Columbiformes |
| Family | Columbidae |
| Genus | *Columbina* |
