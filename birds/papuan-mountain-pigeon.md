# How to identify the Papuan Mountain-Pigeon (*Gymnophaps albertisii*)

Look for a large, slim pigeon with a long tail, blue-grey upperparts, red skin around the eye, and a pale band at the tail’s end. Its light breast contrasts with darker lower plumage. It lives on New Guinea and nearby islands, usually in forested hills and mountains. Flocks may pass high over the tree canopy, though birds also visit lower ground.
