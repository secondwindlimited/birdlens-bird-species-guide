# How to identify the Common Loon (*Gavia immer*)

A large waterbird with a heavy, straight bill, the Common Loon is easiest to recognise in summer by its glossy dark head and boldly patterned back. In winter it is greyer above and white below. Listen for its far-carrying, wavering calls. It breeds on northern lakes and ponds, then spends much of the colder season on sheltered coastal waters.
