# How to identify the Australian Logrunner (*Orthonyx temminckii*)

A reddish-brown rainforest bird with a grey face, bold white bars on dark wings and a distinctive stiff-tipped tail. The Australian Logrunner feeds on the forest floor, often in pairs or small groups, but can be hard to spot among leaf litter and vegetation. Look for it in rainforests of southeastern Australia, from the Illawarra district of New South Wales north to Queensland’s Bunya Mountains.
