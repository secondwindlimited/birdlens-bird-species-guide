# How to identify the Red-browed Pardalote (*Pardalotus rubricatus*)

Look for a tiny, gray-brown bird with a pale underside, a black crown dotted with white, and a red mark just above its short bill. A yellow eyebrow and conspicuous yellow wing patch set it apart from other pardalotes. It occurs across inland and northern Australia, often in eucalypt woodland near watercourses.
