# How to identify the Eastern Meadowlark (*Sturnella magna*)

Look for a brown, heavily marked bird with a bright yellow underside and a bold dark breast mark shaped like a V. It often stands in open grass, including pastures and hayfields, and may sing from a fence post or low perch. In flight, pale outer tail feathers flash beneath its short, rounded wings. Its clear, high whistles help distinguish it from Western Meadowlark.
