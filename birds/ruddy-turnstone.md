# How to identify the Ruddy Turnstone (*Arenaria interpres*)

The Ruddy Turnstone is a compact shorebird with orange legs, a short, wedge-shaped bill and sharply contrasting black-and-white markings. In breeding plumage, rusty-red upperparts make it especially distinctive. Look for it turning stones, shells and other shore debris as it searches for food. It breeds on Arctic coasts and is most likely to be seen on shorelines outside the breeding season.
