# How to identify the Common Quail (*Coturnix coturnix*)

Look for a compact, brown bird with fine dark streaks and a pale stripe above the eye, usually hidden in grass or crops. A male may give a repeated, clear “wet-my-lips” whistle, sometimes after dark. Common Quails breed across Europe and central Asia, then move to wintering areas in Africa and South Asia; sightings often come when one is startled into flight.
