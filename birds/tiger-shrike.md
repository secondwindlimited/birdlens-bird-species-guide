# How to identify the Tiger Shrike (*Lanius tigrinus*)

The Tiger Shrike is a compact, short-tailed bird with a large-looking head and a stout bill. Adult males stand out for their grey crown, dark eye-mask and chestnut back marked with black bars. It breeds in eastern Asia, where it uses woodland margins and open country with scattered trees, then moves south for the winter into tropical Southeast Asia.
