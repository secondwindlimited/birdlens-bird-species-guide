# How to identify the Streaked Xenops (*Xenops rutilans*)

Look for a compact, rufous-brown bird with a pale eyebrow and a distinct white mark along the cheek. Dark-edged pale streaks cover its crown and breast, making it more patterned than the Plain Xenops. It ranges from Costa Rica to Argentina and is usually encountered alone or with a partner, often alongside a mixed-species flock in forest.
