# How to identify the Cave Swallow (*Petrochelidon fulva*)

The Cave Swallow is a small, compact swallow with rounded wings and a short, square-ended tail. Its pale buff-orange rump, orange forehead and light orange throat help distinguish it from other swallows in its range. Look for it around caves, sinkholes, bridges and culverts in parts of Mexico, the Caribbean and Texas. It often feeds on insects over fields or water.
