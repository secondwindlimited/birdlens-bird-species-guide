# How to identify the Yellow-headed Caracara (*Daptrius chimachima*)

Look for a pale, buff-headed raptor with dark brown wings and a light patch in the wing that shows in flight. A slim mark beside the eye, rather than a full dark face mask, helps separate it from a Laughing Falcon. Its smaller build and lack of a dark cap distinguish it from a Crested Caracara. It often perches in plain view over pasture or beside rivers.
