# How to identify the Short-toed Treecreeper (*Certhia brachydactyla*)

Look for a small brown bird climbing a trunk in a spiral, using its stiff tail for support as it probes the bark with a slender, curved bill. Its pale throat contrasts with its greyer, less clean-looking underside. In Europe, it is often found in mature broad-leaved woods, parks and tree-lined farmland, commonly at lower elevations than the Eurasian Treecreeper. A sharp, repeated “tut” can help confirm it.
