# How to identify the Atlantic Royal Flycatcher (*Onychorhynchus swainsoni*)

An uncommon flycatcher of Brazil’s Atlantic Forest, it is usually seen alone or in pairs among the middle levels of humid woodland. Its brown body, long bill and pale cinnamon rump and tail help identify it. The striking fan-shaped crest is usually held flat; when opened, it shows vivid red in males or yellow in females, with dark blue-black tips.
