# How to identify the Cape Grassbird (*Sphenoeacus afer*)

A fairly large, streak-backed bird with a rusty crown, dark stripes beside its white throat and a long, untidy tail. It keeps to thick grass, scrub and fynbos rather than woodland, often slipping through cover before making a low, hurried flight to another bush. Listen for its musical, rising song or its nasal, mewing call in southern Africa.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Cape Grassbird |
| Scientific name | *Sphenoeacus afer* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Macrosphenidae |
| Genus | *Sphenoeacus* |
