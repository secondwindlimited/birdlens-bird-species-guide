# How to identify the Loggerhead Shrike (*Lanius ludovicianus*)

Look for a gray-backed bird with a pale underside, a broad black band through the eyes, and white flashes on dark wings. Its hooked bill gives it a predatory profile. It often watches from a fence or wire in open country dotted with shrubs or trees, across much of southern and western North America.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Loggerhead Shrike |
| Scientific name | *Lanius ludovicianus* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Laniidae |
| Genus | *Lanius* |
