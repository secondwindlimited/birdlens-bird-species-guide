# How to identify the Abbott's Babbler (*Malacocincla abbotti*)

A small, understated babbler with olive-brown upperparts, rusty lower flanks, a pale grey-white throat and a heavy, hooked bill. Its short tail and unhurried movements are useful clues. It is usually encountered alone or in pairs in dense low growth, forest edges and scrub across parts of South and Southeast Asia.

## What kind of bird is it?

| Common name | Abbott's Babbler |
| Scientific name | *Malacocincla abbotti* |
