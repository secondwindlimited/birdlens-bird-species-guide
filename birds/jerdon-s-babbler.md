# How to identify the Jerdon's Babbler (*Chrysomma altirostre*)

Look for a compact, warm-brown bird with a rounded head, stubby bill and long balancing tail, usually moving low and quietly through dense riverbank reeds. Its pale grey face and throat, yellow-gold skin around the eye, and clear, lilting song are useful clues. Jerdon’s Babbler lives year-round in scattered tall-grass and reed patches in South Asia.
