# How to identify the Wattled Ploughbill (*Eulacestoma nigropectus*)

Look for a small, plump forest bird with a heavy wedge-shaped bill. Adult males are especially distinctive, with pink wattles beside the bill and black wings and chest; females are olive-green and lack wattles. The Wattled Ploughbill lives in montane forest in New Guinea’s central ranges, where it forages low and often climbs bamboo.
