# How to identify the Madagascar Hoopoe (*Upupa marginata*)

A fairly large bird with cinnamon-orange plumage, a slim curved bill and a tall crest, the Madagascar Hoopoe is distinctive at close range. Its rounded wings flash bold dark and pale bands during an uneven flap-and-glide flight. It occurs across Madagascar and is most likely to be seen in open woodland, scrub and other lightly wooded country.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Madagascar Hoopoe |
| Scientific name | *Upupa marginata* |
| Order | Bucerotiformes |
| Family | Upupidae |
| Genus | *Upupa* |
