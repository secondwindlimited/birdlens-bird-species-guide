# How to identify the Southern Cassowary (*Casuarius casuarius*)

The Southern Cassowary is a huge, flightless bird with coarse black feathers, a blue face, red neck wattles and a tall brown casque. It is distinctive among birds and is unlikely to be mistaken for another species. Look for it in tropical rainforest in northeastern Australia, New Guinea and Indonesia; it may also cross roads or appear beside them.
