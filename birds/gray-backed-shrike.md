# How to identify the Gray-backed Shrike (*Lanius tephronotus*)

A compact, long-tailed shrike with a slate-grey crown and back, black eye stripe and wings, pale throat, and warm orange flanks is usually distinctive. It is smaller than the Long-tailed Shrike and lacks that species’ orange back. Look for it sitting conspicuously on wires, posts, or dead branches in open country, woodland margins, and farmland. In winter it moves south into Southeast Asia.
