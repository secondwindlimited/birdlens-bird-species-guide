# How to identify the Cinnamon-breasted Bunting (*Emberiza tahapisi*)

The Cinnamon-breasted Bunting is a small bird of open, rocky country. Its warm cinnamon underside contrasts with mottled brown upperparts and a dark-and-orange bill. Males have bold black-and-white head stripes, while females look more subdued. Listen for its rough, rapid trills from a raised perch. It occurs across much of mainland sub-Saharan Africa, especially on sparsely vegetated slopes.
