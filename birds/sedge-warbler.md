# How to identify the Sedge Warbler (*Acrocephalus schoenobaenus*)

The Sedge Warbler is a small brown bird of wet, bushy places. Look for a broad, pale stripe above the eye, set against dark markings on the crown, and warm buff colouring below. It often stays hidden among reeds and scrub, but its long, hurried, chattering song can reveal it. It breeds across much of Europe and western and central Asia, then winters in Africa.
