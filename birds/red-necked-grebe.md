# How to identify the Red-necked Grebe (*Podiceps grisegena*)

The Red-necked Grebe is a large, long-necked waterbird, noticeably bigger than Horned or Eared grebes. In breeding plumage, its rusty-red neck, pale cheeks and black cap make it distinctive. In winter, look for a dusky head, pale throat and long bill with a yellow base. It breeds on northern inland waters and spends winter mainly at sea.
