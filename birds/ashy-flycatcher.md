# How to identify the Ashy Flycatcher (*Fraseria caerulescens*)

A slim, cool-grey flycatcher with an upright stance, a pale eye-ring crossed by a dark line and a short white eyebrow. It often waits on a perch in woodland, then darts out to catch prey before returning to the same spot. Look for it around forest margins, in dense woodland and plantations across much of sub-Saharan Africa.
