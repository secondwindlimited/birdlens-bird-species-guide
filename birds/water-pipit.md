# How to identify the Water Pipit (*Anthus spinoletta*)

A slim, grey-brown pipit with a pale eyebrow and white edges to the tail. In breeding plumage, its breast is often faintly pink and nearly plain; in winter, the underside is whiter and more streaked. Look for it in high mountain grassland in the breeding season, then around wetlands such as marshes, lakeshores and coastal flats after it moves to lower ground.
