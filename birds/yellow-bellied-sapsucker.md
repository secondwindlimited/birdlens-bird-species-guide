# How to identify the Yellow-bellied Sapsucker (*Sphyrapicus varius*)

A medium-sized woodpecker with a broad white panel on each dark wing, a red crown and, in males, a red throat. Its yellow-tinged underside may be hard to spot. It breeds in wooded parts of Canada and the northeastern United States, then moves south for winter. Look for it on tree trunks, where it drills rows of small holes to feed on sap.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
