# How to identify the Malagasy Paradise-Flycatcher (*Terpsiphone mutata*)

Look for a small, restless flycatcher with a bright blue eye ring and, in males, very long tail streamers. Males may be rufous, white, or black and white; females are rufous and have no streamers. It lives on Madagascar and nearby islands, where it can be seen in native forest, plantations, and scrub.

| Common name | Malagasy Paradise-Flycatcher |
