# How to identify the Snowy Owl (*Bubo scandiacus*)

The Snowy Owl is a large, mostly white owl with yellow eyes and dark markings that vary between individuals. Older males are usually the palest, while females and young birds tend to show more brown spots and bars. Look for it on Arctic tundra or, in winter, across open northern landscapes such as shorelines, marshes and fields in Eurasia and North America.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|

| Scientific name | *Bubo scandiacus* |
