# How to identify the Cloud-forest Pygmy-Owl (*Glaucidium nubicola*)

A tiny, round-headed owl with brown or rufous plumage, pale streaks below and small white crown marks. Look for it in wet mountain forests on the Pacific side of Colombia and Ecuador, where it keeps to the middle and upper forest. It may call by day or night, giving paired notes at a steady pitch. The species is rare and resident.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Cloud-forest Pygmy-Owl |
| Scientific name | *Glaucidium nubicola* |
| Order | Strigiformes |
| Family | Strigidae |
| Genus | *Glaucidium* |
