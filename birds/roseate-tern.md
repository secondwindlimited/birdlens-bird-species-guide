# How to identify the Roseate Tern (*Sterna dougallii*)

Look for a slim, very pale tern with long trailing tail streamers, a deeply forked tail and orange-red legs. It is whiter than a Common Tern, especially across the wings, and flies with quick, shallow wing strokes. Roseate Terns nest in scattered colonies, often on offshore islands. Away from nesting sites, watch for them among other terns on beaches, mudflats and estuaries.
