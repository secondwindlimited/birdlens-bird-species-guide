# How to identify the Spot-tailed Nightjar (*Antiurus maculicaudus*)

A small, brown nightjar with a broad buff eyebrow, a pale V-shaped marking on its back and conspicuous spots on its wings. Males have white tips on the outer tail feathers, though these can be hard to see in the field. Look for it in open savanna and grassland with scattered bushes or palmettos; after dark, it may rest beside quiet roads and tracks.
