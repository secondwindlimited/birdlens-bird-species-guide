# How to identify the Green-headed Hillstar (*Oreotrochilus stolzmanni*)

This small Andean hummingbird is easiest to pick out by the broad white areas in its tail. Males also show a bright green throat patch and a dark line down the white underside. Look for it in open, flower-rich highland country, where it may perch on rocks or spiky plants, or visit villages and other open places.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Green-headed Hillstar |
| Scientific name | *Oreotrochilus stolzmanni* |
| Order | Apodiformes |
