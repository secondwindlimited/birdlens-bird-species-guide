# How to identify the Yellow-faced Honeyeater (*Caligavis chrysops*)

Look for a medium-small, gray-brown honeyeater with a bright yellow line curving across each side of the face, framed by dark stripes. Fine dusky marks cross the pale breast. Its brisk, repeated “chip-up” notes are often heard before the bird is seen. It occurs in eastern Australian forests and woodlands, and many birds travel north in autumn in passing flocks.
