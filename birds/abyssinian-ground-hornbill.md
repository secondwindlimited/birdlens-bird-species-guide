# How to identify the Abyssinian Ground-Hornbill (*Bucorvus abyssinicus*)

One of Africa’s largest hornbills, this mostly black bird is often seen striding across open ground. Adults show vivid blue skin on the face; males also have red on the throat, while females are blue throughout. In flight, conspicuous white wing feathers stand out. It occurs across open country south of the Sahara, often in pairs or small parties.
