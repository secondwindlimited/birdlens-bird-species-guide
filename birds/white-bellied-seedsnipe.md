# How to identify the White-bellied Seedsnipe (*Attagis malouinus*)

A chunky, grouse-like bird with a short, stout bill, finely patterned upperparts and a bright white belly. White-bellied Seedsnipe usually occurs in pairs or small groups on exposed high-country moorland, bogs and rocky slopes above the tree line in southern Patagonia. In winter it may descend to the Patagonian steppe, where it can sometimes be spotted beside roads.
