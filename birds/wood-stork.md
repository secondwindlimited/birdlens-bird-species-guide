# How to identify the Wood Stork (*Mycteria americana*)

The Wood Stork is a very large wading bird with a white body, dark wings and long legs. Adults have a bare, dark-looking head and a heavy, down-curved bill. Look for it in shallow wetlands, where it sweeps its bill through the water to catch fish. In flight, it often circles high overhead, sometimes alongside vultures.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Wood Stork |
| Scientific name | *Mycteria americana* |
| Order | Ciconiiformes |
| Family | Ciconiidae |
| Genus | *Mycteria* |
