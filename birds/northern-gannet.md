# How to identify the Northern Gannet (*Morus bassanus*)

The Northern Gannet is a large white seabird with a pale buff head, long pointed bill and black wing ends. Young birds are brown and gain white feathers as they age. It plunges headfirst into the sea to catch fish. Look for it around North Atlantic coasts, especially near breeding cliffs, or offshore on a boat trip.
