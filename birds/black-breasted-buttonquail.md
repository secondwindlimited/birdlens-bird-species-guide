# How to identify the Black-breasted Buttonquail (*Turnix melanogaster*)

A plump, dark buttonquail with intricate pale spotting and bars, found on the forest floor in southeastern Queensland and nearby northeastern New South Wales. Females have a mostly black head and stronger markings than males. Rather than relying on a sighting, look for the deep, round scrapes it leaves while feeding in leaf litter, or listen for clucking calls.
