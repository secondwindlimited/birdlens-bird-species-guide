# How to identify the Bush Thick-knee (*Burhinus grallarius*)

A tall, slim ground bird with very long legs, a striking yellow eye and bold dark streaking across its pale body. It often rests beneath shrubs or small trees in daylight, then calls and feeds after dark. Look for it in open woodland and grassland across Australia, especially in the north; it also turns up in parks and camping areas in some northern cities.

## What kind of bird is it?
