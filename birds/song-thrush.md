# How to identify the Song Thrush (*Turdus philomelos*)

A compact thrush with a plain brown back and dark, rounded spots on its pale breast and belly. It is smaller and neater-looking than a Mistle Thrush. Listen for a clear, ringing song with musical phrases repeated several times. Song Thrushes often feed on grass near trees or shrubs, including in gardens, parks and farmland with hedges.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Song Thrush |
| Scientific name | *Turdus philomelos* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Turdidae |
| Genus | *Turdus* |
