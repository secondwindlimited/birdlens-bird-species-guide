# How to identify the Blue Jay (*Cyanocitta cristata*)

A large, loud songbird of eastern North America with a blue crest and back, a white face and underparts, and a black collar that runs around the neck. The wings and tail are bright blue barred with black and marked with white. Blue Jays are bold at feeders, fond of acorns, and often heard before they are seen.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Blue Jay |
| Scientific name | *Cyanocitta cristata* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Corvidae |
| Genus | *Cyanocitta* |
