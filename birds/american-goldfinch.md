# How to identify the American Goldfinch (*Spinus tristis*)

A small, neatly built finch with a pointed bill and short, notched tail, the American Goldfinch is easiest to recognise in spring and summer. Adult males are bright yellow, with a black forehead and wings. Females are less vivid, and both sexes become brownish and olive in winter. Look for them in weedy fields, gardens, orchards, or at feeders.
