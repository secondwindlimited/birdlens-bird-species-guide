# How to identify the Greater White-fronted Goose (*Anser albifrons*)

Look for a medium-sized, grey-brown goose with orange legs, a white patch around the base of the bill, and dark marks across the belly. Young birds lack the white face and belly markings. It is a bird of northern regions that travels south for winter, when flocks may be seen on farmland, wet ground, and other open feeding areas.
