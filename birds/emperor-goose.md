# How to identify the Emperor Goose (*Anser canagicus*)

Look for a compact, blue-gray goose with a white head, dark chin and throat, and fine black-and-white markings that give its body a patterned look. Its bill is small and pink, and its legs are orange. It is most likely to be seen along Alaska’s coast, especially on tidal flats and rocky shores; in winter, it gathers in small flocks.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Emperor Goose |
| Scientific name | *Anser canagicus* |
| Order | Anseriformes |
| Family | Anatidae |
| Genus | *Anser* |
