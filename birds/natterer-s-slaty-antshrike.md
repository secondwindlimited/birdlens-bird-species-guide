# How to identify the Natterer's Slaty-Antshrike (*Thamnophilus stictocephalus*)

A compact antshrike with a graduated tail ending in white tips is a useful first clue. Males are mostly gray, with a black crown and a pale belly centre; females show warm cinnamon-brown upperparts. It lives low in short, partly deciduous woodland and white-sand forest in Brazil and Bolivia. Its clear, accelerating song ends in a rattle.
