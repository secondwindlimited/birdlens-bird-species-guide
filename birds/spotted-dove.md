# How to identify the Spotted Dove (*Spilopelia chinensis*)

A slim, brown dove with a rosy wash below and a distinctive black-and-white spotted patch on the back of the neck. Its repeated, loud coo is often heard before the bird is seen. It is common in gardens and parks across much of Asia, and also occurs in several introduced populations around the world.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Spotted Dove |
| Scientific name | *Spilopelia chinensis* |
| Order | Columbiformes |
| Family | Columbidae |
| Genus | *Spilopelia* |
