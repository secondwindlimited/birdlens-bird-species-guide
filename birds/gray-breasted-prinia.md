# How to identify the Gray-breasted Prinia (*Prinia hodgsonii*)

Look for a small, compact prinia with a fine black bill, pinkish legs and a graduated gray tail. In breeding plumage, the gray breast band and smoky upperparts stand out against the pale throat; outside that season, the band may be incomplete or absent. It is often seen moving actively through scrub, forest-edge growth and clearings, sometimes in flocks.
