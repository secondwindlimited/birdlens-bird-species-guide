# How to identify the Military Macaw (*Ara militaris*)

A large, long-tailed parrot, mostly vivid green, with a scarlet mark on the forehead and blue and yellow in the wings and tail. Its loud, low calls can carry across a wide area. Look for it in pairs or small groups in wooded foothills and canyons, especially where tall trees remain.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Military Macaw |
| Scientific name | *Ara militaris* |
| Order | Psittaciformes |
| Family | Psittacidae |
| Genus | *Ara* |
