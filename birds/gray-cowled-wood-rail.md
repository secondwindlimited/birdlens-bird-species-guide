# How to identify the Gray-cowled Wood-Rail (*Aramides cajaneus*)

A large, chicken-shaped rail with a gray head and neck, rufous chest, black tail, bright yellow-green bill, and coral-red legs. It lives in wet forests and marshes, and is often seen walking in the open beside ponds or rivers. Unlike many rails, it can be conspicuous, usually alone or in pairs, with its short tail twitching as it moves.
