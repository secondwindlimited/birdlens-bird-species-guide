# How to identify the Galapagos Petrel (*Pterodroma phaeopygia*)

The Galápagos Petrel is a large, long-winged seabird with dark upperparts and a pale underside crossed by bold dark markings on the wings. A dark hood continuing along the neck helps identify it. It is usually seen over open water, often well away from shore; it visits its highland nesting grounds after dark and breeds only on the Galápagos Islands.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Galapagos Petrel |
| Scientific name | *Pterodroma phaeopygia* |

| Genus | *Pterodroma* |
