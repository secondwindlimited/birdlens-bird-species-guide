# How to identify the Invisible Rail (*Gallirallus wallacii*)

The Invisible Rail is a large, flightless bird with dark plumage, a bright red bill and red legs. It keeps to the forest floor and is difficult to see, though its low, rhythmic calls may reveal it. Look for it in swampy forest on Halmahera, especially around sago palms, where it lives alone or in pairs.

| Common name | Invisible Rail |
| Scientific name | *Gallirallus wallacii* |
