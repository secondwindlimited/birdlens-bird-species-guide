# How to identify the Indigo Bunting (*Passerina cyanea*)

The Indigo Bunting is a small, sparrow-sized songbird. In the breeding season, the male is strikingly blue, with a darker head; the female is brown, with a pale throat and subtle streaking. Look for it along brushy woodland edges and field margins across eastern and central North America. In winter, it travels south and may gather with others in weedy fields.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Indigo Bunting |
| Scientific name | *Passerina cyanea* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
