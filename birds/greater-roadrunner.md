# How to identify the Greater Roadrunner (*Geococcyx californianus*)

Look for a large, streaky brown cuckoo with a raised crest, long legs and an unusually long tail. It usually runs across the ground rather than flying, often in dry scrub where bushes provide cover. Its slow, low-pitched cooing carries from a fence, rock or other perch. The Greater Roadrunner lives year-round across the southwestern United States and northern Mexico.
