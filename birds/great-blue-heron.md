# How to identify the Great Blue Heron (*Ardea herodias*)

The Great Blue Heron is a very large, long-legged gray wader, recognized by its sturdy yellow-orange bill, dark head plumes and contrasting shoulder markings. It is often seen alone beside water, though several may gather where food is plentiful. Across much of North America, it uses habitats ranging from small ponds and marshes to coastal bays.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Great Blue Heron |
| Scientific name | *Ardea herodias* |
| Order | Pelecaniformes |
| Family | Ardeidae |
| Genus | *Ardea* |
