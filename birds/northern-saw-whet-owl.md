# How to identify the Northern Saw-whet Owl (*Aegolius acadicus*)

A very small, round-bodied owl with a pale face, yellow eyes and brown-and-white plumage. Look for it in wooded parts of Canada, the United States and Mexico, especially where conifers and dense low growth provide cover. It is often heard before it is seen: its repeated, clear whistles carry through the trees, while its shy habits make a perched bird easy to overlook.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Northern Saw-whet Owl |
| Scientific name | *Aegolius acadicus* |
| Order | Strigiformes |
| Family | Strigidae |
| Genus | *Aegolius* |
