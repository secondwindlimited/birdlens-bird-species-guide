# How to identify the Eurasian Nuthatch (*Sitta europaea*)

The Eurasian Nuthatch is a small, sturdy bird with blue-grey upperparts, a dark stripe through the eye and a pointed bill. It creeps along trunks and thick limbs, often head-down, without bracing with its tail. Look for it in mature deciduous or mixed woodland, parks and gardens across much of Europe and Asia. Its clear, ringing notes may give away its presence before it is seen.
