# How to identify the Lesser Adjutant (*Leptoptilos javanicus*)

Look for a tall, dark-backed stork with a bare head and throat, a pale belly and broad wings with white patches near the body. Adults have reddish facial skin and a yellow-orange neck; young birds show browner heads and more feathering. It stands less hunched than Greater Adjutant and is seen around wetlands, fields and wooded waterways across South and Southeast Asia.
