# How to identify the Shoebill (*Balaeniceps rex*)

The Shoebill is a tall, grey marsh bird with a thick neck and an enormous, hooked bill that looks like a wooden clog. Its broad wings and long trailing legs are clear in flight, while its still, stork-like outline is typical in wetlands. Look for it in large freshwater swamps of central tropical Africa, often standing alone or with one other bird.

## What kind of bird is it?

| Scientific name | *Balaeniceps rex* |

| Genus | *Balaeniceps* |
