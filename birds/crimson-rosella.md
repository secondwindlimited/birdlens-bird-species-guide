# How to identify the Crimson Rosella (*Platycercus elegans*)

Adults are easy to pick out by their vivid red, yellow or orange plumage and a blue patch on each cheek. Young birds look much greener, often with mixed colors as they mature. Crimson Rosellas occur across southeastern Australia, including Tasmania, in forests and woodlands, and may also be seen in parks and gardens. Their clear, two-part whistle is a useful clue.
