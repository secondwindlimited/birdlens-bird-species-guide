# How to identify the Immaculate Cupwing (*Pnoepyga immaculata*)

A very small, round-bodied cupwing with long legs and almost no visible tail, the Immaculate Cupwing stays close to the ground. Its brown plumage may show either dark breast spots or pale-and-black scaling. It lives in dense, shaded forest undergrowth in the Himalayan foothills, where its thin, descending song is often easier to detect than the bird itself.
