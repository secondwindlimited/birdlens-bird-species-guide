# How to identify the Egyptian Plover (*Pluvianus aegyptius*)

Look for a compact river bird with a blue-grey back, warm orange belly and sharply divided black-and-white head and chest. Pale and dark markings flash across its wings in flight, often accompanied by a repeated, sharp “tchep” call. It is most likely to be seen on exposed sandbanks beside large rivers in tropical sub-Saharan Africa.

## What kind of bird is it?

| Scientific name | *Pluvianus aegyptius* |
