# How to identify the Black-capped Chickadee (*Poecile atricapillus*)

A small, rounded chickadee with a large-looking head, it has a dark crown and throat, bright white cheek patches, and warm brownish sides. Black-capped Chickadees are familiar in wooded areas across Canada and the northern United States, and they often visit backyard feeders. Look for them moving among branches in busy flocks, or listen for a clear, two-note whistle.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
