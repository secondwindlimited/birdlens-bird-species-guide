# How to identify the Pied Currawong (*Strepera graculina*)

The Pied Currawong is a large, mostly black bird with a heavy hooked bill and bright yellow eyes. White marks on the tail and wings are especially clear in flight. Listen for its ringing, sometimes flute-like calls, which may resemble “curra-wong.” It is often seen in eastern Australian woodland, gardens and parks, searching for food on the ground or in trees.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Pied Currawong |
| Scientific name | *Strepera graculina* |

| Genus | *Strepera* |
