# How to identify the Dark-eyed Junco (*Junco hyemalis*)

Look for a small, sparrow-shaped bird with a pinkish bill and pale edges to its tail, which show as it moves. Its coloring varies widely: many are gray above and across the chest, with a light belly, while some have warmer brown tones. In North America, juncos are often found in wooded places. In winter they also gather at feeders and forage in yards.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Dark-eyed Junco |
| Scientific name | *Junco hyemalis* |

| Genus | *Junco* |
