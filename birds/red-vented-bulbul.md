# How to identify the Red-vented Bulbul (*Pycnonotus cafer*)

Look for a dark, compact bird with a squared-off head, a small black crest and a white patch at the base of its tail. The red beneath the tail may be hidden. Red-vented Bulbuls are active in both towns and countryside, often feeding or calling in trees and sometimes sitting on overhead wires.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Red-vented Bulbul |
| Scientific name | *Pycnonotus cafer* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Pycnonotidae |
| Genus | *Pycnonotus* |
