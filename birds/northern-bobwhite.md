# How to identify the Northern Bobwhite (*Colinus virginianus*)

Look for a compact, rounded quail with finely marked brown, black and pale plumage, usually walking through fields or low, tangled cover. Males often show a strong black-and-white pattern on the head; females have softer tan markings. Its clear, two-part whistle, often rendered as bob-white, can reveal a bird hidden in vegetation. It lives year-round across much of the eastern and central United States.
