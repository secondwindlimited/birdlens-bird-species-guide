# How to identify the Southern Screamer (*Chauna torquata*)

Look for a very large, gray, goose-like bird with long rosy legs, a bare red face and a loose tuft at the rear of the head. A dark band circles the lower neck, edged above by a narrow pale line. It is most often encountered on lakes and marshes in southern South America, sometimes in sizeable groups; its far-reaching paired calls can reveal it before it is seen.
