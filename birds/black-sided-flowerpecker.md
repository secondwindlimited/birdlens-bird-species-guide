# How to identify the Black-sided Flowerpecker (*Dicaeum monticolum*)

At roughly 8 cm long, the Black-sided Flowerpecker is a tiny bird of Borneo’s uplands. Males stand out by their dark, blue-sheened backs and vivid red breast patch; females are olive and gray, with a pale throat and yellowish rump. Listen for persistent dry clicking and thin, high notes. It is most likely to be found in mountain forest and nearby scrub or gardens.
