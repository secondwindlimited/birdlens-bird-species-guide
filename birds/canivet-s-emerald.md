# How to identify the Canivet's Emerald (*Cynanthus canivetii*)

Canivet's Emerald is a tiny hummingbird of lowland Central America and eastern Mexico. Males are almost entirely shining green, with a long, deeply split dark tail; females are grayer below and show a pale line behind the eye. Both sexes repeatedly swing the tail as they hover, a useful clue when the bird is hard to see. It is often found around woodland margins, scrub and gardens, including fairly dry country.
