# How to identify the Andean Condor (*Vultur gryphus*)

The Andean Condor is unmistakable for its enormous wings, long, separated flight feathers and, in adults, a white neck ruff and bold wing patches. It glides over the Andes, usually alone or with one companion. Look for it above mountain slopes and ridgelines from the northern Andes to Patagonia; in the south it also descends into lower country.
