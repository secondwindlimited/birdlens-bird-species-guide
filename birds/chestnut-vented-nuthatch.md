# How to identify the Chestnut-vented Nuthatch (*Sitta nagaensis*)

A small, blue-gray nuthatch with pale underparts and conspicuous rusty-red patches on its flanks. It moves head-first and sideways over trunks and branches, often in the company of mixed-species flocks. Listen for a clear, ringing series of trills or its nasal, chattering calls. It is found in upland forests from the eastern Himalaya and southern China to parts of mainland Southeast Asia.
