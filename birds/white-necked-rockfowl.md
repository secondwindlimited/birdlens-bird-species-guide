# How to identify the White-necked Rockfowl (*Picathartes gymnocephalus*)

Look for a tall, slim forest bird with a long dark tail and a bare yellow head marked by bold black patches behind the eyes. Its white throat and underside contrast with dark upperparts. White-necked Rockfowls are most likely to be seen on the ground in West African forests, often on rocky slopes. They may feed in pairs or groups and are usually quiet.
