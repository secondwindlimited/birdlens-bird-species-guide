# How to identify the Philippine Scops-Owl (*Otus megalotis*)

The Philippine Scops-Owl is a relatively large scops-owl with upright ear tufts and a dark outline around its pale facial disk. It occurs on islands across the Philippines, usually in lowland or foothill forest and along wooded edges. Gray-brown and reddish birds both occur; listen for a falling whistle, repeated descending notes or a thin hiss.
