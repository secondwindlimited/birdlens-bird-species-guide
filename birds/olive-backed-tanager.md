# How to identify the Olive-backed Tanager (*Mitrospingus oleagineus*)

Look for a fairly large, solidly built tanager with olive to coppery-green upperparts, a gray face and paler neck, and a heavy silvery bill. It moves in noisy parties through the treetops of humid forest on tepuis, searching leafy growth and epiphytes. Its sharp, strained squeal is distinctive. The species lives only around the Venezuela–Guyana–Brazil borderlands and stays there year-round.
