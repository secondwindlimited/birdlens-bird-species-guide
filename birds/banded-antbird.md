# How to identify the Banded Antbird (*Dichrozona cincta*)

A small, compact antbird with a very short tail and two pale bars across its dark wings. It has brown upperparts, a gray face and pale underparts marked with black across the breast. Listen for a measured series of rising whistles. It lives close to the ground in evergreen forest across parts of the western and central Amazon Basin.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Banded Antbird |
| Scientific name | *Dichrozona cincta* |

| Family | Thamnophilidae |
| Genus | *Dichrozona* |
