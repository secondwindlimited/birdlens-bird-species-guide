# How to identify the Red-capped Robin (*Petroica goodenovii*)

Look for a tiny robin in dry scrub or woodland across much of inland Australia. The male is unmistakable at close range, with a bright red forehead and breast set against black upperparts and bold white wing markings. Females are subdued brown and buff, with a faint reddish forehead. Birds are often seen alone or in pairs, taking prey from the ground.
