# How to identify the Tawny Owl (*Strix aluco*)

Look for a stocky, round-headed owl with dark eyes and brown or grey-brown plumage, often sitting motionless among leaves by day. It is usually heard rather than seen: its wavering hoot and sharp “kee-wik” call carry through the night. Tawny Owls live in wooded countryside, parks and gardens across much of Europe, including towns with mature trees.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Tawny Owl |
| Scientific name | *Strix aluco* |
| Order | Strigiformes |
| Family | Strigidae |
| Genus | *Strix* |
