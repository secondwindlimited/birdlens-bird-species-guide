# How to identify the African River Martin (*Pseudochelidon eurystomina*)

The African River Martin is a dark, compact swallow with a vivid red eye and a broad orange-red bill. Adults show a blue-green sheen on the head and greener tones across the back. Its short, square-ended tail and bulky-headed shape are useful clues in flight. Look for it in groups over wooded rivers, wetlands and coastal scrub in central Africa.
