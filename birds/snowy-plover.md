# How to identify the Snowy Plover (*Anarhynchus nivosus*)

Snowy plovers are small, pale shorebirds with a fine black bill, gray-to-black legs and a breast band that is broken at the front. In breeding plumage, males show black marks on the forehead, beside the neck and behind the eye. Look for them on open sandy beaches or bare shores of inland lakes, often alone or in loose groups.
