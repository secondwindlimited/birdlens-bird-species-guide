# How to identify the Broad-billed Hummingbird (*Cynanthus latirostris*)

A small hummingbird with a red bill, dark tail and, in good light, a male’s shining blue-green body. Females are plainer, with grayish underparts and a pale line extending back from the eye. The species is mainly found in Mexico, with local breeding in the southwestern United States, especially around wooded streams and canyon slopes.
