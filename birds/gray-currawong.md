# How to identify the Gray Currawong (*Strepera versicolor*)

The Gray Currawong is a large, crow-like bird with a heavy dark bill and bright yellow eyes. Look for white at the tail tip and beneath the tail, plus a fine pale edge along the back of the wing in flight. It occurs in southern Australia, where it uses forests, scrub and open ground near trees.
