# How to identify the White Stork (*Ciconia ciconia*)

A very large white bird with black wing feathers, long red legs and a straight red bill. It is easiest to spot in open country, where it walks across fields and wet grassland in search of food. Look for its bulky stick nest on a tree or building, or watch for flocks passing high overhead on migration.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | White Stork |
| Scientific name | *Ciconia ciconia* |
| Order | Ciconiiformes |
| Family | Ciconiidae |
| Genus | *Ciconia* |
