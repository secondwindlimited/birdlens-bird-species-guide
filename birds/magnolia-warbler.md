# How to identify the Magnolia Warbler (*Setophaga magnolia*)

Look for a small warbler with yellow underparts, a gray head, dark streaks and pale bars on the wings. Breeding males are especially vivid. The underside of the tail is a useful clue: much of it is white, with a black tip. The bird breeds in northern forests and may be seen in wooded places during migration.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
