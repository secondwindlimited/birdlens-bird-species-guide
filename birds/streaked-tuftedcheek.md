# How to identify the Streaked Tuftedcheek (*Pseudocolaptes boissonneautii*)

A chunky brown bird of Andean cloud forests, the Streaked Tuftedcheek is easiest to spot by the bright white feather tufts projecting from its cheeks. Look also for a streaked back, a scaly-looking breast and a rich rufous tail. It is usually alone or in pairs, often moving along broad branches in the middle and upper forest from Venezuela south to Bolivia.
