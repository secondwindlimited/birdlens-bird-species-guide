# How to identify the Falkland Steamer-Duck (*Tachyeres brachypterus*)

Look for a bulky, grey, flightless duck along the shores of the Falkland Islands. Adult males have a vivid orange bill and pale head; females and young birds are darker, with greener bills. Its very short wings and tail, thick neck and heavy bill help distinguish it from the similar Flying Steamer-Duck, although the two can be difficult to tell apart.
