# How to identify the Chattering Gnatwren (*Ramphocaenus sticturus*)

Look for a tiny, active forest bird with a buff face, an unusually slender, elongated bill and a dark tail marked with white. It moves through vine tangles around the middle and upper foliage, often flicking its raised tail from side to side. Its chattering song can give it away before it is seen in humid tropical woodland or at forest edges.
