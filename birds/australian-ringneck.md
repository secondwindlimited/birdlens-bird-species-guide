# How to identify the Australian Ringneck (*Barnardius zonarius*)

The Australian Ringneck is a medium-sized green parrot, about 33 cm long, with a yellow band around the back of its neck and blue-green in its wings and tail. Look for it in Australia in settings from dry mulga woodland to suburban parks. Its head and belly colours vary between regional forms. Its loud, ringing chatter can reveal its presence before it is seen.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Australian Ringneck |
| Scientific name | *Barnardius zonarius* |
| Order | Psittaciformes |
| Family | Psittaculidae |
| Genus | *Barnardius* |
