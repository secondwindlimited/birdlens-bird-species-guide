# How to identify the White-tailed Jay (*Cyanocorax mystacalis*)

Look for a fairly large jay with a black head, white nape and underside, and blue-grey wings and back. Its white tail is especially clear in flight, while yellow eyes mark adults. It has no similar-looking bird in its range. White-tailed Jays are often seen in noisy groups moving through dry woodland in western Ecuador and north-western Peru.

| Common name | White-tailed Jay |
| Scientific name | *Cyanocorax mystacalis* |

| Genus | *Cyanocorax* |
