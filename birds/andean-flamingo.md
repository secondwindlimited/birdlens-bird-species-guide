# How to identify the Andean Flamingo (*Phoenicoparrus andinus*)

A large, pale-pink flamingo of the high Andes, the Andean Flamingo is easiest to pick out by its yellow legs and the sharp black patch on its folded wings. Its long bill bends down at the tip and is mostly black. Look for it at high-altitude salt lakes and bogs, often among Chilean and James’s Flamingos.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Andean Flamingo |
| Scientific name | *Phoenicoparrus andinus* |
