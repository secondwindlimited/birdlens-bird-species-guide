# How to identify the Long-tailed Widowbird (*Euplectes progne*)

A breeding male Long-tailed Widowbird is hard to mistake: he is black, with red and white patches on his wings and an exceptionally long tail. Females and non-breeding males are brown and streaked, but retain a distinctive wing pattern. Look for this resident species in grassy, often wet open country, especially in southern Africa.
