# How to identify the Collared Forest-Falcon (*Micrastur semitorquatus*)

A large, slim forest raptor, the Collared Forest-Falcon is more often detected by its call than by sight. Look for long yellow legs and an extended, rounded tail marked with fine white bands. Adults are usually pale beneath, with dark patches beside the face. It lives year-round in forests and wooded, semi-open country across Central and South America.
