# How to identify the Ihering's Antwren (*Myrmotherula iheringi*)

A tiny, short-tailed antwren of the western Amazon. Males are dark grey with a broad black throat, while females are grey above and buff below; both show white marks on the wings. Look for it in dense, tangled growth beneath mature rainforest, often in bamboo stands. Its slow, spaced, falling whistles help separate it from the similar Long-winged Antwren.
