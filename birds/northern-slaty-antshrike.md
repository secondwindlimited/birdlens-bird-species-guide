# How to identify the Northern Slaty-Antshrike (*Thamnophilus punctatus*)

The Northern Slaty-Antshrike is a small, stout-billed bird of dry woodland and forest, usually seen below the canopy. Males are slate-gray and black, while females have a warm brown crown and clear pale markings on the wings. It is often found in pairs. Its paired songs and preference for drier sites can help distinguish it from similar antshrikes.
