# How to identify the Hadada Ibis (*Bostrychia hagedash*)

Look for a sizeable, grey-brown ibis with a slim pale line beneath each eye and a green-purple sheen on its folded wings. Its long bill is dark, with red near the base of the upper part in breeding condition. Hadadas are often noticed first by their harsh, repeated “ha-da-da” cries. They forage in pairs or small groups in grass, wet places and tree-lined settlements.
