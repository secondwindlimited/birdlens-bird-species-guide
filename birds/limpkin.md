# How to identify the Limpkin (*Aramus guarauna*)

The Limpkin is a tall, long-necked wader with dark brown plumage patterned in white, especially across the head and neck. Its heavy, yellowish bill bends down at the tip. It is usually found alone or in pairs around freshwater marshes and ponds, where its loud, rough wailing call may be heard before the bird is seen.

| Common name | Limpkin |
| Scientific name | *Aramus guarauna* |
