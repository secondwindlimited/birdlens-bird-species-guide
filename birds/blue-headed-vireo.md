# How to identify the Blue-headed Vireo (*Vireo solitarius*)

The Blue-headed Vireo is a small, green-backed songbird with a slate-blue head, bright pale eye-rings and two light bars on each wing. Its white throat stands out against its darker face. It breeds in northern forests and the Appalachians; during migration, look for it in wooded areas, sometimes among mixed groups of small songbirds.
