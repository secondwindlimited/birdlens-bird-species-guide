# How to identify the Masked Shrike (*Lanius nubicus*)

A small, fine-built shrike with a long tail, the Masked Shrike is easy to pick out by its bold black-and-white pattern. Adult males also show rusty orange across the breast and flanks. Look for it in wooded scrub, orchards and leafy gardens, often perched where it can watch for prey. It breeds from southeastern Europe into western Asia and winters in Africa.
