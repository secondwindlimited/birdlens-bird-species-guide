# How to identify the American Herring Gull (*Larus smithsonianus*)

The American Herring Gull is a hefty, long-billed gull. Adults have a light gray back, white body, yellow bill and usually pink legs. Look for it along coasts and lakes, around fishing boats and at refuse sites across North America. It often gathers with other gulls. Young birds are brown and mottled, gradually taking on adult plumage over about four years.
