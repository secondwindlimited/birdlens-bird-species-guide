# How to identify the Spotted Shag (*Phalacrocorax punctatus*)

Look for a slender, grey-blue shag with yellow-orange feet, a narrow hooked bill and small dark spots across its back and wings. In breeding plumage, white bands curve down each side of the neck, and the face shows bright colour around the eyes. It lives around New Zealand’s coasts, especially those of the South Island.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Spotted Shag |
| Scientific name | *Phalacrocorax punctatus* |
