# How to identify the Indian Roller (*Coracias benghalensis*)

The Indian Roller is a stocky bird with a large head and vivid blue on its wings, tail and underside. The blue is especially striking when it flies; pale streaks mark its throat and breast. It is usually seen in open country, including farmland, often perched in plain view on a wire or tree. Across much of its range it stays year-round.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Indian Roller |
| Scientific name | *Coracias benghalensis* |
| Order | Coraciiformes |
| Family | Coraciidae |
| Genus | *Coracias* |
