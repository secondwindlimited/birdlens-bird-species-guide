# How to identify the Barrow's Goldeneye (*Bucephala islandica*)

Look for a compact diving duck with a steep forehead and a rounded, raised nape. Adult males have a curved white mark beside the bill, set against a dark, violet-sheened head; females often show a yellow bill. The species breeds on forested northern waters and winters on coasts, rivers and lakes, often in flocks with Common Goldeneyes.
