# How to identify the Chestnut Quail-thrush (*Cinclosoma castanotum*)

Look for a patterned, ground-dwelling bird with a rich chestnut back, pale brow line and sharply marked wings. Males show a dark breast; females are greyer and less strongly marked. Chestnut Quail-thrushes occur in inland scrub and open mallee across parts of southeastern Australia, where they are more often noticed walking through cover than perched in view.
