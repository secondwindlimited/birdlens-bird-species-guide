# How to identify the Dartford Warbler (*Curruca undata*)

Look for a small, dark warbler with a noticeably lengthy tail, grey upperparts and muted wine-red underparts. A thin red ring around the eye and yellowish legs are useful details. It usually keeps low and slips through dense scrub, especially gorse-covered heath, in parts of Europe and the UK's southern districts. Singing birds may appear briefly on a bush top.
