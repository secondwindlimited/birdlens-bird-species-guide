# How to identify the Eastern Wood-Pewee (*Contopus virens*)

A small, subdued flycatcher with gray-olive upperparts, pale underparts, and two light bars on each wing. Its long, narrow, pointed wings look different from those of smaller Empidonax flycatchers. Look for it perched among trees or along a woodland edge before it darts after insects. Its clear, distinctive whistle, often heard as “pee-a-wee,” is the surest identification clue.
