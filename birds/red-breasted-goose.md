# How to identify the Red-breasted Goose (*Branta ruficollis*)

A compact goose with a small bill and dark plumage can disappear among larger geese at a distance. Close views reveal a vivid russet breast and a sharply patterned head in rust, black and white. Look for a clear white line along the flanks and uniformly dark underwings in flight. It breeds on Arctic Siberian tundra and is most likely to be seen in winter around the Black Sea.
