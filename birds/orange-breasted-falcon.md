# How to identify the Orange-breasted Falcon (*Falco deiroleucus*)

A bulky, dark falcon with a black head, pale throat and vivid rusty-orange chest is the key combination. It is easily mistaken for the smaller, more widespread Bat Falcon, especially where the two overlap. Look for it in or above extensive tropical forest, often resting alone or with a mate on a high branch or dead tree. It is scarce and remains in its range year-round.
