# How to identify the Common Waxbill (*Estrilda astrild*)

Look for a very small, slim finch with a scarlet bill, a red line through the eye, finely barred gray-brown feathers, and a dark area beneath the tail. It usually travels in chattering groups through tall grass or reeds, often close to water. Outside Africa, introduced birds may also be seen in parks, gardens, and other urban green spaces.
