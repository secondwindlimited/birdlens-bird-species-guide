# How to identify the Common Chaffinch (*Fringilla coelebs*)

The Common Chaffinch is a compact finch of woods, parks and gardens. Males show a blue-grey head and warm pinkish face and chest, set off by bold pale wing markings. Females are browner but retain the same patterned wings. The white edges of the tail flash in flight. Outside the breeding season, chaffinches often gather in flocks and visit garden feeders.
