# How to identify the Purple Martin (*Progne subis*)

The Purple Martin is North America’s largest swallow, a dark bird with long wings and a shallowly notched tail. Adult males show a blue-purple shine in close light; females are grayer and mottled below. Look for it in open parts of temperate North America, especially near nest boxes or martin houses in the East. It leaves for South America after breeding.
