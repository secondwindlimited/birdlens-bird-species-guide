# How to identify the American Redstart (*Setophaga ruticilla*)

The American redstart is a small, long-tailed warbler. Breeding males are black with vivid orange marks on the wings, flanks and tail; females and immature birds are grayer or olive, with yellow in those places. It often catches insects among leaves while repeatedly spreading and swinging its tail. It breeds across eastern North America and spends the colder months farther south in wooded places.
