# How to identify the Sao Tome Pigeon (*Columba malherbii*)

A dark pigeon about 28 cm long, with a grey head and breast, red feet and a greenish or pink sheen across the neck and upper back. Males have a grey belly; females show warmer buff and rufous tones below. It lives on São Tomé, Príncipe and Annobón, where it is most often seen among trees in forest, plantations and some altered habitats.
