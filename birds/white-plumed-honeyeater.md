# How to identify the White-plumed Honeyeater (*Ptilotula penicillata*)

This small-to-medium honeyeater is easiest to pick out by the white feather tuft running along each side of its neck. Its upperparts are olive-grey, and yellow tones show on the face and below. Look for it in mainland Australia, often in open woodland near water. It also turns up in parks and gardens, and usually stays close to its home area.
