# How to identify the Crowned Eagle (*Stephanoaetus coronatus*)

A very large, crested eagle with rounded, wide wings and a long tail. Adults show striking black marks across pale underparts, sometimes with a reddish-brown tint on the chest. Listen for repeated, high, wavering calls during a display flight. It lives year-round in wooded parts of sub-Saharan Africa, especially forest and wooded river valleys, and its population is declining.
