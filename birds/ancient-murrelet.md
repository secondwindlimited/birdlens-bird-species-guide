# How to identify the Ancient Murrelet (*Synthliboramphus antiquus*)

A small auk with a dark head, pale collar, grey back and white underparts. In breeding plumage, fine white lines above the eye give the head a streaked look. Its short yellow-tipped bill and quick, low flight help with identification. Look for Ancient Murrelets in small groups on coastal waters, especially near offshore breeding islands around the North Pacific.
