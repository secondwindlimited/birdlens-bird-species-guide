# How to identify the Eastern Chat-Tanager (*Calyptophilus frugivorus*)

The Eastern Chat-Tanager is a slim, long-tailed brown bird, most readily picked out by its bright yellow eye-ring and clean white throat against a dusky breast. It keeps low and often feeds among fallen leaves, so it may be heard before it is seen. Look for it in dense, damp forest in the Sierra de Neiba of western Dominican Republic.
