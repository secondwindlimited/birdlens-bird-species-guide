# How to identify the Hooded Robin (*Melanodryas cucullata*)

The Hooded Robin is a sturdy, roughly 16 cm Australian bird. Adult males are easy to recognise by their black heads and bold white markings on dark wings. Females are much duller grey-brown but retain pale wing bars. It is usually found in dry, open country, often in pairs, perched in view before dropping to the ground to catch prey.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Hooded Robin |
| Scientific name | *Melanodryas cucullata* |

| Genus | *Melanodryas* |
