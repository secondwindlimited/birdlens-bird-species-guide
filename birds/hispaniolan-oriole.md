# How to identify the Hispaniolan Oriole (*Icterus dominicensis*)

Adult Hispaniolan Orioles are mostly black, with clear yellow areas on the shoulders, rump and lower body. They have a slim bill and no white marks on the wings. Look for them in forests and palm-rich places across Hispaniola, sometimes also in gardens and parks. They stay on the island year-round.
