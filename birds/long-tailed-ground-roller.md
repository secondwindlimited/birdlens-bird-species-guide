# How to identify the Long-tailed Ground-Roller (*Uratelornis chimaera*)

The Long-tailed Ground-Roller is a heavy-bodied, leggy bird with an exceptionally extended tail and bold dark bands across its face and breast. It spends much of its time on the ground, where it may slip away through cover rather than allow a close approach. Search in shaded, sandy spiny scrub in a small coastal zone of southwestern Madagascar; it stays there year-round.
