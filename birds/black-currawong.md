# How to identify the Black Currawong (*Strepera fuliginosa*)

A large, crow-sized black bird with a stout dark bill and bright yellow eyes. White marks on the wings and tail can show in flight, though they may be hard to spot. It is found across Tasmania, especially in damp forests, and may also turn up in open country, gardens or highland scrub. Its ringing, flute-like calls are often heard from moving flocks.

## What kind of bird is it?
