# How to identify the Abyssinian Scimitarbill (*Rhinopomastus minor*)

A small, glossy black bird with a long, pointed orange-red bill that bends downward. In flight, northern birds show pale bars on their wings; southern birds lack these markings. Look for it in dry, open bush and thorn scrub in eastern Africa. Its curved bill and black legs help distinguish it from other scimitarbills and woodhoopoes.
