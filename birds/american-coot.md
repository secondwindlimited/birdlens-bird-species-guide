# How to identify the American Coot (*Fulica americana*)

Look for a rounded, gray waterbird with a darker head, bright white bill and pale forehead plate, often marked by a small red-brown patch. Its short wings and tiny tail contrast with large yellow-green feet whose toes have broad lobes. It swims with a rhythmic head-bob and is often seen on ponds, marshes, reservoirs and other waters, including urban parks.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | American Coot |
| Scientific name | *Fulica americana* |

| Genus | *Fulica* |
