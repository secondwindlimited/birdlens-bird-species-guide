# How to identify the Redwing (*Turdus iliacus*)

The Redwing is a small thrush with a pale stripe over each eye and rusty-red patches on its sides. In flight, the orange-red underwings are especially clear. It breeds in northern woods, often among birches, and is most likely to be seen in open country, orchards and parks during winter, often feeding in groups with other thrushes.

|---|---|
