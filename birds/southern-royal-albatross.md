# How to identify the Southern Royal Albatross (*Diomedea epomophora*)

Look for an enormous albatross with a white back and a clean black-and-white appearance. At sea, it ranges across temperate and subantarctic waters of the Southern Hemisphere; it nests on islands south of New Zealand. A pink bill with a narrow black line along its upper cutting edge is a useful close-up clue, especially when comparing it with wandering albatrosses.
