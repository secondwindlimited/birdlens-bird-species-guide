# How to identify the Cliff Swallow (*Petrochelidon pyrrhonota*)

A compact swallow with rounded wings, a stubby square-ended tail and a pale orange-buff patch above the tail. Its dark bib and light forehead help distinguish it from many other swallows. Look for it across much of North America, often around bridges and culverts in the breeding season; it leaves for South America in winter.

## What kind of bird is it?

| Common name | Cliff Swallow |
| Scientific name | *Petrochelidon pyrrhonota* |
