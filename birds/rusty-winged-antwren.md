# How to identify the Rusty-winged Antwren (*Herpsilochmus frater*)

Look for a small, long-tailed antwren with rusty-red wing edges, bold white wing bars, a dark line through the eye and a yellow underside. The male has a black crown; the female’s is chestnut. It is usually seen in pairs in lowland or foothill forest, often in mixed-species feeding flocks. Its quick, rough trill speeds up and deepens into a growl.
