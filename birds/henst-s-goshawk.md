# How to identify the Henst's Goshawk (*Astur henstii*)

Henst’s Goshawk is a large, powerful hawk of Madagascar’s forests. Adults are grey above and finely barred underneath; young birds are browner, with heavier markings. It usually keeps to the trees, so listen for its repeated, loud “khew” call, sometimes given in flight. It is a resident but scarce, and is found mainly in forested parts of the island.
