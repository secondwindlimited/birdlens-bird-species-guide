# How to identify the Tawny Eagle (*Aquila rapax*)

A medium-large eagle with variable brown, buff or rufous plumage, a strong bill and conspicuously feathered legs. Its broad, slightly rounded tail and dark flight feathers help with identification. Tawny Eagles are most often encountered over open country, including plains and scrub, across much of Africa and parts of South Asia. They wander rather than following a regular migration route.
