# How to identify the Guianan Cock-of-the-rock (*Rupicola rupicola*)

Look for a stocky, roughly 30 cm cotinga with a bold, curved crest. Adult males are vivid orange, while females and young birds are brown-grey and have smaller crests; their pale eyes can help with identification. The species lives in humid forest across the Guiana Shield, especially near rocky outcrops. It may cross a road or stream in flight, but is often hard to find away from display sites.
