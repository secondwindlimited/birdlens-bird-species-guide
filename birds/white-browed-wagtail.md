# How to identify the White-browed Wagtail (*Motacilla maderaspatensis*)

Look for a large, black-and-white wagtail with a bold white stripe over the eye and a broad pale mark across each wing. Its long tail is in constant motion, and its flight rises and dips. It lives year-round on the Indian subcontinent and is often seen near freshwater, from natural wet ground to small pools around buildings, usually in pairs or family groups.
