# How to identify the Brown Treecreeper (*Climacteris picumnus*)

The Brown Treecreeper is a slim, brown-grey bird with a light face, a noticeable pale eyebrow and fine markings below. It climbs tree trunks, often circling upwards, but may also feed on stumps or on the ground. Look for it in open woodland and savannah across eastern Australia. Its repeated, brief whistles can help locate it.
