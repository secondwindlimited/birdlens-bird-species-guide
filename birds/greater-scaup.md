# How to identify the Greater Scaup (*Aythya marila*)

Greater Scaup is a robust diving duck with a smoothly curved head and a broad, bluish bill. Adult males show a dark, green-glossed head, black chest and pale sides; females are brown, often with a conspicuous pale area beside the bill. It is most often seen in winter on coastal bays and sheltered sea inlets, though flocks also use lakes.
