# How to identify the Kea (*Nestor notabilis*)

Look for a large olive-green parrot with orange-red on its rump and bright orange beneath the wings, especially in flight. The kea lives only on New Zealand’s South Island, where it is often seen in mountain country, including near roads, huts and ski areas. It may approach people and vehicles, and its loud, wavering call is a useful clue. Its population is declining.
