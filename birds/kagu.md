# How to identify the Kagu (*Rhynochetos jubatus*)

The kagu is a tall, ground-living bird with soft blue-grey plumage, a red bill and red legs. Its loose crest lies flat until it is raised in display. Listen for drawn-out nasal hoots and cackling, often from two birds calling together before sunrise. It occurs only on New Caledonia’s main island, in forest and some shrubland.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Kagu |
| Scientific name | *Rhynochetos jubatus* |

| Genus | *Rhynochetos* |
