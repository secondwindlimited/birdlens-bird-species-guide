# How to identify the White-throated Swift (*Aeronautes saxatalis*)

A swift with a dark back and wings and conspicuous white underparts, including a bright throat and white patches near the rump. It is usually seen cutting rapidly through the air near rocky cliffs, especially in desert country. Listen for a falling run of rough, squealing calls. Northern breeding birds migrate south, while some populations remain in the south year-round.
