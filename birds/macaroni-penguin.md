# How to identify the Macaroni Penguin (*Eudyptes chrysolophus*)

Look for a stocky penguin with a thick orange-brown bill and a vivid yellow-orange plume sweeping back from the forehead. Its dark head and back contrast with white underparts; red eyes and pink feet offer extra clues. It is most often encountered in loud breeding groups on rocky subantarctic islands, though birds travel widely at sea to find food.
