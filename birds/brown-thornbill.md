# How to identify the Brown Thornbill (*Acanthiza pusilla*)

A small, slim thornbill with a fine pointed bill, warm brown back and conspicuous dark-red eye. Check for a russet forehead and a greyish throat marked with dark streaks. Brown Thornbills are most often found in dense shrubs and undergrowth in eastern and south-eastern Australia, including Tasmania, usually in pairs or small groups.
