# How to identify the Black-crowned Antshrike (*Thamnophilus atrinucha*)

The Black-crowned Antshrike is a small, stout-billed bird of tropical forest. Males are gray with a dark cap, black wings marked in white, and a black tail with white tips; females are brown and paler below. It is usually found in pairs, feeding in low or middle foliage around forest edges, plantations, open woodland, and second growth. It remains in its range year-round.
