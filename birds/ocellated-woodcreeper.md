# How to identify the Ocellated Woodcreeper (*Xiphorhynchus ocellatus*)

Look for a slender woodcreeper about 20–24.5 cm long. Its upperparts are mostly brown, with cinnamon wings and tail; buff marks break up the head and breast, while the throat has a scaly look. Listen for nasal notes that fall in pitch. It lives in western Amazonian forest, south of the Amazon, often below the canopy alone or with feeding flocks.
