# How to identify the Gold-ringed Tanager (*Bangsia aureocincta*)

Look for a compact, dark tanager with a vivid yellow arc behind the eye, a yellow cheek stripe and an orange-yellow breast centre. These marks stand out against green underparts and dark wings edged in blue. The species lives in wet cloud forest on Colombia’s western Andean slopes. It may feed in mixed flocks or call from an exposed perch.
