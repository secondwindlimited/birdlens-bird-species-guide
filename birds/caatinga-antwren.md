# How to identify the Caatinga Antwren (*Radinopsyche sellowi*)

Look for a small gray antbird with a long, stepped tail. The male has a dark cap, a white eyebrow and a narrow black mark behind the eye; the female is warmer buff below, with buff edging on the crown. Its quick rattling song helps identify it. It lives in Brazil’s dry Caatinga woodlands and scrub, sometimes joining mixed-species flocks.
