# How to identify the Rufous Hummingbird (*Selasphorus rufus*)

A tiny hummingbird, often recognised by the male’s rusty-orange body, pale chest and flashing throat, which changes colour with the light. Females and young birds are greener but show rusty tones on the sides and tail. Look for this complete migrant in wooded country across western North America, or in brushier places, meadows and suburbs while it is travelling.
