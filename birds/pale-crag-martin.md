# How to identify the Pale Crag-Martin (*Ptyonoprogne obsoleta*)

Look for a small, plain gray-brown martin with a short, nearly square tail marked by white patches. Its pale throat and lack of a breast band help distinguish it from sand martins; the tail marks are useful at a distance. It is most likely around rocky slopes, ravines and cliffs in dry parts of North Africa and the Middle East, but also nests on buildings.
