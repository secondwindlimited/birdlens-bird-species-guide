# How to identify the Yellow-throated Euphonia (*Euphonia hirundinacea*)

A tiny canopy finch with a short bill and tail. The male is glossy blue-black above, with a yellow forehead mark and bright yellow below; the female is olive above, with pale central underparts and yellowish sides. Look for it in tropical forest, wooded clearings and gardens, often in pairs or feeding with other euphonias at mistletoe.
