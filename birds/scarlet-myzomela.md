# How to identify the Scarlet Myzomela (*Myzomela sanguinolenta*)

A tiny honeyeater, the Scarlet Myzomela is easiest to pick out when a male’s vivid red head, upper breast and back show among the dark wings. Females and young birds are much plainer, with brown-grey plumage and sometimes a little red on the face. It occurs in eastern Australia, often feeding high in trees where its clear, tinkling song may reveal it first.
