# How to identify the Wallcreeper (*Tichodroma muraria*)

A small, blue-grey bird that creeps over cliffs and boulders, often hard to pick out while its wings are folded. In flight, broad red wings marked with black and white make it striking and distinctive. Look for it on steep mountain rock faces, or at lower elevations in winter, when it may visit quarries and tall buildings.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Wallcreeper |
| Scientific name | *Tichodroma muraria* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Tichodromidae |
| Genus | *Tichodroma* |
