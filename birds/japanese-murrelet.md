# How to identify the Japanese Murrelet (*Synthliboramphus wumizusume*)

Look for a small, rounded auk with a dark blue-grey back, white underside and a short, heavy bill. In the breeding season, its raised crest and white plumes help distinguish it from the similar Ancient Murrelet. It lives around Japanese island coasts and may be seen offshore, or sometimes in sheltered bays and harbours in late winter and spring. Its population is declining.
