# How to identify the Rufous Bristlebird (*Dasyornis broadbenti*)

A skulking, red-brown bird with a long, ragged-tipped tail, the Rufous Bristlebird is usually noticed moving through dense vegetation rather than sitting in view. Look for pale markings around the eye and along the face, plus a speckled grey-brown underside. It lives in coastal heath and shrubland in south-eastern South Australia and western Victoria, where its clear repeated notes may reveal its presence.
