# How to identify the Hairy-breasted Barbet (*Tricholaema hirsuta*)

A large, dark barbet with yellow spots on its back and yellow, black-marked underparts. Its head pattern changes across its range, but the dark throat and substantial bill help identify it. It lives in humid forest in West and Central Africa and parts of East Africa, usually at low or middle elevations. Listen for a slow sequence of deep, hollow hoots.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Hairy-breasted Barbet |
| Scientific name | *Tricholaema hirsuta* |
| Order | Piciformes |
| Family | Lybiidae |
| Genus | *Tricholaema* |
