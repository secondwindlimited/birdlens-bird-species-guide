# How to identify the Buff-throated Woodcreeper (*Xiphorhynchus guttatus*)

Look for a large, warm reddish-brown woodcreeper with pale buff marks on the head and breast, plus a substantial, down-curved bill. Its throat can look plain because the buff patch is often hidden. It occurs in lowland forests and along wooded edges across much of South America. Though it may be heard before it is spotted, listen for a repeated, rising and falling chain of clear “chew” notes.
