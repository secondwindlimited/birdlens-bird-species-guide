# How to identify the Spruce Grouse (*Canachites canadensis*)

The Spruce Grouse is a compact forest grouse, usually encountered quietly on the ground or beside a track in northern conifer woods. Males are grey and black, with a red patch above each eye and white marks along the flanks; females are brown and barred. It occurs across much of Canada and in scattered northern and mountain forests of the United States, and stays in the same broad region year-round.
