# How to identify the Dusky-tailed Canastero (*Pseudasthenes humicola*)

Look for a small brown canastero with a pale eyebrow, cinnamon-colored sides and a dark tail. It usually keeps among shrubs, where it can be difficult to spot, but may dash across open ground with its tail raised. In Chile it occurs in scrubby lowlands and foothills, often in pairs. Bulky stick nests in bushes can reveal its presence.
