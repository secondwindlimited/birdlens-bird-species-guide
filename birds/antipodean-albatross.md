# How to identify the Antipodean Albatross (*Diomedea antipodensis*)

A very large albatross, about 110 cm long, with long wings and plumage that ranges from mostly brown in young birds to much whiter in older ones. It breeds on islands around New Zealand and spends the rest of the year over the South Pacific. At sea, its appearance can help separate it from a Southern Royal Albatross, but other wandering-type albatrosses can be difficult to tell apart.
