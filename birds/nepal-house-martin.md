# How to identify the Nepal House-Martin (*Delichon nipalense*)

A compact martin with dark, sometimes blue-sheened upperparts, pale underparts and a bright white rump. The dark chin—and, in some populations, a larger dark throat patch—helps separate it from other house-martins. It lives mainly among Himalayan and nearby mountain valleys and wooded ridges, where it hunts insects in flight. It usually stays in the region year-round, though it may move downhill outside the breeding season.
