# How to identify the Blue-breasted Bee-eater (*Merops variegatus*)

Look for a green bee-eater with a bright yellow throat, a pale cheek and a broad breast band that may look almost black. In flight, rufous patches and dark bars show on its wings and tail. It is a resident of central Africa, where it frequents damp, open places such as marsh edges, grassy savanna and cultivated land.

|---|---|
