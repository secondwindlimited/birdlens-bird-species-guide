# How to identify the Harpy Eagle (*Harpia harpyja*)

A huge forest eagle with a pale head, a dark double crest and a broad dark stripe across its chest. Its powerful feet and exceptionally large talons are striking at close range. It is most often encountered sitting high in a mature rainforest tree, or making a direct flight over a road or river. The species is elusive and uncommon across much of its range.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Harpy Eagle |
| Scientific name | *Harpia harpyja* |
| Order | Accipitriformes |
| Family | Accipitridae |
| Genus | *Harpia* |
