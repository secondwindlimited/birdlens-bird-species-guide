# How to identify the Palm-nut Vulture (*Gypohierax angolensis*)

The adult Palm-nut Vulture is a compact, striking bird of prey, with a mostly white body, dark wing and tail feathers, and bare red skin around the eyes. Its broad wings and stubby tail give it a distinctive outline in flight. It occurs in palm-rich parts of sub-Saharan Africa, especially near coastal forest and wetlands; young birds are brown.
