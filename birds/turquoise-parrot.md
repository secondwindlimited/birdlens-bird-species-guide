# How to identify the Turquoise Parrot (*Neophema pulchella*)

The Turquoise Parrot is a small, slim bird with green upperparts, a yellow underside and blue markings on its face and wings. Adult males show a stronger blue face and a red shoulder patch; females look duller. Look for it in eastern Australia, especially in open woodland near grassland, where small groups may feed on the ground.
