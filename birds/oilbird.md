# How to identify the Oilbird (*Steatornis caripensis*)

The Oilbird is a large, russet-brown night bird with small white marks on its wings, body and tail. Its hooked, pale pink bill and loose, wavering flight make it distinctive at twilight. Look for it around forested mountain slopes in northern South America, especially near cave or gorge colonies; away from these sites it is often hard to find.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Oilbird |
| Scientific name | *Steatornis caripensis* |
| Order | Steatornithiformes |
| Family | Steatornithidae |
| Genus | *Steatornis* |
