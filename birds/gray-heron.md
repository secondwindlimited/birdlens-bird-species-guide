# How to identify the Gray Heron (*Ardea cinerea*)

A tall, long-legged gray bird with a pale neck and a straight, dagger-like bill, the Gray Heron is often seen waiting motionless beside shallow water. Adults show a white head with a dark stripe above the eye. It occurs across much of Europe, Asia and Africa, in places ranging from tidal shorelines and marshes to ponds, ditches and wet fields.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Gray Heron |
| Scientific name | *Ardea cinerea* |
| Order | Pelecaniformes |
| Family | Ardeidae |
| Genus | *Ardea* |
