# How to identify the Himalayan Rubythroat (*Calliope pectoralis*)

Look for this shy bird in thick scrub or tall grass in the high mountains of Central and South Asia. The male stands out with a scarlet-orange throat between a white brow and a dark face and breast, plus a pale belly and white on the tail. Females are much duller brown-grey, with a faint pale eyebrow and smoky underparts.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Himalayan Rubythroat |
| Scientific name | *Calliope pectoralis* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Muscicapidae |
| Genus | *Calliope* |
