# How to identify the Western Capercaillie (*Tetrao urogallus*)

The Western Capercaillie is an exceptionally large grouse, most often found in mature conifer woodland with open ground nearby. The male is dark, with a long tail and a pale wing mark; the smaller, patterned female is easier to overlook. In early spring, males gather around dawn to display. The species stays in its range year-round.
