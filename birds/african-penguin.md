# How to identify the African Penguin (*Spheniscus demersus*)

A stocky penguin with a dark back, pale face and belly, and a single dark band across its chest. It lives along the southwestern coast of Africa and is the only penguin regularly found there. Look for it at coastal breeding colonies, where it waddles on land; at sea, it swims and dives after small fish. Its loud, repeated braying call is distinctive.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | African Penguin |
| Scientific name | *Spheniscus demersus* |
| Order | Sphenisciformes |
| Family | Spheniscidae |
| Genus | *Spheniscus* |
