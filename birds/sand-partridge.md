# How to identify the Sand Partridge (*Ammoperdix heyi*)

The Sand Partridge is a small, rounded bird with sandy-brown plumage and a grey-headed male marked by a white cheek patch and barred flanks. It usually runs over rocky ground rather than taking flight. Listen for the male’s repeated, carrying call, or the sharp whistle of its wings as it lifts off. It lives year-round in arid parts of the Middle East.
