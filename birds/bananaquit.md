# How to identify the Bananaquit (*Coereba flaveola*)

A tiny, busy bird with a narrow bill that bends slightly down at the tip. Most have dark head and back plumage, a bright white stripe above the eye, and yellow below. Look for Bananaquits around tropical forest margins, in open woodland, and in gardens. Their colours vary between islands, but their small size and bold face pattern help identify them.

## What kind of bird is it?
