# How to identify the Trinidad Euphonia (*Euphonia trinitatis*)

A compact, short-tailed finch with a short bill, the Trinidad Euphonia is easiest to recognise by the male’s dark blue-black head and throat set against yellow crown and belly. Both sexes show conspicuous white beneath the tail. Look for it in pairs in woodland, scrub and tree-filled cultivated areas across northern South America and Trinidad.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Trinidad Euphonia |
| Scientific name | *Euphonia trinitatis* |
| Order | Passeriformes |

| Genus | *Euphonia* |
