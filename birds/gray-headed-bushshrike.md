# How to identify the Gray-headed Bushshrike (*Malaconotus blanchoti*)

Look for a bulky bushshrike with a broad gray head, a strong hooked black bill, a vivid yellow eye and a small pale mark just ahead of it. It is usually seen alone or in pairs in thorn scrub, woodland or thick growth beside streams. Listen for a low, mournful whistle repeated in long series; the bird may remain hidden in foliage.
