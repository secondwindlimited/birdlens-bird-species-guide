# How to identify the Carolina Wren (*Thryothorus ludovicianus*)

The Carolina Wren is a fairly large, warm-brown wren with pale, unmarked underparts and a clear white stripe above the eye. It often holds its tail upright. Look for it in dense shrubs, tangled edges and gardens across much of eastern North America. Its loud, ringing song can reveal a bird that is otherwise hard to see.

## What kind of bird is it?

| Common name | Carolina Wren |
| Scientific name | *Thryothorus ludovicianus* |
