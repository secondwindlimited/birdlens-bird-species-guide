# How to identify the Bateleur (*Terathopius ecaudatus*)

The Bateleur is a large, short-tailed eagle with broad wings and a striking red face and legs in adult birds. In flight, it often travels low over open country, holding its wings above its body and swaying from side to side. Look for it over savanna and woodland across much of sub-Saharan Africa; it is generally resident, though some birds move about.
