# How to identify the Rufous-backed Stipplethroat (*Epinecrophylla haematonota*)

Small and often hard to spot, this antwren is best picked out by a male’s spotted black-and-white throat, pale wing marks and rufous-toned back. Females are plainer brown, without a bold throat pattern. It occurs in Amazonian rainforest, usually low in the understory, where birders are most likely to find it moving with other small forest birds.
