# How to identify the Crested Myna (*Acridotheres cristatellus*)

Look for a glossy black myna with a short tuft above its pale yellow bill. White areas flash on the wings in flight, and the tail has white tips. Across much of its Asian range, it is often seen in small groups around towns, parks, farms and roadsides. It may feed near livestock or on recently worked ground.
