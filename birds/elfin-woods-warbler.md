# How to identify the Elfin-woods Warbler (*Setophaga angelae*)

A small black-and-white warbler of Puerto Rico’s mountain forests, it has a dark crown, a streaked white underside and white markings around the face. Watch for it moving quickly through dense foliage in the canopy and among small branch tips. Its fine, high song and thin “Peet” call can be hard to hear, but help distinguish it from other warblers in the same forests.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|

| Genus | *Setophaga* |
