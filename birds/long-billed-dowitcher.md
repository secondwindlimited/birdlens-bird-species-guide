# How to identify the Long-billed Dowitcher (*Limnodromus scolopaceus*)

A stocky shorebird with a long, straight bill and a conspicuous white patch across the rump. In breeding plumage it has rich rusty-red underparts; in winter it is mostly gray. Its sharp, high “keek!” call helps separate it from the similar Short-billed Dowitcher. Look for it around shallow wetlands, both on its northern breeding grounds and farther south during migration and winter.
