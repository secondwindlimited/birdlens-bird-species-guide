# How to identify the Olive-gray Saltator (*Saltator olivascens*)

A sturdy, mostly gray saltator with a heavy dark bill, a narrow white mark above the eye and black lines bordering its pale throat. Adults show little olive colouring despite the name; young birds look more yellow-green. It lives in northern South America, where it is often found in scrubby growth, along woodland margins and around gardens.
