# How to identify the Sapphire-throated Hummingbird (*Chrysuronia coeruleogularis*)

Look for a compact hummingbird with a long, deeply forked tail and bright green body. The male has a broad blue-violet throat patch, which may seem black when light is poor; the female is pale underneath, with green flecks on the breast. It occurs in Panama and northern Colombia, especially in open coastal woods, scrub, and around flowering trees.
