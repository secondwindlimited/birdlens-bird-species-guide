# How to identify the Palmchat (*Dulus dominicus*)

The Palmchat is easy to recognise by its olive-brown back, streaked pale underside, sturdy yellow bill and reddish eye. Listen for its varied squeaks, buzzes and whistles. It lives on Hispaniola and nearby islands, where small groups are often seen high in palm crowns. It is distinctive across its range, with no regular lookalike noted.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Palmchat |
| Scientific name | *Dulus dominicus* |
