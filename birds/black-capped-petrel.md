# How to identify the Black-capped Petrel (*Pterodroma hasitata*)

A large, long-winged black-and-white petrel, usually seen far from land. Its dark, hooded head contrasts with a white collar and rump. At sea, look for it over the Gulf Stream, especially off the southeastern United States and in the Caribbean. It comes ashore to nest on steep mountain cliffs, where adults visit their burrows after dark.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Black-capped Petrel |
| Scientific name | *Pterodroma hasitata* |
| Order | Procellariiformes |
| Family | Procellariidae |
| Genus | *Pterodroma* |
