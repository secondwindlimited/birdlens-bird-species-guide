# How to identify the Wing-banded Antbird (*Myrmornis torquata*)

A compact, low-set antbird with a very short tail, a large bill and pale buff bars across dark wings. Males have a black throat; females show a warm rufous breast. It lives in humid forest from Central America into the Amazon region and is usually found near the forest floor, where it turns over leaves for food. It is resident but often hard to find.
