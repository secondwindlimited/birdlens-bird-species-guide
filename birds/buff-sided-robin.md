# How to identify the Buff-sided Robin (*Poecilodryas cerviniventris*)

Look for a small, rounded robin in dense greenery beside creeks and in wet forest across northern Australia. It has a brown back, pale belly, broad white eyebrow, dark face, and warm rufous colouring along the sides. White patches show in the wings and tail. It often holds its tail upright and gives a soft, repeated whistle.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Buff-sided Robin |
| Scientific name | *Poecilodryas cerviniventris* |
| Order | Passeriformes |

| Genus | *Poecilodryas* |
