# How to identify the Canada Goose (*Branta canadensis*)

Look for a large brown goose with a dark head and neck and a white mark curving under the throat. Canada Geese are often seen in family groups or flocks on lakes, marshes, lawns and other open ground. Their loud honking is distinctive, especially when groups pass overhead in a V-shaped line. They are widespread across the United States and Canada.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
