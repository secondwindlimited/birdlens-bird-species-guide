# How to identify the Brolga (*Antigone rubicunda*)

The Brolga is a very tall, silvery-grey crane with long legs, a narrow neck and conspicuous red skin on its head, including a hanging throat pouch. Unlike the similar Sarus Crane, its red colouring does not extend down the neck. Look for it in wetlands, open floodplains and grasslands in northern and eastern Australia.
