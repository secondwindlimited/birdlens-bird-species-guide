# How to identify the Black-necked Stork (*Ephippiorhynchus asiaticus*)

A very large stork with a black head and neck, a huge dark bill, and long red legs. In flight, look for its white body and wings crossed by a broad black panel; it flies with its neck held straight out. It lives around wetlands and ponds across parts of South and Southeast Asia, New Guinea, and northern Australia, and is usually seen alone or in pairs.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Black-necked Stork |
| Scientific name | *Ephippiorhynchus asiaticus* |
| Order | Ciconiiformes |
| Family | Ciconiidae |
| Genus | *Ephippiorhynchus* |
