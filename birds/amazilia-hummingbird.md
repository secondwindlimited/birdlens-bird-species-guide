# How to identify the Amazilia Hummingbird (*Amazilis amazilia*)

A small hummingbird with a straight, pinkish-red bill ending in a dark tip. Most birds show green upperparts, a bright green throat, a pale breast, and chestnut on the belly and tail, though colouring changes across the range. Look for it in western Ecuador and Peru, especially in dry scrub, thorny woodland, gardens, and other open places.
