# How to identify the White-winged Warbler (*Xenoligea montana*)

At 13–14 cm, the White-winged Warbler is a small forest bird with an olive-green back, gray head and pale underside. A conspicuous white mark across the dark wing is the key field clue. Search dense low growth in high mountain forests of Hispaniola, especially where birds forage in pairs or join mixed feeding groups. Its sharp, high notes may reveal it before it is seen.
