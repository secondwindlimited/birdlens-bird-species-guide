# How to identify the Crescent Honeyeater (*Phylidonyris pyrrhopterus*)

A small honeyeater with bright yellow areas on its wings and curved dark marks on the male’s breast. Females are browner, with less distinct markings, while young birds show little or no breast pattern. It is most often found in dense, damp forest in Tasmania and southeastern mainland Australia, though some birds move to lower ground in colder weather.
