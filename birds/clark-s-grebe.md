# How to identify the Clark's Grebe (*Aechmophorus clarkii*)

Clark’s Grebe is a large, long-necked bird with a gray body, a white face, and a bright yellow-orange bill. The black cap ends before the red eye, making the eye stand out. It is most often seen on marshy lakes in the breeding season, then on Pacific bays and open coastal water in winter, though some remain on lakes.
