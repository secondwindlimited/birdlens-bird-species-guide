# How to identify the Olive Warbler (*Peucedramus taeniatus*)

An Olive Warbler is best picked out by the male’s orange head, dark eye stripe and paired white wing marks on a grey body. Females and young birds have yellow rather than orange on the head. Watch the upper branches of mountain conifer forests, where it may feed among other small songbirds; its repeated, tuneful phrases and sharp “peew” call can reveal it before it is seen.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Olive Warbler |
| Scientific name | *Peucedramus taeniatus* |
