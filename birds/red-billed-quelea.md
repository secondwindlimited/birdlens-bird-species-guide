# How to identify the Red-billed Quelea (*Quelea quelea*)

Look for a sparrow-sized weaver with a stubby tail, streaked brown back and stout, cone-shaped bill. The bill and legs change colour with sex and season; breeding males usually show a dark facial patch bordered by pale or warm tones. Red-billed Queleas gather in huge flocks across open African grasslands, thorn scrub and farmland, often close to water.
