# How to identify the Asian Woolly-necked Stork (*Ciconia episcopus*)

Look for a tall, dark stork with a shaggy white neck, pale lower belly and a white tail that is often hidden at rest. Long red legs and a heavy bill, usually dark with a reddish tip, add useful clues. In tropical Asia it is most often seen around wetlands and farmland, feeding alone or in small groups.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Asian Woolly-necked Stork |
| Scientific name | *Ciconia episcopus* |

| Genus | *Ciconia* |
