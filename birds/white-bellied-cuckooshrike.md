# How to identify the White-bellied Cuckooshrike (*Coracina papuensis*)

The White-bellied Cuckooshrike is a slim, pale gray bird with a dark mark beside the bill, a light underside and a faint pale eye rim. It is smaller and more compact than the similar Black-faced Cuckooshrike. Watch for it in woodland and eucalypt forest across Australia and nearby islands; it may also reveal itself with a sharp, high two-part squeak.
