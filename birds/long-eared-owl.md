# How to identify the Long-eared Owl (*Asio otus*)

Look for a slim, medium-sized owl with long upright tufts set near the middle of its head. Its streaked plumage and striking facial disc help, but it is often hard to find because it hunts mainly at night. It occurs across much of the Northern Hemisphere, especially where wooded or brushy roosting cover lies close to open hunting ground.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
