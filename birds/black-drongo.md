# How to identify the Black Drongo (*Dicrurus macrocercus*)

Look for a sleek, all-black bird with a deeply notched tail, sitting conspicuously on a wire or exposed branch. In good light its feathers show a faint blue-green sheen. It is often encountered over fields, pasture, wetlands, and village edges, where it darts after insects and may boldly drive larger birds away.
