# How to identify the Gray Grasswren (*Amytornis barbatus*)

The Gray Grasswren is a small, pale brown bird with fine dark streaks and a long, narrow tail often held upright. A bold black-and-white face pattern helps distinguish it from other plain floodplain birds. It occurs in a tiny area of arid inland Australia, especially among dense Lignum on river and lake floodplains. It can be very hard to find without a focused search.
