# How to identify the Chatham Albatross (*Thalassarche eremita*)

At sea, look for a Chatham Albatross with a dark grey head, white body and yellow bill marked with black near the tip. The underside of the wing is mostly white, with a fine dark edge and black tips. It breeds on The Pyramid in New Zealand’s Chatham Islands and ranges across the South Pacific when away from its colony.
