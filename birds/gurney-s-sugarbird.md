# How to identify the Gurney's Sugarbird (*Promerops gurneyi*)

A large nectar-feeder with a curved bill, a long tapering tail and a bright yellow rump. Look for a rusty crown and a wide russet band across the chest, set against a pale underside. Gurney’s Sugarbird is most often found among flowering Protea shrubs in southern African mountain country, though it may move to lower ground when flowering ends.
