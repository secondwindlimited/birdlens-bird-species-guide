# How to identify the Golden-headed Quetzal (*Pharomachrus auriceps*)

A large, green-and-red trogon of Andean and nearby mountain forests, the Golden-headed Quetzal is recognised by its vivid red underside, dark tail and dark eye. Males show a bronze-gold crown and yellow bill; females have a brown head and muted bill. It often sits quietly below the canopy, especially near a fruiting tree, and may be heard giving a plaintive, repeated “go home” call.
