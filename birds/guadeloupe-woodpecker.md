# How to identify the Guadeloupe Woodpecker (*Melanerpes herminieri*)

This stocky, 26–28 cm woodpecker is almost wholly black, with a dark red to burgundy wash beneath. It lives only on Guadeloupe, where it can be found in forest, woodland, mangroves and swampy areas, from the coast to high mountain forest. Listen for a rough, drawn-out rasp or a quick series of clipped notes.
