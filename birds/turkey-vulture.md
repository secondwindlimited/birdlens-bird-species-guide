# How to identify the Turkey Vulture (*Cathartes aura*)

Look for a broad-winged, dark soaring bird with a small bare red head. From below, its wings show pale gray along much of the rear edge, rather than only at the tips as in a Black Vulture. Its wings tilt upward in a shallow V and it often rocks from side to side. It searches low over open ground and is common across the Americas.
