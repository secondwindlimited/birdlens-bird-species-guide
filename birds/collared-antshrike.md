# How to identify the Collared Antshrike (*Thamnophilus bernardi*)

Look for a compact, crested antshrike with a heavy hooked bill, patterned wings and a sharply contrasting face. Males show a dark head and chest against pale underparts; females are warmer brown and buff. It lives in dry country on the Pacific side of Ecuador and Peru, with a separate population in the Marañón basin. Listen for a rapid, nasal trill.
