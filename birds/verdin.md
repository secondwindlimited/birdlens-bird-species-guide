# How to identify the Verdin (*Auriparus flaviceps*)

Look for a tiny, gray bird with a bright yellow head and a fine, pointed bill in dry southwestern scrub. Adults may also show a rusty shoulder mark. Verdins often forage alone or with one companion, picking insects from shrubs, especially mesquite. Their small size can suggest a Bushtit, but the sharply tapered bill is a useful clue.

## What kind of bird is it?
