# How to identify the Tree Swallow (*Tachycineta bicolor*)

Look for a compact swallow with bright white below and a shining blue-green back in adult males. Fairly broad wings and a shallowly forked tail give it a neat outline in flight. Females and young birds tend to be browner above. It occurs across much of North America, often over open country near ponds, marshes and grassy ground.
