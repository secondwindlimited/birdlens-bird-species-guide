# How to identify the Snares Penguin (*Eudyptes robustus*)

A medium-sized penguin with a dark upper body, white underside and a narrow golden brow that extends into a loose crest behind each eye. Its thick reddish bill has a striking pink patch at the base. It breeds only on New Zealand’s Snares Islands, nesting in colonies beneath forest cover or on rocky shores; outside the breeding season, it disperses across nearby seas.

| Common name | Snares Penguin |
| Scientific name | *Eudyptes robustus* |

| Genus | *Eudyptes* |
