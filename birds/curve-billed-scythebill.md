# How to identify the Curve-billed Scythebill (*Campylorhamphus procurvoides*)

A slim, brown woodcreeper with a very long bill that bends sharply downwards. Pale markings streak its head and neck, while the tail and flight feathers are a warmer chestnut. It lives in Amazonian rainforest and nearby regions, where it climbs through the middle and upper forest, often in bamboo-rich areas. It is shy and can be hard to find.
