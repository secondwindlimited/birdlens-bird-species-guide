# How to identify the Nicobar Pigeon (*Caloenas nicobarica*)

A large island pigeon with a rounded body and long, shimmering neck feathers is distinctive at close range. Its short white tail is easiest to see when it takes flight. The bird is usually wary and can be difficult to find. Listen for its repeated, low call in forest on small islands across the Indo-Pacific, from the Andamans towards New Guinea and the Solomons.
