# How to identify the Red-headed Myzomela (*Myzomela erythrocephala*)

Look for a tiny, compact honeyeater with a short tail in coastal mangroves. Adult males are unmistakable: red covers the head and rump, set against dark wings and back and pale grey-brown lower parts. Females and young birds are duller, with little or no red. The species occurs along tropical northern Australia and in parts of New Guinea and nearby islands.
