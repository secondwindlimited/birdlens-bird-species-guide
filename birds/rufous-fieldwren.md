# How to identify the Rufous Fieldwren (*Calamanthus campestris*)

A small, muted-brown bird with a pale eyebrow, light underside and long tail usually held upright. It often stays out of sight while feeding on the ground or among low, dense shrubs. Look for it in dry, open shrubland, heath and grassland across southern mainland Australia. In spring, a singing bird perched in low vegetation may be easier to spot.
