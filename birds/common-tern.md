# How to identify the Common Tern (*Sterna hirundo*)

A medium-sized tern with a pale grey body, black cap and deeply forked tail. In breeding plumage, its narrow bill is usually red with a dark tip, though some birds have an all-black bill. Look for it over open water, along beaches and beside large lakes. It often gathers in flocks, especially towards the end of summer.
