# How to identify the Great Frigatebird (*Fregata minor*)

Look for a very large, mostly black seabird with long, narrow wings held in a bent shape and a forked tail. Males have a red throat pouch; females show a white breast, while young birds have a pale head and breast. It occurs around tropical ocean islands and may be seen soaring high over shorelines or out at sea.
