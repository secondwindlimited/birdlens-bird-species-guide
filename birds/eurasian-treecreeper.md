# How to identify the Eurasian Treecreeper (*Certhia familiaris*)

A small, bark-coloured bird with a slim, curved bill and pale underparts, the Eurasian Treecreeper is easiest to spot as it climbs a tree trunk in short, looping paths. It searches bark for tiny prey, bracing itself with its stiff tail. Look for it in mature woodland, parks and gardens with large trees; listen for thin, high calls.
