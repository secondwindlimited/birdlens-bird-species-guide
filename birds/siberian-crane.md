# How to identify the Siberian Crane (*Leucogeranus leucogeranus*)

An adult Siberian Crane is a large, almost entirely white bird, with a bare red face, dark bill and black flight feathers that show in flight. Its pink legs and yellowish eyes provide further clues. Young birds have brownish feathers across the head and upperparts. Look for it in wetlands and nearby fields; outside the breeding season, many gather at Poyang Lake in China.
