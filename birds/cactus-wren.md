# How to identify the Cactus Wren (*Campylorhynchus brunneicapillus*)

A chunky, long-tailed wren, the Cactus Wren is easiest to pick out by its pale stripe above the eye and heavy dark spotting on the breast. It lives year-round in the arid Southwest of the United States and parts of Mexico. Look for it among spiny cacti and desert scrub, often in pairs or family groups, and listen for its rough, rasping calls.
