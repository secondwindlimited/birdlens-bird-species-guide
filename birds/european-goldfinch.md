# How to identify the European Goldfinch (*Carduelis carduelis*)

A compact finch with a red face, pale bill and bright yellow bars across black wings is usually identifiable even at a distance. European Goldfinches occur across much of Europe, North Africa and western and central Asia. Look for them in gardens, scrub and open woodland, especially where thistles or other seed-bearing plants are available; outside the breeding season they often gather in flocks.
