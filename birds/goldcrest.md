# How to identify the Goldcrest (*Regulus regulus*)

At roughly 9 cm long, the Goldcrest is a tiny olive-green bird with pale underparts, a bold yellow crown patch edged in black and a bright white wingbar. Its large dark eye stands out on an otherwise plain face. Look for it moving quickly among the upper branches of conifers in woodland, plantations and gardens; outside the breeding season, it may also appear in hedges.
