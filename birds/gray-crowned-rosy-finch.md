# How to identify the Gray-crowned Rosy-Finch (*Leucosticte tephrocotis*)

Look for a medium-large, brown finch with a gray cap, black forehead and throat, and pink on the belly and wings. Its long, forked tail is another useful clue. It breeds on high alpine ground across Alaska and western North America, then moves to lower elevations in winter, when it may join other rosy-finches or visit feeders.
