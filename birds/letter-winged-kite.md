# How to identify the Letter-winged Kite (*Elanus scriptus*)

Look for a small, pale grey-and-white kite with vivid red eyes and dark marks at the shoulders. In flight, a bold black M-like pattern across the underside of each wing is its clearest clue. It is active mainly after dark and is most likely to be encountered in Australia’s dry inland, though it is scarce and may wander far from its usual range.
