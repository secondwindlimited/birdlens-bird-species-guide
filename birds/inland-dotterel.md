# How to identify the Inland Dotterel (*Peltohyas australis*)

Look for a plover with sandy, dark-speckled upperparts, a pale rump and a bold black Y across the chest and belly. A dark stripe runs from the crown through each eye. Inland Dotterels live in dry, open country in Australia, often feeding after dark; by day their muted colours make them easy to overlook. They may also be seen beside outback roads.
