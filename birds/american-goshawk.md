# How to identify the American Goshawk (*Astur atricapillus*)

A large, strong-built forest hawk, the American Goshawk has gray upperparts, a pale, finely barred underside, a clear light eyebrow and reddish eyes as an adult. Young birds are brown above and heavily streaked, and can be mistaken for Cooper’s Hawks. Look for it in extensive, older woodland, especially in mountain regions; it is less likely than a Cooper’s Hawk in suburban areas.
