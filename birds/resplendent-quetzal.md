# How to identify the Resplendent Quetzal (*Pharomachrus mocinno*)

A very large trogon of Central American mountain forests, the Resplendent Quetzal is easiest to identify by its vivid green plumage, red underside and, in males, exceptionally long trailing tail plumes. Look for one sitting quietly in a fruiting tree. It occurs from southern Mexico through Central America, and its seasonal movements take it between different elevations.
