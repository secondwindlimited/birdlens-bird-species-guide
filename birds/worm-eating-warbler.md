# How to identify the Worm-eating Warbler (*Helmitheros vermivorum*)

Look for a small, muted olive-brown warbler with pale underparts, a long pointed bill and dark-and-buff lines across its head. Its pinkish legs and dry, high trill also help identify it. It keeps to low cover, where it searches dead leaves for insects. It breeds in the eastern United States and spends the non-breeding season in Central America and the Caribbean.
