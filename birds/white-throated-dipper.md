# How to identify the White-throated Dipper (*Cinclus cinclus*)

Look for a round-bodied, short-tailed bird beside a clear, fast-flowing stream. Its bright white throat and upper chest stand out against a dark head and back, while a chestnut band marks the lower breast in many populations. It often feeds among stones, bobbing on a rock or swimming in a pool; it may also vanish beneath the surface to forage.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | White-throated Dipper |
| Scientific name | *Cinclus cinclus* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Cinclidae |
| Genus | *Cinclus* |
