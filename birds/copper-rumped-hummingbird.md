# How to identify the Copper-rumped Hummingbird (*Saucerottia tobaci*)

Look for a small hummingbird with a bright green head and body, a copper-toned rump and a blue, forked tail. The female is less vivid below, with a brownish belly. It often patrols forest margins, young woodland and gardens, especially in Trinidad and Tobago and nearby northern Venezuela. Its flight may give away its presence with a brief trilling call.

| Common name | Copper-rumped Hummingbird |
