# How to identify the Yellow-shouldered Amazon (*Amazona barbadensis*)

A large, green parrot with a yellow crown and face, this species also has a yellow patch at the wing bend and a red mark on the wing. Look for it in dry scrub and woodland in northern Venezuela and nearby Caribbean islands. It stays in its range year-round, often feeding in pairs or small groups and gathering in larger numbers at night.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Yellow-shouldered Amazon |
| Scientific name | *Amazona barbadensis* |
| Order | Psittaciformes |
| Family | Psittacidae |
| Genus | *Amazona* |
