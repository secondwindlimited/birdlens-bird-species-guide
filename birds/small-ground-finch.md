# How to identify the Small Ground-Finch (*Geospiza fuliginosa*)

Look for a very small finch with a rather slim, pointed bill on most Galápagos islands. Breeding males are nearly black, while females and young birds are brown and streaked below. The bill is dark in breeding males but orange-toned in females and males outside the breeding season. It is common in dry coastal country and also uses higher ground at some times of year.
