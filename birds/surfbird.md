# How to identify the Surfbird (*Calidris virgata*)

Surfbirds are sturdy, short-legged sandpipers with a blunt bill, orange at its base, and muted yellow legs. Their gray upperparts contrast with a pale belly; in breeding plumage, dark marks spread across the underparts and rusty color appears on the back. They are most often found in groups on rocky Pacific shores, close to breaking waves and often among turnstones.
