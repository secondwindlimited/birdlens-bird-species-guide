# How to identify the Northern Giant-Petrel (*Macronectes halli*)

Look for a bulky, dark seabird with a heavy pale bill ending in pink. Northern Giant-Petrels are roughly small-albatross sized and often travel in company with Southern Giant-Petrels. They range widely over southern waters, where they may follow ships, and come ashore to breed on remote subantarctic and nearby islands.

## What kind of bird is it?

| Scientific name | *Macronectes halli* |
