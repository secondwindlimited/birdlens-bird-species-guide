# How to identify the Olive-backed Woodcreeper (*Xiphorhynchus triangularis*)

This medium-sized climber is olive-brown above, with warmer reddish wings and tail, pale spots on its crown and breast, and a buff eyebrow and eye-ring. It moves up trunks and thick branches, usually alone or with a pair, and may join mixed-species flocks. Look for it in Andean forests, from Venezuela south through the mountains to Bolivia.
