# How to identify the Fawn-throated Foliage-gleaner (*Automolus cervinigularis*)

Look for a fairly large, dark brown foliage-gleaner with a warm buff throat, pale facial markings and a rusty tail. It stays in thick cover in humid forest and is often easier to hear than to see. It occurs from southern Mexico through Central America to Panama, usually alone or with a mate, moving among tangled plants and sometimes on the forest floor.
