# How to identify the Bewick's Wren (*Thryomanes bewickii*)

Look for a slim, brown wren with a long, often flicking tail, pale underparts and a bright stripe over each eye. Its bill curves slightly down. It is often hidden in brush, hedges and open woodland, but may sing from an exposed perch. In the western part of its range, it also turns up in gardens, parks and residential areas.
