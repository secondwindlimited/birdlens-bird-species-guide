# How to identify the Phainopepla (*Phainopepla nitens*)

Across the deserts and dry scrub of the southwestern United States and Mexico, watch for a crested, long-tailed bird perched upright in oak or mesquite. Males look black with a blue gloss and show pale wing panels in flight; females and young are gray, with red eyes. It often feeds in small groups, taking berries, especially mistletoe.
