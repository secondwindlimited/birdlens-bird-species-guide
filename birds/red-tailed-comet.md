# How to identify the Red-tailed Comet (*Sappho sparganurus*)

Look for a hummingbird with a very long, bright red tail in the high Andes. Adult males have a gleaming green-and-gold head and a green body; females are smaller, paler below and also long-tailed. Red-tailed Comets live in open, often dry mountain country, where they may feed around flowering cacti and tree tobacco. They remain in their range year-round.
