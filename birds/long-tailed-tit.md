# How to identify the Long-tailed Tit (*Aegithalos caudatus*)

A tiny, round-bodied bird with a very small bill and a tail that looks strikingly long for its size. Its soft plumage combines white, black, grey and pink, though the pattern varies across its range. Long-tailed Tits are usually found moving through trees or shrubs in lively groups, and their thin, high calls often reveal them before they come into view.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Long-tailed Tit |
| Scientific name | *Aegithalos caudatus* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Aegithalidae |
| Genus | *Aegithalos* |
