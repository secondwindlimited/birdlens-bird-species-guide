# How to identify the Black-cowled Oriole (*Icterus prosthemelas*)

Look for a medium-sized oriole with a slim, slightly hooked black bill and bright yellow on the wings, rump and underside against dark plumage. In the north, adult males are mostly black, while females have olive-yellow upperparts. The species lives in humid lowlands from southern Mexico to western Panama, often around forest margins, plantations and flowering trees.
