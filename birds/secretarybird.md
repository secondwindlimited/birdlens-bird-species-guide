# How to identify the Secretarybird (*Sagittarius serpentarius*)

The Secretarybird is a very tall, ground-hunting raptor with long bare legs, a grey body, black crest feathers and red-orange skin around the eyes. In flight, look for dark panels along the rear of the wings and a long, banded tail. It lives across sub-Saharan Africa, usually in open grassland and savanna, where it walks in search of prey.
