# How to identify the Vegetarian Finch (*Platyspiza crassirostris*)

A large Galápagos finch with a deep, rounded bill that gives it a parrot-like profile. Males show a dark head and throat above a yellowish, streaked belly; females are brown and paler below. Look for it in forest, especially around the middle elevations of larger islands. The smaller-billed Large Tree-Finch is the species it most closely resembles.
