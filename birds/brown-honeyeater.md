# How to identify the Brown Honeyeater (*Lichmera indistincta*)

A small, grey-brown honeyeater with a slim, downcurved black bill and muted yellow patches in the wings and tail. Look for a dark mark beside the eye, sometimes with a brighter yellow spot behind it. Brown Honeyeaters are often heard chattering in lively groups. They occur across much of Australia, in settings from mangroves and woodland to gardens and dry inland scrub.
