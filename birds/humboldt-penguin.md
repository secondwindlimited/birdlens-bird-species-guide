# How to identify the Humboldt Penguin (*Spheniscus humboldti*)

The Humboldt Penguin is a medium-sized, black-and-white penguin of the Pacific shores of Peru and Chile. Adults show a fleshy pink area where the bill meets the face and one dark band across the chest. It nests in coastal colonies, often in burrows, and is usually the only penguin in much of its range. In central Chile, it may occur with Magellanic Penguins.
