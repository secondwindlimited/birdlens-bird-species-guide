# How to identify the Red-backed Fairywren (*Malurus melanocephalus*)

Recognise this small fairywren by its long, usually raised tail. In breeding plumage, the male is strikingly dark with a red back and rump; females and nonbreeding males are brownish and much less conspicuous. The species lives in grassy, often damp places across northern Australia and down the eastern coast, where it is resident but may move locally.
