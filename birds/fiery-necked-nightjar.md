# How to identify the Fiery-necked Nightjar (*Caprimulgus pectoralis*)

A medium-sized nightjar, about 23–25 cm long, with mottled brown and grey plumage and a bold, tawny-rufous band at the back of the neck. Males show white marks at the tail corners; females have buff ones. Listen at dusk or after dark for a repeated, whistled song. It occurs in woodland, savannah and other wooded habitats across much of Africa.
