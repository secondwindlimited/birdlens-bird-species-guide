# How to identify the Southern White-fringed Antwren (*Formicivora grisea*)

This small, long-tailed antwren is easiest to recognise by its contrasting plumage: males are dark underneath with a fine white line along the head and sides, while females have warm cinnamon underparts. Both sexes show conspicuous pale markings on the wings and tail. It inhabits wooded country from northern South America into Brazil, but often keeps low and partly hidden in thick vegetation.
