# How to identify the Marsh Tit (*Poecile palustris*)

The Marsh Tit is a small, plain-looking tit with grey-brown wings and back, pale underparts, and a black cap and bib. Its head markings are crisp, but it can be hard to separate from the Willow Tit; voice is often the best clue in western Europe. Look for it in broadleaved woodland, older orchards and gardens, where it may visit feeders.
