# How to identify the Lined Antshrike (*Thamnophilus tenuepunctatus*)

A stout-billed, crested antshrike with striking black-and-white patterning. Males are barred throughout, while females combine a rusty chestnut back and crown with barred underparts. Listen for a nasal series of notes that bobs in pitch before ending higher. It lives in thick growth along forest margins in the Andean foothills of Colombia, Ecuador and Peru.
