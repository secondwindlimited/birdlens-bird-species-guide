# How to identify the Yellow-billed Chough (*Pyrrhocorax graculus*)

A glossy black bird with a compact yellow bill and red legs is the mountain chough to look for above the tree line. It often travels in noisy groups and may approach people at ski areas, villages, or mountain monasteries for scraps. Its quick, flowing flight and high, squeaky calls help separate it from other dark corvids nearby.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Yellow-billed Chough |
| Scientific name | *Pyrrhocorax graculus* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Corvidae |
| Genus | *Pyrrhocorax* |
