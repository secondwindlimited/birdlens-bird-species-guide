# How to identify the Cooper's Hawk (*Astur cooperii*)

A medium-sized hawk with a broad-looking head, rounded wings and a long, banded tail. Adults are gray above and warm orange-barred below; young birds are brown and streaked. It can be hard to separate from a Sharp-shinned Hawk, but Cooper’s Hawk is larger and flies with slower, deeper wingbeats. Look for it in wooded places and suburban neighborhoods, where it may hunt near bird feeders.
