# How to identify the Indian Peafowl (*Pavo cristatus*)

An Indian peafowl is hard to mistake: the adult male has a vivid blue body and a long, spotted display train, which he raises into a broad fan when courting. Females are smaller, mostly brown, with a green sheen on the neck. Look for this ground-dwelling bird in woodland and farmland in its native South Asian range, including India and Sri Lanka. It often feeds close to cover and may tolerate people at close range.
