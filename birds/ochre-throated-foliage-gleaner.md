# How to identify the Ochre-throated Foliage-gleaner (*Automolus ochrolaemus*)

The Ochre-throated Foliage-gleaner is a stocky, 18–20 cm brown bird with a sturdy bill, warm buff throat, pale buff marks around and behind the eye, and a rusty tail. It stays in thick growth in humid forests, creeping through vines or leaf litter and often remaining out of sight. Its quickening, nasal song can give away its presence.
