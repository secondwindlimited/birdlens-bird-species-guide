# How to identify the Northern Hawk Owl (*Surnia ulula*)

Look for the Northern Hawk Owl high on a spruce crown or dead tree, where it scans for prey. Its unusually long tail, pale face and yellow eyes stand out. In flight it has the quick, direct wingbeats of a small hawk. It inhabits northern coniferous forests and may appear far south in winter when food becomes scarce.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Northern Hawk Owl |
| Scientific name | *Surnia ulula* |
| Order | Strigiformes |
| Family | Strigidae |
| Genus | *Surnia* |
