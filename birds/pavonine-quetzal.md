# How to identify the Pavonine Quetzal (*Pharomachrus pavoninus*)

Look for a sturdy, green-and-red quetzal with a red, yellow-tipped bill and long green feathers draping over the tail. It lacks the conspicuous eye-ring seen on many trogons. Pavonine Quetzals are most likely encountered in the lower and middle levels of Amazonian rainforest, especially mature terra firme forest and places near water. They stay in the region throughout the year.
