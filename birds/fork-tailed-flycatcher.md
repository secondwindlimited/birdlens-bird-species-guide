# How to identify the Fork-tailed Flycatcher (*Tyrannus savana*)

Large, pale-bodied and black-capped, the Fork-tailed Flycatcher is easiest to identify by the adult’s exceptionally long, deeply forked tail. It sits in view on wires, fences or low shrubs in open country, then flies off with a swift, direct flight and its tail streaming behind. Look for it in grasslands, savannas and other open places; some birds wander far beyond their usual range during migration.
