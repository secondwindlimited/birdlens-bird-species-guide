# How to identify the Spotted Bowerbird (*Chlamydera maculata*)

A slim, mottled brown bowerbird with amber spots across its dark back and a small lilac-pink crest at the nape. Its harsh, rasping calls can carry through dry woodland. It is found in inland Queensland and New South Wales, and may also turn up in local parks, gardens and orchards.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Spotted Bowerbird |
| Scientific name | *Chlamydera maculata* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Ptilonorhynchidae |
| Genus | *Chlamydera* |
