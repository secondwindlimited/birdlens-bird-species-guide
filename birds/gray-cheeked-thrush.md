# How to identify the Gray-cheeked Thrush (*Catharus minimus*)

Look for a modest-sized, gray-brown thrush with a plain gray face, a barely visible pale eye ring and dark flanks. Its small bill has yellow at the base. In summer, it sings in northern conifer woods. During migration, it is more likely to be concealed among thick shrubs or low forest growth, where it can be hard to spot.
