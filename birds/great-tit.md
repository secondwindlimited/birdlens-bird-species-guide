# How to identify the Great Tit (*Parus major*)

A fairly large tit with bright yellow underparts, white cheeks, and a black cap and bib. The male usually shows a broad black stripe down the belly; the female’s is narrower. Great Tits are common in woods, parks, gardens, and farmland hedges, and often come to feeders. Listen for a clear, repeated rising-and-falling song and sharp chattering calls.
