# How to identify the Algerian Nuthatch (*Sitta ledanti*)

Look for a compact, blue-gray nuthatch with a crisp white stripe above the eye and warm buff-pink underparts. Males have a black forehead and eye-line; females are grayer in those areas. The species occurs only in scattered forest sites in northeastern Algeria, especially mature oak and mixed woodland, so encounters are confined to a very small part of the country.
