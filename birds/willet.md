# How to identify the Willet (*Tringa semipalmata*)

Look for a large, solidly built gray shorebird with a straight, thick bill and gray legs. Its strongest clue is in flight: dark wings crossed by a wide white stripe, with a pale rump. Willets breed in North American coastal marshes and inland prairie wetlands, then move to shorelines farther south. On migration and in winter, they may gather on mudflats or beaches.
