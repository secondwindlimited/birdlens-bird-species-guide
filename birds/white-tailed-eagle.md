# How to identify the White-tailed Eagle (*Haliaeetus albicilla*)

The White-tailed Eagle is a very large, dark raptor with wide wings and a short tail. Adults are easiest to identify by their white tail, pale brown head and yellow bill. Young birds have brown tails marked with white and darker bills. Look for it soaring over northern coasts and large rivers, where it often searches for fish.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | White-tailed Eagle |
| Scientific name | *Haliaeetus albicilla* |
