# How to identify the Chukar (*Alectoris chukar*)

A compact, sandy-grey partridge with a sharply edged black band across its face and throat, a pale throat, and red bill and legs. It usually moves about in small groups on stony ground among thin grass and low scrub. In its native Asian and Middle Eastern range it lives on dry hills; introduced birds are established in parts of western North America, Hawaii and New Zealand.
