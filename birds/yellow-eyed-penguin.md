# How to identify the Yellow-eyed Penguin (*Megadyptes antipodes*)

Look for a large, upright penguin with a pale yellow stripe sweeping behind its head and around each yellow eye. Adults have a slate-coloured back and white underparts; young birds lack the stripe and have duller eyes. In New Zealand, it is most often seen near breeding colonies on the South Island’s southeast coast and nearby islands, or alone offshore.
