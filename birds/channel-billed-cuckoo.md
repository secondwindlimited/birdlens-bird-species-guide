# How to identify the Channel-billed Cuckoo (*Scythrops novaehollandiae*)

The Channel-billed Cuckoo is a very large, pale gray cuckoo with a red bare ring around the eye and an oversized, curved bill that is gray at the base and yellowish near the tip. In flight, its long wings form a cross-like outline. In Australia it is most often encountered in the north and east during the austral summer, calling at dawn and using fruiting fig trees.
