# How to identify the Yellow-throated Miner (*Manorina flavigula*)

Look for a grey, medium-sized honeyeater with a light crown, yellow bill and eye skin, and a dark cheek mark. Its pale rump is easiest to see when it flies away; yellow at the throat may be faint. It is a familiar bird of inland Australia, including town parks, where noisy groups defend their territory.
