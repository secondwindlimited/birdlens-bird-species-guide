# How to identify the Black Crowned-Crane (*Balearica pavonina*)

Look for a dark, long-legged crane with a stiff golden crown, a pink-and-white patch on each cheek and pale areas on the wings. In flight it looks hunched, and the light wing markings contrast strongly with its dark body. It lives year-round in African savanna country and may be seen on wet ground or in open, dry fields, often with a mate or in a group.
