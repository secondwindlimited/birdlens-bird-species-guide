# How to identify the Wood Lark (*Lullula arborea*)

Look for a compact, streaked brown lark with a small crown tuft and pale underparts. A light stripe above each eye curves around the back of the head. The black-and-white patch at the wing bend is distinctive but easy to miss. Wood Larks occur on open heath, rough old fields and woodland clearings; their flowing song, which speeds up as it goes, may give them away first.
