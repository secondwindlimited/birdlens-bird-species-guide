# How to identify the Black Honeyeater (*Sugomel nigrum*)

Look for a very small honeyeater with a narrow, curved bill and a quick, restless way of feeding. Adult males are strikingly black with white below; females are brownish and mottled. It occurs in Australia’s dry interior, often around flowering emu bushes, and may appear far from other suitable patches as it moves with seasonal blooms. Its population is reported to be declining.
