# How to identify the Painted Bunting (*Passerina ciris*)

The male Painted Bunting is unmistakable when seen well: its blue head, red body and green back form a vivid mix of colors. Females and young birds are green and less conspicuous. Look for the species in brushy places, woodland margins and gardens with dense shrubs; even a singing male may remain partly hidden in foliage.
