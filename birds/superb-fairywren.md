# How to identify the Superb Fairywren (*Malurus cyaneus*)

A small, long-tailed bird, it often holds its tail upright. Breeding males show a bright blue head and back, dark facial markings, and a blue tail. Females and males outside breeding plumage are much duller, though males retain blue tails. Look for it in parks, gardens, and open, shrubby woodland across much of southeastern Australia; it remains there throughout the year.
