# How to identify the Black-footed Albatross (*Phoebastria nigripes*)

A large, dark albatross with long, narrow wings and small pale marks around the face. Adults can show a lighter belly, while young birds tend to look darker overall. It spends most of its life over the North Pacific and is rarely seen from shore. Look for it alone or among seabirds gathering to feed.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Black-footed Albatross |
| Scientific name | *Phoebastria nigripes* |
| Order | Procellariiformes |
| Family | Diomedeidae |
| Genus | *Phoebastria* |
