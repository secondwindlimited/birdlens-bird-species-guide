# How to identify the Maccoa Duck (*Oxyura maccoa*)

Look for a compact duck that sits low on the water, with a broad bill and a long tail often held upright. In breeding plumage, the male is especially easy to pick out: his head is black, his body is chestnut, and his bill is bright blue. Maccoa Ducks occur on wetlands across southern and eastern Africa and may dive when disturbed.
