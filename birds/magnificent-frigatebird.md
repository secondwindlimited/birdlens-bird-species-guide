# How to identify the Magnificent Frigatebird (*Fregata magnificens*)

A very large, dark seabird with long, pointed wings and a narrow tail split into two long tips. Adult males show a red throat pouch at breeding sites; females have a white breast, and young birds have white on the head and underside. Look for it soaring over warm tropical seas and coastlines, sometimes chasing other seabirds for food.
