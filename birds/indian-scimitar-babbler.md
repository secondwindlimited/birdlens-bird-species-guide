# How to identify the Indian Scimitar-Babbler (*Pomatorhinus horsfieldii*)

Look for a bold black-and-white face and a long, curved yellow bill. Indian Scimitar-Babblers often stay partly hidden as they move among low branches and leaf litter, alone in pairs or small groups. Their bubbling calls, sometimes answered by a partner, can give them away before they are seen. They live in peninsular India, especially in wooded hills and foothills.
