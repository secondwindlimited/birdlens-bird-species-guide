# How to identify the Madeira Firecrest (*Regulus madeirensis*)

Look for a tiny, active olive-green bird with a boldly marked black-and-white face, paired white wing bars and bronze-gold shoulder patches. The male has an orange stripe on its crown; the female’s is yellow. Madeira Firecrests occur on Madeira, especially in upland woodland, where they often forage among the upper branches and give thin, high-pitched notes.
