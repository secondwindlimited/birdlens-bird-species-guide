# How to identify the Plain-mantled Tit-Spinetail (*Leptasthenura aegithaloides*)

A small, slim furnariid with a finely marked face, subdued brown back and unusually long, pointed tail is the Plain-mantled Tit-Spinetail. It moves actively through shrubs and trees, often with a partner, and may hang head-down while searching branch tips. It occurs widely in southern South America, in dry scrub, Patagonian steppe, Andean uplands, parks and gardens.
