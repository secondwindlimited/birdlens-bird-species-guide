# How to identify the Gray Catbird (*Dumetella carolinensis*)

The Gray Catbird is a medium-sized, slate-gray songbird with a dark crown and rusty feathers beneath its tail. It is more often heard than seen: its varied, rough-edged song may include borrowed sounds, and the bird often sings from cover. Look for it in dense shrubs, woodland margins, overgrown fields, and thickets near water across much of eastern North America.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Gray Catbird |
| Scientific name | *Dumetella carolinensis* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Mimidae |
| Genus | *Dumetella* |
