# How to identify the Red-billed Chough (*Pyrrhocorax pyrrhocorax*)

A large, dark member of the crow family, the Red-billed Chough is recognised by its long, curved scarlet bill and matching legs. It often feeds on short grass near coastal cliffs or in mountain country, alone, in pairs or in flocks. In the air, it wheels and dives on rising currents, showing broad wings with clearly separated tips.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Red-billed Chough |
| Scientific name | *Pyrrhocorax pyrrhocorax* |

| Genus | *Pyrrhocorax* |
