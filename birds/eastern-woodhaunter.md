# How to identify the Eastern Woodhaunter (*Automolus subulatus*)

A shy, fairly large brown ovenbird of western Amazonia, usually found inside forest at low or middle heights. Its soft, buffy head and breast streaks, sturdy straight bill and lack of a clear pale eyebrow help separate it from similar foliage-gleaners. It hops through tangled plants rather than climbing trunks, and its call combines nasal whistles with a sharp, rapid trill.
