# How to identify the Whinchat (*Saxicola rubetra*)

Look for a small, slim chat with a pale stripe above the eye, a warm orange breast in breeding males and pale marks at the tail’s outer base. Whinchats use open heaths, moors and rough grassland, where they often sit prominently on a bush or fence. They are summer visitors to Europe and western Asia, then migrate to Africa.
