# How to identify the Great Gray Shrike (*Lanius excubitor*)

Look for a thrush-sized bird with a cool grey back, pale underparts and a bold black stripe through the eye. White marks on the dark wings are especially clear in flight. Great Gray Shrikes often watch from an exposed branch, post or wire in open country, scanning for small animals and birds.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Great Gray Shrike |
| Scientific name | *Lanius excubitor* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Laniidae |
| Genus | *Lanius* |
