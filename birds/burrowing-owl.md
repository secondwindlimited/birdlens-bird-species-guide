# How to identify the Burrowing Owl (*Athene cunicularia*)

A small, brown owl with long grayish legs, bright yellow eyes and pale spots is often seen standing on the ground or on a low post. It lives in open grassland and other open country, where it nests in underground burrows. It is active by day as well as after dark, so it may be easier to spot than many owls.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Burrowing Owl |
| Scientific name | *Athene cunicularia* |
| Order | Strigiformes |
| Family | Strigidae |
| Genus | *Athene* |
