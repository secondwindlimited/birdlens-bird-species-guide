# How to identify the Rufous-eared Warbler (*Malcorus pectoralis*)

Rufous ear patches, a narrow dark throat stripe and an unusually long, often upright tail make this warbler distinctive. It is a small, pale bird, with streaked brown upperparts and whitish underparts. Watch for it in dry, open country in southern Africa, especially among low shrubs on broad plains. Its piercing, repeated whistle may reveal it before it comes into view.
