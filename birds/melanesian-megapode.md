# How to identify the Melanesian Megapode (*Megapodius eremita*)

A stout, dark bird with a small head, oversized feet and almost no visible tail. Look for a short pale crest, red bare skin on the forehead and a yellow bill tip. It occurs on islands in the Bismarck Archipelago and Solomon Islands, sometimes approaching settlements. Its loud, wavering calls are often heard in the morning and evening.

## What kind of bird is it?

| Common name | Melanesian Megapode |
| Scientific name | *Megapodius eremita* |
