# How to identify the Rufous-cheeked Nightjar (*Caprimulgus rufigena*)

The Rufous-cheeked Nightjar is a medium-sized, brown nightjar with a subdued rusty collar and pale flashes on the wings and tail. Males show white marks; females have buff ones. Listen after dark for its long, rapid, dry trill. It breeds in dry southern African savanna and scrub, then moves north for the non-breeding season, when it may turn up in varied open country.
