# How to identify the Red-naped Ibis (*Pseudibis papillosa*)

Look for a dark, long-legged ibis with a curved bill, a vivid red, bumpy patch over the rear crown and a pale mark on each shoulder. Its wings may flash blue-green in good light. It is usually encountered in open country and farmland across the Indian Subcontinent, often alone or in small, scattered groups.
