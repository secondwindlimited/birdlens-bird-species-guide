# How to identify the White-throated Piping-Guan (*Pipile grayi*)

Look for a large forest guan with a dark body, bright white head and neck, and a broad white panel on the folded wing. Bare throat skin ranges from pale to blue; mature birds may carry a narrow hanging wattle. It lives in riverine forests across southeastern Peru, Bolivia, southwestern Brazil, and northern Paraguay, where it usually stays near water.
