# How to identify the Blue-throated Mountain-gem (*Lampornis clemenciae*)

The Blue-throated Mountain-gem is a notably large hummingbird with a gray underside, greenish back, brown rump and dark tail marked by white outer corners. Look for a white line above the eye and a smaller pale stripe below it. Males show a vivid blue throat when light catches it. It occurs mainly in Mexican mountain forests and canyons, with a limited reach into the southwestern United States.
