# How to identify the Common Woodshrike (*Tephrodornis pondicerianus*)

A medium-sized, ashy brown woodshrike with a dark cheek patch, a pale brow and white markings at the tail. Its hooked bill and large head give it a distinctive profile. Look for it in dry woodland and scrub across much of the Indian subcontinent. Its quick, ringing song often begins with two clear, sharp notes.
