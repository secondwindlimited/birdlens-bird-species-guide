# How to identify the Blue-capped Manakin (*Lepidothrix coronata*)

The Blue-capped Manakin is a small, stocky bird of shaded rainforest. Adult males are usually black, set off by a vivid blue crown; females are bright green with a yellow underside. Look for it in South American forests on the eastern side of the Andes. It often stays alone, though fruiting trees may draw several birds together.

## What kind of bird is it?
