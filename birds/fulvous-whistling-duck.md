# How to identify the Fulvous Whistling-Duck (*Dendrocygna bicolor*)

An elongated, warm tawny duck with a long neck and legs, the Fulvous Whistling-Duck shows pale stripes along its flanks and a grey-blue bill and feet. In flight its wings lack the conspicuous pale panels seen on some similar whistling-ducks. Look for it in groups on lowland marshes, wet ponds and flooded rice fields; its piping whistles may carry after dark.
