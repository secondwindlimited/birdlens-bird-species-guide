# How to identify the Cerulean Warbler (*Setophaga cerulea*)

A small canopy warbler, the Cerulean is easiest to pick out in spring and summer by its bright, fast-buzzing song. Adult males are blue above and white below, with dark marks along the sides and a narrow breast band. Females are greener and duller, with a pale stripe over the eye. Look for it in mature deciduous woodland in eastern North America.
