# How to identify the Kirtland's Warbler (*Setophaga kirtlandii*)

A large warbler with a blue-grey back, yellow underside, dark flank marks and a broken white ring around the eye. Its repeated up-and-down tail movement is a useful clue. It nests in young jack pine stands, mainly in Michigan and also in nearby areas, then spends the non-breeding season in the Caribbean. It is rarely encountered while travelling between these regions.
