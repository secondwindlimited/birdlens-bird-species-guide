# How to identify the Long-whiskered Owlet (*Xenoglaux loweryi*)

The Long-whiskered Owlet is a minute brown owl with fine facial feathers that project beyond its face, creating a distinctive whiskered look. Check for pale brows and amber eyes as well. It is found only in the eastern Andes of northern Peru, in humid mountain forest and elfin woodland. Its quiet, repeated whistle may be easier to hear than the bird is to see.

## What kind of bird is it?
