# How to identify the Pallas's Leaf Warbler (*Phylloscopus proregulus*)

Pallas's Leaf Warbler is a tiny, rounded-looking bird with a large head, short tail and striking yellow markings. Look for a yellow stripe along the crown, yellow lines above the eyes, two yellow wing bars and a lemon-coloured rump against olive-green upperparts. It is most likely to be encountered in forest and scrub in its Asian wintering area; in Europe, it is an uncommon autumn visitor, sometimes on coastal headlands.
