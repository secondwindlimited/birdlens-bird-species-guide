# How to identify the White Wagtail (*Motacilla alba*)

A slim black, white and grey bird with a long tail that bobs as it walks or runs. White Wagtails use open ground, from wet grassland to lawns and car parks, and may appear alone, in pairs or in small groups. Listen for a sharp, two-part call, especially when a bird takes flight. Plumage varies across its wide range.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|

| Scientific name | *Motacilla alba* |
