# How to identify the Western House-Martin (*Delichon urbicum*)

Look for a compact swallow with dark blue upperparts, a clean white belly and a bright white patch above the tail. Its short tail has only a shallow notch and no long streamers, unlike a barn swallow’s. Western House-Martins are often seen sweeping over fields or water, or gathering around mud nests attached beneath the eaves of buildings.
