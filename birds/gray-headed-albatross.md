# How to identify the Gray-headed Albatross (*Thalassarche chrysostoma*)

An adult Gray-headed Albatross is identified by its slate-grey head, white body and bold black borders on the underwings. Its heavy bill has yellow ridges and a pinkish tip. It lives in the far southern oceans, usually over cold, open water rather than near land. Breeding birds gather at remote subantarctic islands.
