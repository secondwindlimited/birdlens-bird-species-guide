# How to identify the Wreathed Hornbill (*Rhyticeros undulatus*)

A very large, dark hornbill with a pale bill and a striking white tail. Adult males show yellow skin on the throat and face; females have blue throat skin and reddish bare skin around the eye. Look for it in forested highlands across South and Southeast Asia, often travelling in pairs or groups between trees bearing fruit. Its loud, rough calls and deep wingbeats can reveal it in flight.
