# How to identify the Goldenface (*Pachycare flavogriseum*)

Look for a small bird with a yellow face and underside set against a grey head and back. Black edging around the face and neck, plus white marks on the wings, make its pattern distinctive. Goldenfaces live in the forest canopy of New Guinea’s foothills and lower mountains. They stay in their range year-round and may give a sharp, ringing “whip!” call.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Goldenface |
| Scientific name | *Pachycare flavogriseum* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Acanthizidae |
| Genus | *Pachycare* |
