# How to identify the Black-crested Antshrike (*Sakesphorus canadensis*)

A bushy black crest is a useful first clue. Males show a dark head, pale collar and conspicuous white marks on the wings; females are warmer brown, with a rusty crown. Black-crested Antshrikes live in northern South America, where they are often found in pairs in scrub and open woodland, usually among the lower and middle branches.
