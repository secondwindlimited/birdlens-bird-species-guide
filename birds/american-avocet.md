# How to identify the American Avocet (*Recurvirostra americana*)

The American Avocet is easy to identify by its long, slender bill, which curves upward, and its blue-gray legs. In flight, its bold black-and-white wings are especially clear. Breeding adults have a warm orange-buff head and neck, which become gray and white at other times of year. Look for it in groups on shallow wetlands, estuaries and other waters across western and central North America.
