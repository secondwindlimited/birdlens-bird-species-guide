# How to identify the Hawfinch (*Coccothraustes coccothraustes*)

The Hawfinch is a compact, powerful finch with an unusually large, strong bill. Look for its orange-brown head, blue-grey neck and, when it flies, a bright white stripe on the wing and white at the tail tip. It keeps quietly among the high branches of mature woodland, so its sharp, high “tsik!” call may reveal it before the bird does.
