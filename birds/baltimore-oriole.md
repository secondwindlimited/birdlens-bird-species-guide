# How to identify the Baltimore Oriole (*Icterus galbula*)

Look for a medium-sized oriole with a pointed bill and a long tail. Adult males are vivid orange below and on the shoulders, with black across the head and back and pale wing bars. Females are duller, often yellow-orange underneath. In summer, it occurs across eastern North America in leafy open country, parks and gardens, then moves south for winter.
