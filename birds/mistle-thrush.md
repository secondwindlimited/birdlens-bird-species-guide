# How to identify the Mistle Thrush (*Turdus viscivorus*)

The Mistle Thrush is a large, rather upright bird with grey-brown upperparts, a pale underside covered in rounded dark spots, and white at the outer edges of its tail. It is bigger and greyer than a Song Thrush. Look for it on open lawns and grassy verges, in parks, or near trees and hedgerows. Its dry rattling call is a useful clue.

## What kind of bird is it?
