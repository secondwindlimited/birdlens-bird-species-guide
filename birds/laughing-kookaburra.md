# How to identify the Laughing Kookaburra (*Dacelo novaeguineae*)

A very large, sturdy kingfisher with a pale body, dark eye stripe, blue shoulder spots and a rusty, barred tail. Its loud, chuckling laugh is often heard around dawn or dusk. Look for it in eastern Australian forests, parks, towns and campgrounds. In Queensland, check the dark eye and brown facial markings to separate it from the Blue-winged Kookaburra.
