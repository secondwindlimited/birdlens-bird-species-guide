# How to identify the Western Marsh Harrier (*Circus aeruginosus*)

Look for a broad-winged, long-tailed harrier skimming over reed marshes, with its wings held in a slight V. Most birds appear deep brown, often with a pale yellowish head and throat. Adult males can show grey sections across the wings and tail, set against brown and black. The species breeds across Europe and nearby regions, and many birds move south for winter.
