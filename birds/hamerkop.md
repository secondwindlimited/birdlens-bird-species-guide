# How to identify the Hamerkop (*Scopus umbretta*)

Look for a stocky, brown waterbird with a shaggy crest that gives its head a hammer-like outline. It walks briskly beside shallow water, searching for small prey. In flight, its broad wings beat heavily; when it glides, its stretched neck helps distinguish it from birds of prey. It occurs across much of Africa, Madagascar and coastal south-west Arabia.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Hamerkop |
| Scientific name | *Scopus umbretta* |
| Order | Pelecaniformes |
| Family | Scopidae |
| Genus | *Scopus* |
