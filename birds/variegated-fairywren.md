# How to identify the Variegated Fairywren (*Malurus lamberti*)

Look for a small fairywren with an elongated blue tail, usually carried upright. A breeding male has a vivid blue head and back, chestnut shoulders and a dark throat and breast. Females and young birds are mostly grey-brown, with long blue tails; the female’s reddish patch beside the bill is a useful clue. It lives in coastal New South Wales and southeast Queensland, in thick vegetation.
