# How to identify the Black-backed Dwarf-Kingfisher (*Ceyx erithaca*)

Look for a very small, brightly coloured kingfisher with a red bill, violet-red crown, deep blue upperparts and warm orange belly. A black forehead mark and blue-and-white neck patches help confirm it. It usually slips low through shaded forest, often near a stream or pond, but may also appear far from water. Its thin, piercing call can reveal it in flight.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Black-backed Dwarf-Kingfisher |
| Scientific name | *Ceyx erithaca* |
| Order | Coraciiformes |
| Family | Alcedinidae |
| Genus | *Ceyx* |
