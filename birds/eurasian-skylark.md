# How to identify the Eurasian Skylark (*Alauda arvensis*)

Listen for the Eurasian Skylark’s long, bubbling song above open ground. The singer may be little more than a speck as it circles or hangs in the air. On the ground, look for a brown, streaked bird with a short crest and a pale ring around the eye. It is most often found in grassland, farmland and heath.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Eurasian Skylark |
| Scientific name | *Alauda arvensis* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Alaudidae |
| Genus | *Alauda* |
