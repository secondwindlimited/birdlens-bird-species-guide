# How to identify the Harlequin Duck (*Histrionicus histrionicus*)

A small, sharply marked diving duck, the Harlequin is easiest to identify in breeding plumage: males show slate-blue, chestnut and white, while females are dark brown with pale marks on the head. It breeds beside cold, rushing streams and spends the non-breeding season along rocky sea coasts. Look for it in northern North America, Iceland and parts of eastern Asia.
