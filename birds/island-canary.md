# How to identify the Island Canary (*Serinus canaria*)

A small, yellow-green finch with a pale bill and dark, notched tail, the Island Canary is most readily recognised by its melodic, silvery song. Males are brighter below than females, and young birds are brown and streaked. It lives naturally on the Canary Islands, Madeira and the Azores, often in gardens, orchards and open woodland.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Island Canary |
| Scientific name | *Serinus canaria* |

| Genus | *Serinus* |
