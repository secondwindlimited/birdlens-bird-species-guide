# How to identify the Swynnerton's Robin (*Swynnertonia swynnertoni*)

A small forest robin with grey-green upperparts, warm apricot underparts and a dark tail. Look for the white patch across the upper breast, edged in black; it may be concealed when the bird skulks through cover. Swynnerton’s Robin is resident but local, living in forest in parts of Tanzania, Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Its clear, three-part whistle can help reveal it.
