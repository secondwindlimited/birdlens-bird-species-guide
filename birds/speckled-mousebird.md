# How to identify the Speckled Mousebird (*Colius striatus*)

Look for a plump, muted brown-grey mousebird with a dark face, dusky crest and exceptionally long, untidy tail. It clambers through shrubs in groups, using short legs to work along branches. The species ranges widely from west-central Africa to the continent’s south, and may also be seen in gardens, orchards and other settled places.
