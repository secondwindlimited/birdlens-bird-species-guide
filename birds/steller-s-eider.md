# How to identify the Steller's Eider (*Polysticta stelleri*)

Steller’s Eider is a compact sea duck, among the smallest of its kind. In breeding dress, the male has a sharply patterned black-and-white head and body, warm peach underparts and greenish marks by the eye. The brown female is less showy but has a squared head, pale edges on inner wing feathers and white underwings. Look for it on northern tundra in summer or sheltered coastal waters in winter.
