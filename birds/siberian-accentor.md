# How to identify the Siberian Accentor (*Prunella montanella*)

The Siberian Accentor is easiest to pick out by its sharply patterned head: a dark face set off by a broad yellow eyebrow and yellow throat. Chestnut streaks mark its brown back and sides, while two pale wing bars show when the wings are folded. It breeds across northern Russia and Siberia, then moves south for winter in China and Korea. Elsewhere, it occurs as a vagrant.
