# How to identify the Short-tailed Albatross (*Phoebastria albatrus*)

A large albatross of the North Pacific, the Short-tailed Albatross is easiest to identify as an adult by its white body, dark wings, golden-yellow head and neck, and pink bill. Young birds are mostly dark brown. It spends much of its time far offshore, but may occasionally be seen from the coast.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Short-tailed Albatross |
| Scientific name | *Phoebastria albatrus* |
| Order | Procellariiformes |
| Family | Diomedeidae |
| Genus | *Phoebastria* |
