# How to identify the Eastern Shrike-tit (*Falcunculus frontatus*)

Look for a compact olive-and-yellow bird with a stout bill and a striking black-and-white face. Males show a black throat and raised-looking crest; females have an olive-brown throat. It searches eucalypt branches by prising at loose bark, often making a racket. The species lives in eastern and south-eastern Australia, mainly in eucalypt woodland.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|

| Scientific name | *Falcunculus frontatus* |

| Genus | *Falcunculus* |
