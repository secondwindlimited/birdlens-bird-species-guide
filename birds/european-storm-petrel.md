# How to identify the European Storm-Petrel (*Hydrobates pelagicus*)

A very small, dark seabird with a bright white patch across the rump and a white stripe beneath each wing. Its quick, fluttering flight stays low over the sea and can look rather weak. It is usually encountered well offshore, though it breeds on rocky islands around Europe and visits its colonies after dark.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | European Storm-Petrel |
| Scientific name | *Hydrobates pelagicus* |
| Order | Procellariiformes |
| Family | Hydrobatidae |
| Genus | *Hydrobates* |
