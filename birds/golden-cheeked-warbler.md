# How to identify the Golden-cheeked Warbler (*Setophaga chrysoparia*)

The male Golden-cheeked Warbler is marked by a vivid yellow face set against a black throat, crown and back. Look for it in juniper-and-oak woods in central Texas during the breeding season. It spends the rest of the year in mountain forests farther south and is rarely encountered while travelling between its seasonal ranges.
