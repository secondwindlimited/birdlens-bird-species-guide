# How to identify the Hypocolius (*Hypocolius ampelinus*)

Look for a slim, soft-grey bird with a dark band at the end of its long tail; adult males also show a bold black patch across the face. Hypocoliuses often gather in loose groups around fruit-bearing shrubs or trees. They inhabit dry scrub and desert-edge country, and are most likely to be encountered in the Middle East during winter, when many move towards the Arabian coasts.
