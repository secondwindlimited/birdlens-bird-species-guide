# How to identify the Snowy Albatross (*Diomedea exulans*)

A huge albatross with exceptionally long, narrow wings, the Snowy Albatross can glide for long periods over the open Southern Ocean. Its plumage ranges from dark brown in young birds to mostly white in older males. It breeds on remote southern islands and may be seen far out at sea across the southern oceans.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Snowy Albatross |
| Scientific name | *Diomedea exulans* |
| Order | Procellariiformes |
| Family | Diomedeidae |
| Genus | *Diomedea* |
