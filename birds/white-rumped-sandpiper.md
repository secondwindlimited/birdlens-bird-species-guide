# How to identify the White-rumped Sandpiper (*Calidris fuscicollis*)

A small, slim sandpiper with unusually long wings and a pale rump patch that stands out when it flies. In summer, look for warm brown tones on the head and fine marks along the flanks; birds in winter are greyer and less patterned. It breeds on Arctic tundra and spends the non-breeding season around wetlands in southern South America.
