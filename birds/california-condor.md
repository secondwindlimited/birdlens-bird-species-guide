# How to identify the California Condor (*Gymnogyps californianus*)

California Condors are enormous black scavengers, readily picked out by their broad wings, finger-like tips and pale panels underneath. Adults also show bare orange-red heads. Young birds are darker and less sharply marked below. Look for them soaring alone or in pairs over California’s coastal hills and mountains, or around the Grand Canyon, where they remain very scarce.
