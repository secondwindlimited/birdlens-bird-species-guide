# How to identify the Lesser Scaup (*Aythya affinis*)

A medium-sized diving duck with a steeply peaked head, the Lesser Scaup is easiest to pick out when its shape and plumage can be seen together. Males have a dark, shiny head, gray back and pale sides; females are brown with a light patch beside the bill. It breeds across northern North America and winters farther south, often on lakes and sheltered bays.
