# How to identify the Greater Adjutant (*Leptoptilos dubius*)

A very large stork with a bare head, pale eyes, a huge pale bill and a white neck ruff. Its dark wings show a lighter grey area, making it easier to pick out in flight. Look for Greater Adjutants around broad wetlands and grasslands in parts of South and Southeast Asia; they also visit rubbish dumps, sometimes in large groups.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Greater Adjutant |
| Scientific name | *Leptoptilos dubius* |
| Order | Ciconiiformes |
| Family | Ciconiidae |
| Genus | *Leptoptilos* |
