# How to identify the Amur Falcon (*Falco amurensis*)

A small, long-winged falcon, the Amur is easiest to recognise by its compact build and, in males, charcoal-gray plumage with rusty-orange thighs and feathers beneath the tail. Females have pale underparts marked with dark chevrons and vivid reddish-orange bare parts around the eye, bill and feet. It breeds in eastern Asia and passes through India on its way to southern Africa, sometimes gathering in large groups.
