# How to identify the Rufous-naped Lark (*Corypha africana*)

A sturdy, fairly large lark with a short crest, a strong bill and rusty tones. In flight, its reddish wings are a useful clue; the nape is not always rufous, and plumage can be pale or dark. It lives in open country across much of sub-Saharan Africa, where it may be seen singing from a bush, small tree or fence post.
