# How to identify the White-fronted Chat (*Epthianura albifrons*)

A small grey, black and white bird of southern Australia, the White-fronted Chat is easiest to pick out when an adult male shows its bright white face and belly against a bold dark collar. Females are duller, with a grey head and a less distinct breast mark. Look for them in saltmarshes and other places with low, damp growth, often in small groups.
