# How to identify the Aleutian Tern (*Onychoprion aleuticus*)

The Aleutian Tern is a slim seabird with narrow, pointed wings and a deeply notched tail. In summer, adults show a dark crown, bright white forehead and grey body; their bill and legs are black. Listen for soft, rolling whistles rather than the rougher cries of many terns. It breeds on North Pacific shores and spends the non-breeding season in tropical Asian waters.
