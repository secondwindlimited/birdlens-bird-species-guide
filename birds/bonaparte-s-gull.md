# How to identify the Bonaparte's Gull (*Chroicocephalus philadelphia*)

A small, neat gull with pale grey upperparts and a fine bill. In summer, adults show a dark hood and red legs; outside the breeding season, look for a dark spot behind the eye. Its pale front wing edge and dark outer tips are useful clues in flight. It is often encountered in flocks on inland lakes and along coasts during migration and winter.
