# How to identify the Horned Grebe (*Podiceps auritus*)

The Horned Grebe is a compact waterbird with a pointed bill and dark cap. In breeding plumage, golden tufts flare behind its red eye, above a chestnut neck and sides. In winter, look for white cheeks below the cap. It nests on plant-fringed ponds across northern Eurasia and North America, and moves to coasts and larger waters for the colder months.
