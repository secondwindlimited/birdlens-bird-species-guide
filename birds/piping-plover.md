# How to identify the Piping Plover (*Charadrius melodus*)

Look for a small, rounded plover with muted sandy-gray upperparts, orange legs and a short, stout bill. In spring and summer, adults show dark marks on the forehead and breast; outside that season these fade. Piping Plovers are most often encountered on open Atlantic or Great Lakes beaches, usually on dry sand above the waterline.
