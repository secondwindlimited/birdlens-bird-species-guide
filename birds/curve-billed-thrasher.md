# How to identify the Curve-billed Thrasher (*Toxostoma curvirostre*)

Across the desert Southwest and much of Mexico, this resident thrasher is found in cactus scrub, brushy open country, and even leafy neighborhoods. Its gray-brown body, long tail, rounded breast marks, and strongly downcurved dark bill make a useful combination. Listen for a clear, abrupt whistle; the bird may give it from the top of a cactus or shrub.
