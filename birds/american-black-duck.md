# How to identify the American Black Duck (*Anas rubripes*)

Look for a mallard-sized duck with a rich, dark brown body, a pale blue-violet wing panel without white edging, and a yellowish bill in males. Females have a duller green-grey bill. American Black Ducks are found in eastern North America, especially in coastal marshes; winter brings flocks to estuaries and salt marshes.
