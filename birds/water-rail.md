# How to identify the Water Rail (*Rallus aquaticus*)

The Water Rail is a secretive marsh bird, usually detected by its squeals and grunts before it is seen. Look for a long red bill, slate-grey face and breast, and black-and-white bars on the flanks. It slips through reeds and rushes, but may appear briefly along a muddy water’s edge. It occurs in wetlands across Eurasia and parts of North Africa.
