# How to identify the Little Spotted Kiwi (*Apteryx owenii*)

New Zealand’s smallest kiwi is a compact, tailless bird with shaggy, pale gray-brown plumage marked by fine light mottling and a long ivory bill. It is usually detected by sound rather than sight: listen for repeated, rising whistles or close snuffling as it searches for invertebrates. It now occurs in predator-free island sanctuaries and at Zealandia, a mainland sanctuary in Wellington.
