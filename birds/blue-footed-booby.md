# How to identify the Blue-footed Booby (*Sula nebouxii*)

A large Pacific seabird, the Blue-footed Booby is easiest to pick out by its vivid blue feet and brown-and-white head and neck. It is often visible from shore over inshore waters, though usually at a distance. Watch for it gliding high above the sea, then dropping steeply into the water to catch fish. It nests in colonies on islands.
