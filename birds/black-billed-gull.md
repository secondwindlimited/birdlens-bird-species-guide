# How to identify the Black-billed Gull (*Chroicocephalus bulleri*)

Look for a pale, slender gull with a fine black bill, light grey wings and a narrow dark edge at the wingtips. Its legs are usually dark, but can become vivid red as breeding advances. It is found only in New Zealand, most often beside braided rivers in the South Island during the breeding season; many birds later move to coastal areas.
