# How to identify the Mascarene Martin (*Phedina borbonica*)

The Mascarene Martin is a brown swallow with wide wings and bold dark streaks across its pale underside. It is usually easiest to identify in flight, by its strong wingbeats and repeated, rough tearing call. It breeds in Madagascar, Réunion and Mauritius, often near water, though it uses many kinds of habitat. Migrating birds may also be seen on the African mainland.
