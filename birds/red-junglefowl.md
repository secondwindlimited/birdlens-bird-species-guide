# How to identify the Red Junglefowl (*Gallus gallus*)

Red Junglefowl is a compact, wary wild relative of the domestic chicken. Adult males are easy to pick out by their bright orange-gold neck feathers and sweeping dark tail; females are mottled brown. The species occurs across South and Southeast Asia, often in scrubby woodland and near cultivated ground or villages. Listen for a rough crow that ends abruptly, unlike the fuller call of a farmyard rooster.
