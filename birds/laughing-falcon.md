# How to identify the Laughing Falcon (*Herpetotheres cachinnans*)

Look for a pale-headed falcon with a wide dark mask, often sitting on an exposed branch or dead tree in the tropical lowlands of the Americas. It watches for snakes from its perch and is usually seen in wooded or partly open country, rather than deep forest. Its loud, laugh-like calls carry far, and it remains in its range year-round.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Laughing Falcon |
| Scientific name | *Herpetotheres cachinnans* |
| Order | Falconiformes |
| Family | Falconidae |
| Genus | *Herpetotheres* |
