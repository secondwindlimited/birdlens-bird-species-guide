# How to identify the Pheasant-tailed Jacana (*Hydrophasianus chirurgus*)

In breeding plumage, the Pheasant-tailed Jacana has a long, trailing tail, a dark body, white wings and a bright yellow nape. Outside the breeding season, it is shorter-tailed and mostly brown above and pale below. Look for it walking on floating plants in lakes and ponds, where its long toes help it cross the vegetation.
