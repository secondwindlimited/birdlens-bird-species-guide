# How to identify the Atlantic Yellow-nosed Albatross (*Thalassarche chlororhynchos*)

A relatively slim albatross with a gray head, white body, dark upperwings and a fine black bill marked by a yellow-orange line in adults. Look for its narrow wings and dark edging beneath the wings when separating it from similar albatrosses. It breeds on islands in the South Atlantic and spends much of the year over the surrounding ocean.
