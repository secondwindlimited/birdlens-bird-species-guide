# How to identify the Canada Jay (*Perisoreus canadensis*)

Look for a medium-sized gray jay with a pale face, dark cap and nape, and a long tail. Young birds are much darker, with a pale stripe beside the bill. Canada Jays usually occur in twos or small family parties in northern conifer woods and at higher elevations. They may approach campsites or roadside stops in search of food.
