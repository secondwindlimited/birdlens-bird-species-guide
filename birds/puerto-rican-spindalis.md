# How to identify the Puerto Rican Spindalis (*Spindalis portoricensis*)

This small Puerto Rican woodland bird is easiest to recognise by the male’s bright orange chest and black-and-white striped head. Females are much duller, with olive-green plumage and a pale moustache mark. Watch for it feeding deliberately among flowers and fruit, and listen for thin squeaks or brief whistles. It lives across Puerto Rico’s main island, including forests, plantations and gardens.
