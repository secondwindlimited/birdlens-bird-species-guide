# How to identify the Zino's Petrel (*Pterodroma madeira*)

A small, long-winged petrel with a grey back, a dark W-shaped mark across its upper wings and a white belly. It flies with quick, banking turns and can skim high over waves in strong wind. Zino’s Petrel breeds only in Madeira’s central mountains; away from its nesting grounds, it is difficult to identify at sea and is rarely encountered.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Zino's Petrel |
| Scientific name | *Pterodroma madeira* |
| Order | Procellariiformes |
| Family | Procellariidae |
| Genus | *Pterodroma* |
