# How to identify the Waved Albatross (*Phoebastria irrorata*)

Look for a broad-winged, long-necked albatross with a cream-yellow head and an oversized golden bill; its body is mostly brown, with a pale breast. It is the albatross most likely to be seen around the Galápagos. Away from its nesting islands, it is generally encountered well offshore, especially in waters off Ecuador and Peru.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Waved Albatross |
| Scientific name | *Phoebastria irrorata* |
| Order | Procellariiformes |

| Genus | *Phoebastria* |
