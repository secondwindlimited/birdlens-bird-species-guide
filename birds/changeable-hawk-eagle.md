# How to identify the Changeable Hawk-Eagle (*Nisaetus cirrhatus*)

A medium-sized, slender eagle with broad, rounded wings and a head that looks small in flight. Adults occur in pale and dark forms: pale birds have a streaked white underside, while dark birds are mostly sooty brown. Look for it over forests, woodland edges, plantations and large parks across the Indian subcontinent and Southeast Asia. It is generally resident.
