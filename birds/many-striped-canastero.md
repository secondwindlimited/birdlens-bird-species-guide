# How to identify the Many-striped Canastero (*Asthenes flammulata*)

Look for a compact, brown high-Andean bird with conspicuous pale lines on its back, breast and flanks, plus reddish wings and tail. A narrow light eyebrow often shows on its dark face. It lives in shrubby grassland and páramo from northern Colombia to central Peru, where it may stay hidden among plants or sing from an exposed perch.
