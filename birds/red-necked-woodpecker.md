# How to identify the Red-necked Woodpecker (*Campephilus rubricollis*)

Look for a large woodpecker with a pale, chisel-shaped bill, a black back and wings, and vivid red across the head, neck and breast. Females show a pale facial stripe; males have a small light spot near the ear. It lives year-round in Amazonian forests, most often in mature forest, and is also found at forest edges.
