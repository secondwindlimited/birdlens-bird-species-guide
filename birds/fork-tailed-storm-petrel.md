# How to identify the Fork-tailed Storm-Petrel (*Hydrobates furcatus*)

This small seabird is blue-grey overall, with a darker face, dark underwings and a deeply notched tail. It flies low over cool North Pacific waters, beating its wings in short, stiff strokes. Look for it around offshore islands during the breeding season, or farther out at sea; it may appear alone or among other storm-petrels.
