# How to identify the Plains-wanderer (*Pedionomus torquatus*)

The Plains-wanderer is a small, ground-dwelling Australian bird with finely marked brown upperparts and a pale underside. Females stand out for a red throat and a boldly patterned collar. It lives in sparse native grassland in parts of central and southern Australia, but its camouflage and secretive habits make it difficult to find without a focused search.
