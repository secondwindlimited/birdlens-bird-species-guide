# How to identify the Banded Stilt (*Cladorhynchus leucocephalus*)

The Banded Stilt is a large, mostly white shorebird with black wings, very long pink legs and a straight, narrow bill. Adults show a broad chestnut band across the breast; young birds do not. Look for it in groups on shallow salt lakes and coastal lagoons in southern Australia. Flocks can number in the thousands when inland lakes fill after rain.
