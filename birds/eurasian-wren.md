# How to identify the Eurasian Wren (*Troglodytes troglodytes*)

A tiny, rounded brown bird with a stubby tail that often points upwards. It can be hard to spot as it slips through low, tangled cover, but may sing from an open perch. Listen for its strong, rippling song or sharp repeated chips. Look for it in woodland, gardens, hedges and other places with dense low growth.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Eurasian Wren |
| Scientific name | *Troglodytes troglodytes* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Troglodytidae |
| Genus | *Troglodytes* |
