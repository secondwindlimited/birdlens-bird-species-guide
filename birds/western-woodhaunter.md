# How to identify the Western Woodhaunter (*Automolus virgatus*)

A small, brown forest bird with a stout, straight bill and blurred buff marks on its head and chest. It is usually found alone or in pairs, moving through vine and moss tangles in the lower or middle forest. Listen for sharp nasal notes or rough scolding calls. It lives from eastern Nicaragua south to western Ecuador.
