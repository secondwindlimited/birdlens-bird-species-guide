# How to identify the Copper-throated Sunbird (*Leptocoma calcostetha*)

This 12–13 cm sunbird is easiest to spot by the male’s shining wine-red throat and blue-green sheen, which may be hard to see in shade. The female is plainer, with yellow underparts, a pale throat and a fine dark stripe through the eye. Listen for a ringing trill or an inflected “tslee.” It is most often found in coastal mangroves and nearby gardens in Southeast Asia.
