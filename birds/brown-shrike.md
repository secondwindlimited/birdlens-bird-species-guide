# How to identify the Brown Shrike (*Lanius cristatus*)

A medium-sized, brown-backed shrike with a slender black eye-mask, pale underside and reddish-brown rump and tail. Its sturdy-looking bill and lack of white patches on the wings help separate it from some similar shrikes. Brown Shrikes breed across parts of northern Asia and migrate south for winter, where they use open woodland, fields, parks and gardens.
