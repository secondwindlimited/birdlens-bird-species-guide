# How to identify the Paradise Riflebird (*Ptiloris paradiseus*)

The male Paradise Riflebird is mostly black, with bright blue-green shine on its head and underside; the female is brown with a pale, finely patterned belly. Both have long, curved bills. Look for this bird in rainforest in southeast Queensland and northeastern New South Wales, where it usually stays year-round but may shift to nearby drier forest.
