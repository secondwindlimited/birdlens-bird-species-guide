# How to identify the Varied Sittella (*Daphoenositta chrysoptera*)

A tiny, short-tailed tree-climber with a fine, slightly upturned bill and a bright yellow-orange ring around each eye. Its head and body colours vary greatly between regional forms, but a broad pale stripe across the dark wing is a useful clue in flight. Look for it in Australian woodlands, often with companions, creeping over trunks as it searches the bark for insects.
