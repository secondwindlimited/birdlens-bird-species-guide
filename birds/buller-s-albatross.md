# How to identify the Buller's Albatross (*Thalassarche bulleri*)

Look for a medium-sized albatross with a silver-white crown, grey hood, dark back and a broad black strip along the front of the underwing. Adults have a black bill edged in yellow. Buller’s Albatross breeds on New Zealand’s offshore islands; outside the breeding season it ranges widely over the temperate South Pacific, including waters off Australia and western South America.
