# How to identify the Russet Sparrow (*Passer cinnamomeus*)

Look for a compact sparrow with a warm reddish-brown back. Males show a chestnut crown, pale cheeks and a small black throat patch; females are streaked brown above, with a light eyebrow and pale wing marks. It is most often seen around upland farms, pastures and open woodland, usually near villages rather than in large urban centres.
