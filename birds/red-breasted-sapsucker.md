# How to identify the Red-breasted Sapsucker (*Sphyrapicus ruber*)

Identify it by the mostly crimson head and chest, white lower body, dark barred wings, and prominent pale shoulder marks. Red-breasted Sapsuckers live in forests along the Pacific side of western North America. They make lines of small feeding holes in tree trunks. Nasal calls and irregular drumming can help locate one when foliage hides it.
