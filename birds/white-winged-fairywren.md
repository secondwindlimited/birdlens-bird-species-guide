# How to identify the White-winged Fairywren (*Malurus leucopterus*)

A small fairywren with a slim, upward-pointing tail. In breeding plumage, the male is vivid blue with conspicuous white on the wings; females and males out of breeding plumage are muted brownish grey. Look for it in dry, open country with scattered low shrubs, where it may sit on a bush top. Its call is a quick, fine trill.
