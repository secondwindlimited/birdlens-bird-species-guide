# How to identify the Sedge Wren (*Cistothorus stellaris*)

Look for a tiny, buff-and-cinnamon wren moving low through thick grass, often in wet fields and meadows. Its dark-streaked crown, barred wings and faint eyebrow help distinguish it from the Marsh Wren. It may stay hidden until it sings, then rise onto a grass stem. Sedge Wrens breed in central North America and travel south for winter, reaching northeastern Mexico.
