# How to identify the Satin Berrypecker (*Melanocharis citreola*)

At 13–14 cm, the Satin Berrypecker is a small, slim bird with a long tail. Adult males stand out for their blue-black sheen and bright, satin-white underside, often tinged yellow. It is known only from cloud forest in the Kumawa and Fakfak mountains of West Papua, where it stays at middle elevations and may be seen among mixed feeding flocks.
