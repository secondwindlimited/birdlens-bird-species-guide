# How to identify the Northern Red Bishop (*Euplectes franciscanus*)

A tiny, short-tailed weaver of open country, often seen in flocks. Breeding males stand out with a black head and vivid red-orange feathers over the back and around the throat, plus brown wings and tail. Females and males outside the breeding season are much duller, with brown-and-pale plumage. Look for the species in tall grass, farmland and wetland margins across Africa.
