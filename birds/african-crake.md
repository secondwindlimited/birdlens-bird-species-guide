# How to identify the African Crake (*Crecopsis egregia*)

Look for a compact rail with a slate-grey head and breast, a reddish bill, pale legs, a finely barred underside and a dark, scalloped back. It often stays hidden in grass, then flies low with its legs hanging before dropping back into cover. Watch for it in seasonal marshes and flooded grassland across much of sub-Saharan Africa.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | African Crake |
| Scientific name | *Crecopsis egregia* |
