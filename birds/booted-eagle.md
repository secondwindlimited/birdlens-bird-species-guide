# How to identify the Booted Eagle (*Hieraaetus pennatus*)

A small eagle with a long, square-ended tail, fingered wing tips and feathering down the legs. The pale form has a striking light-and-dark pattern; dark birds are less contrasting, but both may show pale patches on the shoulders when seen head-on. Look for it above wooded, broken country with open areas, often in hills. Northern birds travel south for winter.
