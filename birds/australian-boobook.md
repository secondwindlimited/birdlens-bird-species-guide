# How to identify the Australian Boobook (*Ninox boobook*)

A compact brown owl with conspicuous dark markings around its eyes and pale spots on the wings and belly. Its familiar two-part “boo-book” call is often the easiest clue to its presence. It occurs across mainland Australia and also uses wooded farmland and suburbs, so it may be encountered well beyond large forested areas.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Australian Boobook |
| Scientific name | *Ninox boobook* |
| Order | Strigiformes |
| Family | Strigidae |
| Genus | *Ninox* |
