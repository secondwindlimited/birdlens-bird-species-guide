# How to identify the Desert Wheatear (*Oenanthe deserti*)

Desert Wheatear is a small, pale-bodied wheatear with dark wings and a bright white rump set against a black tail. Males show a black face in breeding plumage; females look greyer and less boldly marked. Look for it in dry, open country, where it often sits on rocks, posts or wires. During migration, it may appear well beyond its usual range.

| Genus | *Oenanthe* |
