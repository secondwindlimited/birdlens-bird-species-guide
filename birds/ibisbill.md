# How to identify the Ibisbill (*Ibidorhyncha struthersii*)

The Ibisbill has a long, curved red bill, a dark band across its chest and a distinctive dark mark on the face. Its grey-brown back blends with stones, so it can be hard to pick out beside rushing water. Look for it feeding among the rocks of mountain rivers in Central Asia and the Himalayas; it usually forages alone and may move to lower ground outside the breeding season.
