# How to identify the Markham's Storm-Petrel (*Hydrobates markhami*)

Look for a slim, dark storm-petrel with a deeply forked tail and clear pale-grey markings across the upper wings. It flies over offshore Humboldt Current waters, often using shallow wingbeats and short glides. Markham’s Storm-Petrel is rarely seen from land. Watch for it at sea off Peru and northern Chile; it visits its desert nesting sites after dark.
