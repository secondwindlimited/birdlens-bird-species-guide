# How to identify the Sind Sparrow (*Passer pyrrhonotus*)

The Sind Sparrow is a small, House Sparrow-like bird of South Asian wetlands. Look for the male’s chestnut stripe through the eye, gray head, narrow black throat patch and conspicuous white shoulder mark. Females are plainer, with a grayer face than female House Sparrows. It is usually found in scrub or tall grass close to rivers, canals and other water.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Sind Sparrow |
| Scientific name | *Passer pyrrhonotus* |

| Genus | *Passer* |
