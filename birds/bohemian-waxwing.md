# How to identify the Bohemian Waxwing (*Bombycilla garrulus*)

A plump, grey bird with a pointed crest, a rusty face and a rich reddish undertail. Its black wings carry striking white and yellow marks, and its tail has a yellow tip. Bohemian Waxwings breed in northern forests across Eurasia and North America. Outside the breeding season, watch for flocks gathering in fruiting trees, sometimes far south of their usual winter range.
