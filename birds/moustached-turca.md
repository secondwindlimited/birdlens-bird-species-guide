# How to identify the Moustached Turca (*Pteroptochos megapodius*)

Look for a bulky, cinnamon-brown bird with a conspicuous white stripe along each side of the throat and a tail held high. Its lower belly is barred, while the upper breast looks plain brown. It lives in Chilean scrub on rocky hillsides and may perch on stones or move beside roads. Its loud calls can give away its presence before it comes into view.
