# How to identify the White-breasted Mesite (*Mesitornis variegatus*)

On Madagascar’s forest floor, look for a brown-backed bird with a pale face marked by dark cheek stripes, a white belly and a long tail. White-breasted Mesites usually move in pairs or small family groups, walking rather than flying. Their loud, clear whistles followed by quick chuckles may reveal them before they appear. They are most likely in western dry forest, though they also occur locally in rainforest.

## What kind of bird is it?
