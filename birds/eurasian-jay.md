# How to identify the Eurasian Jay (*Garrulus glandarius*)

Look for a compact, warm-brown crow relative with a bold blue-and-black barred panel on the folded wing. Its black facial stripe, pale crown and white rump are useful clues; the rump flashes in flight. Eurasian Jays live wherever mature trees provide cover, from oak woods to parks and leafy gardens, though they can be shy.
