# How to identify the Common Grasshopper Warbler (*Locustella naevia*)

A small, secretive warbler with a finely streaked back and long, marked undertail feathers. It is usually detected by sound rather than sight: its sustained, dry, trilling song resembles an insect’s rasp and may come from grass or a low shrub. Look for it in damp, overgrown grassland, heath and scrub, especially where cover lies close to water.
