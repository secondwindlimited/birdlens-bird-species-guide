# How to identify the Antarctic Tern (*Sterna vittata*)

Look for a medium-sized, pale grey tern with a deeply forked tail. In summer, adults have a dark crown reaching the bill and vivid red legs and bill; outside that season, the dark area shrinks and the bill looks duller. It breeds on rocky islands and shores around Antarctica and the subantarctic, and is often seen near its nesting places.
