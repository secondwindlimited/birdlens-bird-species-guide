# How to identify the Superb Lyrebird (*Menura novaehollandiae*)

A large, brown, ground-living bird with strong legs and an exceptionally long, patterned tail in adult males. Look for the Superb Lyrebird scratching through fallen leaves in forests of southeastern Australia. Its loud, varied song may include imitations of other birds. Females and young birds have shorter, plainer tails and can be harder to distinguish from other lyrebirds.
