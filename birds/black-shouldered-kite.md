# How to identify the Black-shouldered Kite (*Elanus axillaris*)

Look for a compact, pale grey-and-white kite with vivid red eyes and bold black marks on its shoulders. It often hangs in place above open ground, then drops towards prey. In Australia it is seen over grassland, farmland and grassy spaces near towns, especially across the more fertile southern and south-eastern parts of the mainland.
