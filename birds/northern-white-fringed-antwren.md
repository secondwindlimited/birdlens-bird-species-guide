# How to identify the Northern White-fringed Antwren (*Formicivora intermedia*)

A small, slim antwren with a relatively long tail, found in Colombia and western Venezuela. Males are dark underneath, with a broad white line framing the face and sides; females are pale below but share the white facial mark and spotted wings. Look for pairs moving quietly through dense shrubs and low woodland, and listen for their musical, clucking calls.
