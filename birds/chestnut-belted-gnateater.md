# How to identify the Chestnut-belted Gnateater (*Conopophaga aurita*)

A compact, short-tailed bird with a chestnut breast, the Chestnut-belted Gnateater is easiest to identify by the male’s black face and bright white eye tuft. Females are reddish-faced and lack the tuft. It lives in Amazonian rainforest, where it is usually found perched low in the shaded understory.

| Common name | Chestnut-belted Gnateater |
| Scientific name | *Conopophaga aurita* |

| Genus | *Conopophaga* |
