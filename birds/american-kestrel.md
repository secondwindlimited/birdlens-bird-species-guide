# How to identify the American Kestrel (*Falco sparverius*)

A small, slim falcon about the size of a Mourning Dove, with a large head, pointed wings and a broad, square-ended tail. Males show blue-grey wings and a rusty back; females are more uniformly reddish brown. Look for it on wires beside roads or over open country, where it may hover before dropping to the ground for prey.
