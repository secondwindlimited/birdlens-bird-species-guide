# How to identify the Sumatran Green-Pigeon (*Treron oxyurus*)

Look for a slim, mostly olive-green pigeon with a sharply tapered tail, a blue-green bill and bare greenish-blue skin around the eyes. Males may show a subtle rusty-orange breast tint and yellow underparts. It lives in the wooded uplands of Sumatra and western Java and is often seen feeding in the forest canopy.
