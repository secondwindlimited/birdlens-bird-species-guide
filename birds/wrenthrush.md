# How to identify the Wrenthrush (*Zeledonia coronata*)

The Wrenthrush is a compact forest bird best recognized by its vivid orange-yellow crown against a gray face and body. It stays low in wet mountain woods, where dense undergrowth and bamboo offer cover. Watch for it near the ground in Costa Rica and western Panama, especially in the misty upper forests.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|

| Scientific name | *Zeledonia coronata* |
