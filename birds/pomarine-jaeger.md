# How to identify the Pomarine Jaeger (*Stercorarius pomarinus*)

A heavy, broad-winged jaeger, the Pomarine is the largest of the jaegers. Breeding adults have two long, spoon-ended feathers at the centre of the tail. Look for a pale patch across the wings, especially on the underside, and a thick bill. It nests on Arctic tundra and spends the rest of the year over seas, often along coasts in warmer regions.
