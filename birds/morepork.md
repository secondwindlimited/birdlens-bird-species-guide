# How to identify the Morepork (*Ninox novaeseelandiae*)

A small, brown owl with bright yellow eyes, the Morepork is usually detected by sound rather than sight. Its clear, two-part “more-pork” call carries through the night in forests across New Zealand. By day it hides in thick foliage, often in tree ferns, where the attention of mobbing small birds may reveal its roost.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Morepork |
| Scientific name | *Ninox novaeseelandiae* |
| Order | Strigiformes |
| Family | Strigidae |
| Genus | *Ninox* |
