# How to identify the Yellow-billed Stork (*Mycteria ibis*)

A large white stork with a heavy yellow bill and bare red skin across the face. Its short, dark tail can show a green or purple sheen. Watch for it in shallow wetlands, where it wades with its bill in the water and sweeps it through muddy pools for prey. It occurs widely across Africa, especially in the east.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Yellow-billed Stork |
| Scientific name | *Mycteria ibis* |
| Order | Ciconiiformes |
| Family | Ciconiidae |
| Genus | *Mycteria* |
