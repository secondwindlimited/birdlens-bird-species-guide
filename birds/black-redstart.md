# How to identify the Black Redstart (*Phoenicurus ochruros*)

The Black Redstart is a small, upright-perching bird with a dark body and a rusty orange tail that often flicks or flashes in flight. Males are usually darker than females, though both sexes show the bright tail. Look for it in open, stony places and around buildings or industrial sites; in many areas it is most often seen during migration or in winter.
