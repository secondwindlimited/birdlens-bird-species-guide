# How to identify the Chimney Swift (*Chaetura pelagica*)

The Chimney Swift is a dark, narrow-bodied bird with long curved wings and a short, blunt-ended tail. Its fast, erratic flight and constant turning are distinctive. It spends much of the day on the wing, often in small, twittering groups above towns as it catches insects. In North America, it breeds across the central and eastern United States and southern Canada, then travels to South America for winter.
