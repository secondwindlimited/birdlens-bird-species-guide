# How to identify the Banded Broadbill (*Eurylaimus javanicus*)

A large, wine-red broadbill with a wide turquoise bill and vivid yellow markings on its wings and tail is usually easy to pick out. It feeds among the upper branches, often sallying out from a perch. Look for it in damp woodland, particularly near water, across parts of mainland Southeast Asia and the Greater Sunda islands. It is generally present throughout the year.
