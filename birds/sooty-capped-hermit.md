# How to identify the Sooty-capped Hermit (*Phaethornis augusti*)

Look for a fairly large, gray-brown hermit with a rusty rump, pale gray underside, and a bold dark-and-white face. Its long tail shows broad white ends, especially when it flies away. It is found in dry woodland and at savanna margins in northern South America, moving quickly from one separated nectar source to another.
