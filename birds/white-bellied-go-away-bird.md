# How to identify the White-bellied Go-away-bird (*Crinifer leucogaster*)

A tall crest, long tapered tail and clean white underside make the White-bellied Go-away-bird distinctive. In flight, look for white markings on the tail and a pale patch beneath the wing. It lives in dry savanna and open woodland across the Horn of Africa and nearby regions, often moving between trees in small, loose groups.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | White-bellied Go-away-bird |
| Scientific name | *Crinifer leucogaster* |
| Order | Musophagiformes |
| Family | Musophagidae |
| Genus | *Crinifer* |
