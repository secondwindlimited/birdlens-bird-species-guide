# How to identify the Scarlet Macaw (*Ara macao*)

The Scarlet Macaw is a very large parrot with a scarlet body, broad yellow panels on the wings, blue outer feathers and an exceptionally long tail. Look for it in pairs in lowland forest and nearby open country with mature trees. Across much of its range it is now scarce or absent, though protected sites are supporting reintroductions.

## What kind of bird is it?
