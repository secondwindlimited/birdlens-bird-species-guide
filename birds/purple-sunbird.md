# How to identify the Purple Sunbird (*Cinnyris asiaticus*)

Under 10 cm long, the Purple Sunbird has a short, curved bill and a compact shape. Breeding males shine blue-purple, while females are olive above with yellow underparts; males outside breeding season look much duller. Watch for it taking nectar in gardens, cultivated places and woodland, often in pairs. Its wing-fluttering display and song may draw attention.
