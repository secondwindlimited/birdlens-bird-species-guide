# How to identify the Blue-capped Ifrita (*Ifrita kowaldi*)

Look for a compact, brown bird with a bright blue crown, dark crown markings, a pale belly and a long white line behind the eye. Its deliberate, watchful movements as it searches for insects are useful clues in forest. The Blue-capped Ifrita lives only in New Guinea’s mountain forests, usually at higher elevations, where its clear “toowa-tee!” song may be followed by a dry “wik.”
