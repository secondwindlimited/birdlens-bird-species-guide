# How to identify the Common Pochard (*Aythya ferina*)

The Common Pochard is a compact diving duck with a sloping forehead and a dark bill marked by a pale band. Adult males are easiest to pick out: their rusty heads sit above black chests and light grey bodies. Look for the species on lakes and reservoirs across its breeding range, or in southern and western Europe during winter.
