# How to identify the Common Murre (*Uria aalge*)

The Common Murre is a compact black-and-white seabird with a dark upper body, white belly and pointed bill. In breeding season its head is dark; in winter, the face turns white with a dark mark near the eye. Look for it on northern sea cliffs in summer and offshore waters in winter, often alone or in small groups.
