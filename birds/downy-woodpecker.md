# How to identify the Downy Woodpecker (*Dryobates pubescens*)

Look for a tiny black-and-white woodpecker with a stubby bill and dark marks across the white edges of its tail. It lives in wooded parts of much of North America and often visits garden feeders, where it takes suet. Its soft, sharp notes and quick rattling call can help separate it from the larger Hairy Woodpecker.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Downy Woodpecker |
| Scientific name | *Dryobates pubescens* |
| Order | Piciformes |
| Family | Picidae |
| Genus | *Dryobates* |
