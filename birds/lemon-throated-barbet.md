# How to identify the Lemon-throated Barbet (*Eubucco richardsoni*)

Look for a small, brightly coloured bird high in the canopy of lowland rainforest in the western Amazon. Males show a red head, green back and yellow throat, with orange on the breast; females are more subdued. The bird often joins mixed feeding flocks and gives a low, rolling song. It is resident, so it may be encountered throughout the year.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Lemon-throated Barbet |
| Scientific name | *Eubucco richardsoni* |
| Order | Piciformes |

| Genus | *Eubucco* |
