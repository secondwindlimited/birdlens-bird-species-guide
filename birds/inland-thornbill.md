# How to identify the Inland Thornbill (*Acanthiza apicalis*)

This small, slim thornbill has grey-brown upperparts, a pale streaked breast and a reddish rump. Look for white flecks on its forehead and its habit of holding the tail upright. It lives across southern and inland parts of mainland Australia, usually in dry woodland or scrub, though it also uses wetter places in the southwest.
