# How to identify the Crested Owl (*Lophostrix cristata*)

Look for a large forest owl with striking, elongated pale ear tufts that continue a light stripe above each eye. The tufts may lie flat on a resting bird, then stand upright when it becomes alert. Crested Owls live in tropical forests of Central and South America. They may roost in pairs on low perches, while at night they call from the dense canopy.
