# How to identify the Toco Toucan (*Ramphastos toco*)

A huge orange bill, marked with black at its base and near its tip, makes the Toco Toucan easy to pick out. The rest of the bird is mostly black, with a white throat and red beneath the tail. Look for it in South American savannas, open woodland and wooded strips beside rivers, rather than deep, unbroken rainforest.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Toco Toucan |
| Scientific name | *Ramphastos toco* |

| Genus | *Ramphastos* |
