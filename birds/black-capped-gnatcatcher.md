# How to identify the Black-capped Gnatcatcher (*Polioptila nigriceps*)

A small, slim gnatcatcher with a long dark tail and pale outer tail feathers, the Black-capped Gnatcatcher is easiest to recognise when a breeding male displays its glossy black crown. It inhabits dry scrub and woodland in northwestern Mexico. Look for it alone, with a mate, or among its young, searching through vegetation at low or middle levels.
