# How to identify the Bald Eagle (*Haliaeetus leucocephalus*)

A very large bird of prey of North American lakes, rivers and coasts. Adults are unmistakable: a dark brown body and wings with a pure white head and tail, and a heavy yellow bill. Younger birds are mottled brown and white and take about five years to gain adult plumage. Bald Eagles soar on flat, plank-like wings.

|---|---|
