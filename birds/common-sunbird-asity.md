# How to identify the Common Sunbird-Asity (*Neodrepanis coruscans*)

A tiny, round-bodied bird with a short tail and long, curved bill, the Common Sunbird-Asity is easiest to recognise when a breeding male flashes blue above and yellow below, with vivid bare skin around the eye. Females and non-breeding males are much duller olive-green. Look for it darting among flowers high in Madagascar’s rainforest canopy.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Common Sunbird-Asity |
| Scientific name | *Neodrepanis coruscans* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Philepittidae |
| Genus | *Neodrepanis* |
