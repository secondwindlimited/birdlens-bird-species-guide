# How to identify the Painted Honeyeater (*Grantiella picta*)

A striking honeyeater with dark upperparts, a pale underside, a pink-red bill and yellow flashes in its wings and tail. The Painted Honeyeater is most likely to be found in woodland or scrub where mistletoe grows, across inland eastern Australia. Its clear, two-part call is often repeated several times.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Painted Honeyeater |
| Scientific name | *Grantiella picta* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Meliphagidae |
| Genus | *Grantiella* |
