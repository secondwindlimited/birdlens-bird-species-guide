# How to identify the Chestnut-hooded Laughingthrush (*Pterorhinus treacheri*)

Look for a compact grey laughingthrush with a dark chestnut head, orange-yellow bill and bright pale stripe across the wing. A yellowish crescent behind the eye makes the eye look especially large. It lives in Borneo’s uplands and is often seen beside paths, at forest margins and near lodges, where it may come close to people.
