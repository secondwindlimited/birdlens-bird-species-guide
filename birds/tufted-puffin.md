# How to identify the Tufted Puffin (*Fratercula cirrhata*)

Look for a large, dark puffin with a bright white face, a heavy orange-red bill and long yellow plumes sweeping back from the head in breeding season. It nests on rocky Pacific islands and coastal cliffs, where it may be seen commuting between colonies and the sea or diving offshore. In winter, it ranges widely over the North Pacific.
