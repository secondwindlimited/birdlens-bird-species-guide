# How to identify the Northern Royal Albatross (*Diomedea sanfordi*)

A very large albatross with a white back and body, dark upper wings, and a mostly white tail. Its pink bill has a narrow black edge. It breeds on islands and at Taiaroa Head in New Zealand, then ranges across the southern oceans. At sea, it is especially likely to be seen over the Humboldt Current off western South America.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Northern Royal Albatross |
| Scientific name | *Diomedea sanfordi* |
