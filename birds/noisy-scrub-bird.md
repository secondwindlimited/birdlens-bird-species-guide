# How to identify the Noisy Scrub-bird (*Atrichornis clamosus*)

The Noisy Scrub-bird is a small, brown ground-dweller found in a very limited part of southern Western Australia. Its fine, dark feather markings are easy to miss in thick cover, and the bird is more often detected by sound than sight. Listen for the male’s varied, clear, ringing phrases in dense forest undergrowth, especially around leaf litter.

## What kind of bird is it?
