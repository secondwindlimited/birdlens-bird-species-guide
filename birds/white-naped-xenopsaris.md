# How to identify the White-naped Xenopsaris (*Xenopsaris albinucha*)

A small, slender gray-and-white bird with a dark cap and a bright white face and nape. Males show the strongest contrast; females have a warmer brown crown and upperparts. It often sits upright on exposed branches in open, damp country, especially near water. Listen for a delicate sequence of high, wavering whistles and peeps.
