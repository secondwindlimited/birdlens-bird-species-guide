# How to identify the Green-throated Mango (*Anthracothorax viridigula*)

A large, dark hummingbird of coastal areas and major river corridors, the Green-throated Mango is easiest to identify by the male’s green throat in good light. Look for it in mangroves, wet woodland and flowering trees, sometimes high in the canopy. Females are much harder to distinguish from Black-throated Mangoes, which share much of its range and habitat.
