# How to identify the Little Owl (*Athene noctua*)

Look for a compact, short-tailed owl with a level-looking crown, bright yellow eyes and pale marks across brown upperparts. White lines above the eyes can make it seem stern, and it sometimes sits upright in view during daylight. Little Owls favour open places with trees or rocks, so they may be found around farmland, orchards and quarries.
