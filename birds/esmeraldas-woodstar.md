# How to identify the Esmeraldas Woodstar (*Chaetocercus berlepschi*)

Among Ecuador’s tiniest hummingbirds, the Esmeraldas Woodstar is most readily recognised by the adult male’s bright purple throat, green chest band and forked tail. It lives only on the Pacific side of western Ecuador, in forest and wooded hills. It is generally considered resident, though it makes seasonal movements whose pattern is not yet clear.
