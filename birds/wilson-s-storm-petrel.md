# How to identify the Wilson's Storm-Petrel (*Oceanites oceanicus*)

Look for a small, dark storm-petrel with a clean white patch across the rump, pale shading on the upper wings and long legs showing behind the tail in flight. It often hangs above the sea, tapping its feet on the surface as it picks food. Most birdwatchers encounter it offshore, especially in the North Atlantic during the northern summer.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Wilson's Storm-Petrel |
| Scientific name | *Oceanites oceanicus* |

| Family | Oceanitidae |
| Genus | *Oceanites* |
