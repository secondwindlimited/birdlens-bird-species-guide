# How to identify the Little Blue Heron (*Egretta caerulea*)

Look for a small heron with a blue-gray body, a dark purplish head and neck, greenish legs, and a bill with a pale base and dark tip. Young birds can be almost entirely white, so plumage alone may not identify them. Little Blue Herons occur from the eastern United States south through the Caribbean and into South America, often around coastal wetlands.
