# How to identify the Montezuma Quail (*Cyrtonyx montezumae*)

A small, plump quail with a short tail and strikingly patterned wings. Males show a bold, swirling face design, while females are mostly brown and more easily overlooked. Look for it moving through tall grass beneath open woodland in the southwestern United States or Mexico. It often freezes low to the ground, so a bird crossing a track may be the clearest view.
