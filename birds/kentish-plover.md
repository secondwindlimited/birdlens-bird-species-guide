# How to identify the Kentish Plover (*Anarhynchus alexandrinus*)

A small, pale plover with sandy upperparts, a fine black bill and a broken dark collar. In breeding plumage, look for a black face patch and dark marks on the sides of the breast; these become brown outside the breeding season. It is most often seen on sandy shores, mudflats and salt ponds, though it also occurs inland.
