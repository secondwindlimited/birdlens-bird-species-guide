# How to identify the Carnaby's Black-Cockatoo (*Zanda latirostris*)

Look for a mostly charcoal-grey cockatoo with a pale cheek patch and white panels along the sides of its tail. It occurs in southwestern Western Australia, often in eucalypt woodland, near pine plantations, and around farmland or town parks. Its slow, mournful cries and harsher screeches carry over feeding flocks. Baudin’s Black-Cockatoo is a close lookalike, but has a longer upper bill.
