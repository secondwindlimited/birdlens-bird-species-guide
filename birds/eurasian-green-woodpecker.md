# How to identify the Eurasian Green Woodpecker (*Picus viridis*)

A fairly large woodpecker with green upperparts, pale yellow-green underparts, a red crown and a yellow rump. Its loud, ringing calls often reveal it before it is seen. Watch for it on lawns, heath and open ground, where it searches for ants rather than feeding mainly on tree trunks. It occurs widely across much of Europe and parts of western Asia.
