# How to identify the Bonin White-eye (*Apalopteron familiare*)

Look for a small olive-grey bird with a bright yellow head and a bold, triangular black patch around each eye. The Bonin White-eye is found on Hahajima and nearby islands in Japan’s Ogasawara chain, where it uses forest as well as gardens and other altered habitats. It often feeds low down or on the ground, alone, in pairs or in small groups.
