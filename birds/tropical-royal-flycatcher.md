# How to identify the Tropical Royal Flycatcher (*Onychorhynchus coronatus*)

The Tropical Royal Flycatcher is a medium-sized, warm-brown bird with a long bill, cinnamon-toned rump and tail, and a distinctive flattened, hammer-shaped crest. The crest is usually held down; when raised, it forms a bright fan. Look for it alone or in pairs inside dense lowland forest, often in the middle storey and near streams.
