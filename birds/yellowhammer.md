# How to identify the Yellowhammer (*Emberiza citrinella*)

The male Yellowhammer is easy to pick out by its golden head and yellow-toned body, with a rusty rump and white tail edges that show in flight. Females and young birds are more subdued and streaked. Look for them in open farmland with hedges, on heathland, or around woodland clearings; in winter they often feed in flocks on the ground.
