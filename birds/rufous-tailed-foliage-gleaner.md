# How to identify the Rufous-tailed Foliage-gleaner (*Anabacerthia ruficaudata*)

Look for a medium-sized brownish foliage-gleaner with buff underparts, a bright rufous tail and a pale buff stripe behind the eye. Its rump stays brown, making the tail stand out. It lives in tropical rainforest, usually among the middle and upper foliage, where it searches leaf litter caught in branches for insects.
