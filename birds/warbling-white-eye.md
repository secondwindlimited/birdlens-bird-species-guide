# How to identify the Warbling White-eye (*Zosterops japonicus*)

Look for a tiny, olive-backed bird with a clear white ring around each eye, a yellow throat and pale green underparts. Warbling White-eyes are often easier to locate by their high, fine twitters or by watching lively groups move through foliage. They use habitats from woodland and farmland to city parks and gardens, and are especially widespread across the Hawaiian Islands.
