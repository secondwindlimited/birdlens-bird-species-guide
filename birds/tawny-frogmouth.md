# How to identify the Tawny Frogmouth (*Podargus strigoides*)

Look for a bulky, big-headed bird with a huge bristled bill and bright yellow eyes. Its grey, streaked feathers can make it almost disappear against a tree trunk or limb while it rests by day. Tawny Frogmouths occur across much of Australia and are often encountered in leafy suburbs, parks and gardens.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Tawny Frogmouth |
| Scientific name | *Podargus strigoides* |
| Order | Podargiformes |
| Family | Podargidae |
| Genus | *Podargus* |
