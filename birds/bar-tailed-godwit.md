# How to identify the Bar-tailed Godwit (*Limosa lapponica*)

Look for a slim wader with a long bill, pink near the base and dark at the tip, that curves slightly upwards. In breeding season, adults may be reddish below; in other seasons they are mostly grey-brown above and pale underneath. Away from its Arctic nesting grounds, it is most often seen on coastal mudflats and lagoon edges, sometimes feeding in flocks.
