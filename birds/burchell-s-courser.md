# How to identify the Burchell's Courser (*Cursorius rufus*)

Burchell’s Courser is a pale, sandy-rufous bird with long legs and a distinctive blue-grey area on the rear crown. It runs swiftly while feeding, often in pairs or small groups. Look for it in open, dry country across southern Africa, especially on gravelly plains and in sparsely vegetated grassland.
