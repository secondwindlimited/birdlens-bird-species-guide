# How to identify the Sharp-tailed Sandpiper (*Calidris acuminata*)

Look for a compact, pot-bellied sandpiper with a chestnut crown, pale eyebrow and greenish legs. Breeding adults show strong dark markings across the chest and flanks; young birds have a warmer buff breast and brighter head. It breeds on northern Siberian tundra, then occurs at wetlands and muddy shores across Australasia during the non-breeding season.
