# How to identify the Oriental Bay-Owl (*Phodilus badius*)

Look for a compact owl with a sharply angled, heart-shaped face, black eyes and small rounded brow tufts. Its upperparts are reddish brown, while the underside is pale cream with light marks on the flanks. It often stands upright and can blend into foliage. Search wooded areas, including evergreen forest and mangroves, across South and Southeast Asia; it is resident.
