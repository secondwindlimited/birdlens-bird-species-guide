# How to identify the Superb Starling (*Lamprotornis superbus*)

Look for a compact, short-tailed starling with glossy blue-green plumage, a narrow white stripe across the breast and a rusty-red belly. In flight, white beneath the tail and a pale underwing patch stand out. It occurs in East African savanna and open woodland, and is often seen in small groups around scrub, fields or villages.

## What kind of bird is it?

| Scientific name | *Lamprotornis superbus* |
