# How to identify the Streak-backed Canastero (*Asthenes wyatti*)

A small, brown highland bird with a pointed, uneven-looking tail, dark streaks across its back and hints of rusty red in the wings and tail. It lives in open grassland and scrub in the Andes, from western Venezuela south to northern Argentina. Look for it feeding low to the ground; it may also pause on a more exposed perch to sing.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Streak-backed Canastero |
| Scientific name | *Asthenes wyatti* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Furnariidae |
| Genus | *Asthenes* |
