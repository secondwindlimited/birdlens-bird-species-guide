# How to identify the Congo Snake-Eagle (*Circaetus spectabilis*)

The Congo Snake-Eagle is a medium-sized forest raptor with a conspicuously large head, dark brown back, reddish-brown neck band and pale, spotted underparts. A long tail with broad dark bars is useful in flight. It lives in lowland forests of West and Central Africa and stays year-round. Its repeated, hollow, hornbill-like calls often reveal it before it comes into view.
