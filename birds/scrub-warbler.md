# How to identify the Scrub Warbler (*Scotocerca inquieta*)

A small, pale desert warbler, the Scrub Warbler is easiest to pick out by its long, dark-tipped tail, often held high, and its pale eyebrow above a dark eye line. It keeps to scattered bushes and dry wadis, where it hops over the ground or through scrub. Its loud trills and plaintive, two-part whistle often give it away.
