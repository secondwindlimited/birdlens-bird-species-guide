# How to identify the Eurasian Siskin (*Spinus spinus*)

A small finch with bright yellow markings and strong wing flashes is the Eurasian Siskin. Adult males show a yellow face and breast against a dark crown; females and young birds look greener and more streaked. Look for it in coniferous or mixed woods, but it also turns up in gardens and at seed feeders, especially in autumn and winter, often in groups.
