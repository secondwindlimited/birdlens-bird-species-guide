# How to identify the Ruby-throated Hummingbird (*Archilochus colubris*)

A tiny hummingbird with a green back, pale belly and long, fine bill. Adult males show a vivid red throat in good light, while females are mostly white below. Look for it around woodland edges, gardens and flower beds across eastern North America in summer. It also visits sugar-water feeders and spends the colder months farther south.

| Common name | Ruby-throated Hummingbird |
