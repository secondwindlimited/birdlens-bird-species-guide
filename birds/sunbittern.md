# How to identify the Sunbittern (*Eurypyga helias*)

The Sunbittern is usually seen alone or in a pair beside quiet rivers, marshes, or pools in tropical forest. At rest it has a slim, heronlike outline, but holds its body more level and carries a noticeably long tail. Its dark-and-pale striped head and finely barred back help separate it from other waterside birds. A sudden wing-spread reveals bold red, gold, and black markings.
