# How to identify the Fire-capped Tit (*Cephalopyrus flammiceps*)

A very small, compact tit with a fine pointed bill. In the breeding season, the male shows a distinctive orange-red patch on the head; the female has no red and looks more muted. Both have yellow-green upperparts and pale underparts. Look for it in Himalayan broadleaf woodland, or in lower forest and wooded areas in India during winter.
