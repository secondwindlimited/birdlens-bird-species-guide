# How to identify the Saltmarsh Sparrow (*Ammospiza caudacuta*)

An orange-faced sparrow with a pale throat, buffy, finely streaked sides and a short pointed tail is a good candidate. It lives in Atlantic tidal marshes, where it often slips through dense plants but may sing from an open perch. It is most often confused with Nelson’s Sparrow, especially in winter, when both can use the same marshes.

## What kind of bird is it?
