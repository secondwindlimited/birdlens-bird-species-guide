# How to identify the Snow Bunting (*Plectrophenax nivalis*)

Look for a compact, long-winged bird with white underparts and strong dark-and-pale wing markings, especially clear in flight. In winter, brown and rusty tones cover much of its head and back; males become cleaner black and white as breeding approaches. Snow Buntings are often seen in open fields and along shorelines in winter, or on rocky Arctic ground in summer.
