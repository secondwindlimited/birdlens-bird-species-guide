# How to identify the Great Spotted Woodpecker (*Dendrocopos major*)

Look for a compact, boldly patterned woodpecker with a black back, large white shoulder spots and red beneath the tail. Adult males have a red mark at the back of the head; young birds show red across the crown. It is often seen in woodland, parks and gardens across much of Eurasia, and may also visit bird feeders.
