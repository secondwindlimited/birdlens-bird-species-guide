# How to identify the Rose-breasted Grosbeak (*Pheucticus ludovicianus*)

In breeding plumage, the male is easy to pick out by its dark head and back, white wing marks, and vivid rose-red breast patch. Both sexes have a notably deep, pale pinkish bill. Look for this grosbeak in leafy woods across eastern North America in summer; it may also visit backyard feeders. Its clear, gentle song has a robin-like quality.
