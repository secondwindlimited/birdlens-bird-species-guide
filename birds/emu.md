# How to identify the Emu (*Dromaius novaehollandiae*)

The Emu is a very tall, flightless bird with a dark head, long legs and loose, grey-brown feathers. Its neck looks shaggy, with pale blue skin showing through in places. It occurs across Australia, often in open woodland or other lightly wooded country, where it may be seen alone, in pairs or moving with a group.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Emu |
| Scientific name | *Dromaius novaehollandiae* |
| Order | Casuariiformes |
| Family | Casuariidae |
| Genus | *Dromaius* |
