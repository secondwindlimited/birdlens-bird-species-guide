# How to identify the Elegant Crescentchest (*Melanopareia elegans*)

Look for a compact, long-tailed bird with a patterned black-and-cream head, dark breast band, rusty sides and vivid rufous wing patches. It stays close to cover in scrubby woodland, where a loud series of popping chips often gives it away. It occurs along the coast of Ecuador and into northwestern Peru.
