# How to identify the Eurasian Eagle-Owl (*Bubo bubo*)

A very large, broad-winged owl with raised ear tufts, orange eyes and a pale, dark-streaked breast. It is most often detected after dark by its deep, far-carrying hoots, though it may sit in view at dusk on a prominent perch. Look for it in wooded country, rocky ravines and other suitable habitat across much of Europe and Asia.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Eurasian Eagle-Owl |
| Scientific name | *Bubo bubo* |
| Order | Strigiformes |
| Family | Strigidae |
| Genus | *Bubo* |
