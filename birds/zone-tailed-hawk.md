# How to identify the Zone-tailed Hawk (*Buteo albonotatus*)

A dark, slender hawk in soaring flight, the Zone-tailed Hawk is recognized by its angled wings, rocking motion, pale bars across the flight feathers and conspicuous white-banded tail. Its flight silhouette can suggest a Turkey Vulture. It occurs from the southwestern United States through much of Latin America, though northern birds move south for winter.
