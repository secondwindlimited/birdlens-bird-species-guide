# How to identify the Sooty Shearwater (*Ardenna grisea*)

The Sooty Shearwater is a medium-sized seabird with dark brown plumage and long, narrow wings. In good light, a silvery stripe is visible beneath each wing. It banks low over the sea, alternating strong wingbeats with glides. It travels across the oceans in huge numbers and is sometimes seen near shore, especially in the Pacific.

|---|---|
| Common name | Sooty Shearwater |
| Scientific name | *Ardenna grisea* |

| Genus | *Ardenna* |
