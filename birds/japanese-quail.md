# How to identify the Japanese Quail (*Coturnix japonica*)

Usually detected only when it bursts from grass, the Japanese Quail is a compact, brown bird with a pale face and light eyebrow stripes. It spends much of its time concealed on the ground in meadows and crop fields. A flushed bird rises abruptly, its wings giving a whistle; in the breeding season, males may also reveal themselves with a short, forceful call.
