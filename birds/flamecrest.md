# How to identify the Flamecrest (*Regulus goodfellowi*)

On Taiwan’s high mountain slopes, the Flamecrest is easiest to pick out by its bright yellow-orange crown, black eye patches edged in white, and quick movements through evergreen foliage. It is a very small bird, usually encountered in upland conifer woods and nearby scrub. A raised orange crown signals an adult male; thin, high notes may reveal it before it comes into view.
