# How to identify the Red-cheeked Cordonbleu (*Uraeginthus bengalus*)

A small, slim waxbill with a long tail, the Red-cheeked Cordonbleu is easiest to recognise when a male is in view: look for a vivid red or orange cheek mark against brown above and blue below. It is usually found in pairs or small groups in open country, thorn scrub, savanna, farmland, and around villages across much of central and eastern Africa.
