# How to identify the White-winged Chough (*Corcorax melanorhamphos*)

A large, soot-black bird with a narrow, down-curved bill and striking red eyes, the White-winged Chough is easiest to pick out in flight, when broad white areas flash across its wings. On the ground it walks with a purposeful gait, often among a family group. Look for it in open woodland across eastern and south-eastern Australia. Its eyes and wing pattern distinguish it from the ravens it can resemble.
