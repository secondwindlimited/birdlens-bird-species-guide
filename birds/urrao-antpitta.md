# How to identify the Urrao Antpitta (*Grallaria urraoensis*)

The Urrao Antpitta is a small, elusive forest bird with chestnut-brown upperparts and a grey breast and belly. Its chestnut throat and subdued crown help distinguish it from similar antpittas. It lives in high cloud forest in a tiny area of Colombia’s western Andes. Most sightings are difficult to come by, though it has occasionally appeared at a feeder in the Colibrí del Sol reserve.
