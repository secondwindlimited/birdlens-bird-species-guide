# How to identify the Gray Flycatcher (*Empidonax wrightii*)

Look for a small, pale gray flycatcher with weak contrast between its body and wings, a long narrow bill, and a tail that repeatedly dips. That downward tail motion is often the most useful clue among similar Empidonax species. It breeds in open sagebrush and juniper country across the western United States and southern British Columbia, then winters mainly in Mexico.
