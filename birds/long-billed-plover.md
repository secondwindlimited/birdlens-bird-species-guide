# How to identify the Long-billed Plover (*Thinornis placidus*)

On stony riverbanks, look for a medium-sized plover with a brown back, white underparts and dark bands across the forehead and chest. Its long wings and tail give it a more elongated shape than the similar Little Ringed Plover, which also has a more obvious yellow eye-ring. It may stay hidden among the stones until its sharp “piwee” call gives it away.
