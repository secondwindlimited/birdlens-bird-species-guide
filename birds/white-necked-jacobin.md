# How to identify the White-necked Jacobin (*Florisuga mellivora*)

A fairly large hummingbird of humid tropical lowlands, the White-necked Jacobin is easiest to recognise when an adult male shows its dark blue head and chest, white belly and broad white tail. It often feeds high in the canopy, with its tail held up, but also visits forest margins, clearings and gardens. Females vary considerably in appearance.
