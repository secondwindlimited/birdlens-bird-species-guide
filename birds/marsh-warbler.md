# How to identify the Marsh Warbler (*Acrocephalus palustris*)

Look for a medium-sized brown warbler with a short bill, pale legs and light tips on its wing feathers. It can be difficult to distinguish by sight from Eurasian Reed-Warbler, so listen for the male’s loud, varied song, which includes imitations of other birds. It breeds among thick, leafy growth in damp places and spends the non-breeding season in Africa.
