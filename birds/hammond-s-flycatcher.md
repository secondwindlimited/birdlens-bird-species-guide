# How to identify the Hammond's Flycatcher (*Empidonax hammondii*)

A compact, gray-olive Empidonax with a bright white eye-ring, two pale wing bars and a small bill is Hammond's Flycatcher. Its underside is pale, with a gray breast and yellow shading along the flanks. In breeding season, look for it among mature western conifers, often high in the trees; during migration and winter it may occur in varied wooded and brushy habitats.
