# How to identify the Stresemann's Bush-Crow (*Zavattariornis stresemanni*)

The Ethiopian Bushcrow is a small, pale grey crow-like bird with a dark tail and wings and vivid blue skin around its eyes. Look for it in small groups in the dry savannas and farmland of southern Ethiopia. Its high, chattering calls sound more like those of a babbler than the calls of a typical crow.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Stresemann's Bush-Crow |
| Scientific name | *Zavattariornis stresemanni* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Corvidae |
| Genus | *Zavattariornis* |
