# How to identify the Black-tailed Godwit (*Limosa limosa*)

The Black-tailed Godwit is a tall wader with an elongated bill whose base is pink, and a dark tail. In summer, adults show warm orange-red coloring on the head and chest; in winter, they are mostly grey-brown. Its pale wing stripe and rump are easy to see in flight. It breeds across Iceland and northern Eurasia, and winters in wetlands across several continents.
