# How to identify the Talamanca Hummingbird (*Eugenes spectabilis*)

One of the largest hummingbirds in its highland range, the Talamanca is often recognized by its size. A male looks mostly green, but a change in light reveals a violet crown and blue-green throat. Females are less vivid, with gray-brown, mottled underparts and a pale mark behind each eye. Look for it around mountain forest and hummingbird feeders in Costa Rica and western Panama.
