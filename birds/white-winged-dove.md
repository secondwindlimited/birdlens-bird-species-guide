# How to identify the White-winged Dove (*Zenaida asiatica*)

A plump, gray-brown dove, larger and more solid-looking than a Mourning Dove, with a bright white band along each wing. The white stands out most when the bird takes flight, beside the dark outer feathers; white tail tips offer another clue. It occurs from the southern United States through Central America and the Caribbean, and often visits feeders or gathers in flocks.
