# How to identify the Red-lored Whistler (*Pachycephala rufogularis*)

The Red-lored Whistler is a stocky grey songbird with a substantial dark bill and a warm orange-buff throat. In males, the colour reaches onto the face above the bill. Look for it in dry mallee and scrubby woodland across parts of South Australia, Victoria and New South Wales. Its varied whistles and repeated “chew-chew-chew-chew” call can help locate it.
