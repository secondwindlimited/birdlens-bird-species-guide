# How to identify the Rock Pigeon (*Columba livia*)

A large, sturdy pigeon, the Rock Pigeon is easiest to pick out in its blue-grey form by the pale rump, paired black wing marks and green-purple neck sheen. It occurs naturally around rocky cliffs and caves, but many people encounter feral birds on city buildings, bridges and farmland. Feral plumage ranges widely, so pattern and shape are more dependable than colour.
