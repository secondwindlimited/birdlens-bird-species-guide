# How to identify the Long-tailed Shrike (*Lanius schach*)

A large shrike with a dark face stripe, a pale grey back and a long, narrow tail. The amount of rusty orange on its lower back and sides varies among forms. Look for it sitting in view on a bush or wire in open country. Its harsh, ringing calls and occasional imitations of other birds can also help identify it.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Long-tailed Shrike |
| Scientific name | *Lanius schach* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Laniidae |
| Genus | *Lanius* |
