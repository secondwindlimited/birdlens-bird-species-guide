# How to identify the Variable Antshrike (*Thamnophilus caerulescens*)

A small, sturdy antshrike with a hooked bill, the Variable Antshrike shows striking differences between sexes and across its range. Typical males are dark grey with pale marks on the wings and tail; females are mostly rusty brown. Listen for a loud, quick phrase of repeated notes. It lives in woodland, scrub and second growth across parts of South America.
