# How to identify the Eurasian Spoonbill (*Platalea leucorodia*)

Look for a large, white wader with a long, flattened bill that broadens into a rounded spatula at the tip. Breeding adults have a short crest at the back of the head and a yellow patch on the upper breast. The bird feeds by sweeping its bill through shallow water, so wetlands, tidal flats and estuaries are good places to search.
