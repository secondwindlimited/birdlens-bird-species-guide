# How to identify the Greater Painted-Snipe (*Rostratula benghalensis*)

Look for a medium-sized wetland bird with a bold white mark curving around the eye and a white sweep rising across the shoulder. The female is larger and more brightly coloured, with a reddish head and greenish-bronze upperparts; the male is brown and well camouflaged. Greater Painted-Snipes live in marshes and other vegetated wetlands across parts of Africa and Asia.
