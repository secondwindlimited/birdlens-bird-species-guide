# How to identify the Solomons Frogmouth (*Rigidipenna inexpectata*)

Look for a compact, brown to rufous night bird with prominent eyes, pale patches on its wings and barred feathers in its wings and tail. Its mottled plumage blends remarkably well with its surroundings. The Solomons Frogmouth is a resident of the Solomon Islands, where it is seldom seen; records extend from sea level to about 700 metres.
