# How to identify the Black-and-white Warbler (*Mniotilta varia*)

The Black-and-white Warbler is a small bird with sharp black-and-white stripes from its head down its back and sides. It works its way over trunks and limbs, often moving like a nuthatch as it picks at insects. Look for it in mature woodland during the breeding season, or in a broader range of wooded places farther south in winter.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Black-and-white Warbler |
| Scientific name | *Mniotilta varia* |

| Genus | *Mniotilta* |
