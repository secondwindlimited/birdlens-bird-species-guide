# How to identify the Gray Falcon (*Falco hypoleucos*)

Look for a mostly gray falcon with a white underside and a yellow cere. This uncommon, nomadic bird lives mainly in Australia's dry interior and may perch high on a communications tower. In flight, the outer wing is darker than the inner part, and the wings are less sharply tapered than a Peregrine Falcon's. A pale Brown Falcon is the main look-alike.
