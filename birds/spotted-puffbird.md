# How to identify the Spotted Puffbird (*Bucco tamatia*)

Look for a stocky, big-headed bird with a dark bill, red eye, black marks on the sides of its neck and conspicuously spotted pale underparts. It often stays still among middle or lower branches, making its patterned face and belly useful clues. The Spotted Puffbird is resident in northern and western Amazonia and nearby regions, where it uses river-edge forest and other fairly open woodland.
