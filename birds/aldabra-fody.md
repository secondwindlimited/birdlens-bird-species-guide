# How to identify the Aldabra Fody (*Foudia aldabrana*)

This compact, sparrow-shaped weaver is easiest to identify when a breeding male shows its scarlet head and breast above a bright yellow belly. Females and young birds are less vivid, but have a yellowish cast and a strong bill. The species lives only on Aldabra in the Seychelles, where it occurs in scrub, mangroves and wooded areas.
