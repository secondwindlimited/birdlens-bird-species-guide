# How to identify the Roseate Spoonbill (*Platalea ajaja*)

A large pink wader, the Roseate Spoonbill is easiest to pick out by its long grey bill, which broadens into a rounded paddle at the tip. Adults have vivid pink plumage, red patches on the wings and an orange tail. Look for it in shallow wetlands around the Gulf Coast, the Caribbean, Central America and much of South America.
