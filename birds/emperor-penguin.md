# How to identify the Emperor Penguin (*Aptenodytes forsteri*)

The Emperor Penguin is the largest penguin, with a bulky build, black head and back, white belly, and yellow patches beside the neck. It lives around Antarctica, where adults gather to breed on sea ice. Look for its upright stance and listen for loud, trumpet-like calls, especially among courting birds. Young penguins may travel beyond the pack ice.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Emperor Penguin |
| Scientific name | *Aptenodytes forsteri* |
| Order | Sphenisciformes |
| Family | Spheniscidae |
| Genus | *Aptenodytes* |
