# How to identify the Chatham Islands Shag (*Leucocarbo onslowi*)

A large black-and-white shag with pink feet, orange-red facial skin and bold pale patches across the back and wings. Adults show a dark head above a white underside; young birds are browner. It is found only in the Chatham archipelago, where it feeds close to shore and may be seen around bays, inlets and Te Whanga Lagoon.
