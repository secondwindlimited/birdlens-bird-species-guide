# How to identify the White-browed Babbler (*Pomatostomus superciliosus*)

The White-browed Babbler is a compact, long-tailed bird with a down-curving bill, pale eyebrow and dark stripe through the eye. Its light throat and chest contrast with a darker lower belly. Look for it in southern Australian woodland and scrub, usually in a noisy group searching among fallen leaves.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | White-browed Babbler |
| Scientific name | *Pomatostomus superciliosus* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Pomatostomidae |
| Genus | *Pomatostomus* |
