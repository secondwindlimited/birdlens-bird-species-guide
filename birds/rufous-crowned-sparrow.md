# How to identify the Rufous-crowned Sparrow (*Aimophila ruficeps*)

This sparrow is recognised by its rusty-red crown and bold facial markings: a pale ring around the eye, a reddish line behind it, and a dark-edged white cheek stripe. Its body is mostly gray and brown, without breast streaks in adults. It keeps close to the ground on dry, brushy slopes in the southwestern United States and Mexico, and its song may reveal it before it comes into view.
