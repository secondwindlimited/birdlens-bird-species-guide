# How to identify the Red-wattled Lapwing (*Vanellus indicus*)

Look for a large lapwing with a black head and chest, bright red facial wattles, and a red bill with a dark tip. White bands along the sides of the head stand out against the black and brown plumage. It is usually seen in pairs or small groups on open ground, especially near wetlands, fields, and pools across South Asia.
