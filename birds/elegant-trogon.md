# How to identify the Elegant Trogon (*Trogon elegans*)

A medium-sized trogon, the Elegant Trogon is easiest to pick out by the male’s metallic green upperparts, white chest band and vivid red-orange underside. Females are greyer and browner, with a pale mark behind the eye. Look for it sitting quietly in wooded canyons and dry forests; its harsh calls often come in descending series.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Elegant Trogon |
| Scientific name | *Trogon elegans* |
| Order | Trogoniformes |
| Family | Trogonidae |
| Genus | *Trogon* |
