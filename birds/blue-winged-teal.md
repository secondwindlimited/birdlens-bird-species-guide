# How to identify the Blue-winged Teal (*Spatula discors*)

Small and compact, the Blue-winged Teal is easiest to identify in flight, when pale blue areas show across the front of each wing. A breeding male has a crisp white mark beside the eye and spotted flanks. Females are mottled brown, with a subtle pale facial mark. It is most often seen on quiet, shallow marshes and ponds, alone, in pairs, or among other ducks.
