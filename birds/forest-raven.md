# How to identify the Forest Raven (*Corvus tasmanicus*)

The Forest Raven is a large, stocky black raven with a powerful bill and loose feathers beneath the throat. Its slow, low calls and lack of wing-flicking while calling help separate it from other Australian corvids. It is widespread in Tasmania and also occurs in parts of southeastern mainland Australia, including scattered areas of New South Wales.

|---|---|
