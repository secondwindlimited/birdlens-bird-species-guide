# How to identify the White Ibis (*Eudocimus albus*)

Look for a bulky, white wader with a long orange-red bill that bends down at the tip and red legs. Dark wing tips show best when it flies. White Ibises often feed together in shallow wetlands and salt marshes, but in some areas they also search lawns and parking areas for scraps. The species is resident, though individuals may wander beyond its usual range.
