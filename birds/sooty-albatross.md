# How to identify the Sooty Albatross (*Phoebetria fusca*)

Look for a dark, smoky-brown albatross with a narrow pale arc around most of each eye and a fine yellow-orange stripe along the lower bill. It is a bird of open southern seas, usually seen far from land. Nesting birds gather on remote islands in the South Atlantic and southern Indian Ocean, where they use steep ground and grass-covered slopes.
