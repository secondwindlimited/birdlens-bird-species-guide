# How to identify the Eurasian Jackdaw (*Coloeus monedula*)

A compact crow with a dark body, pale grey hood and nape, and striking light eyes is usually easy to pick out. Eurasian Jackdaws often feed and travel in company, sometimes alongside larger crows or Rooks. They occur in villages and cities as well as farmland, parks and coastal cliffs; stonework, chimneys and other cavities can signal a nearby nest.
