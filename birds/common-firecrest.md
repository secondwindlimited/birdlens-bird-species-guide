# How to identify the Common Firecrest (*Regulus ignicapilla*)

A tiny, restless woodland bird, barely nine centimetres long, with a boldly patterned face. Look for the white eyebrow sitting above a black line through the eye, the bronze patch on the shoulder, and a crown stripe that is orange in males and yellow in females. It is brighter and more sharply marked than the Goldcrest, which has a plain face.
