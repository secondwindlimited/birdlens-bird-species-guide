# How to identify the Eurasian Tree Sparrow (*Passer montanus*)

A neat, slightly smaller relative of the House Sparrow in which males and females look the same. The whole crown and nape are rich chestnut, and each white cheek carries a clear black spot. A small black bib and a narrow white collar complete the pattern. In Europe it favours farmland and woodland edges; across much of East Asia it is the everyday town sparrow.
