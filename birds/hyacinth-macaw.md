# How to identify the Hyacinth Macaw (*Anodorhynchus hyacinthinus*)

The Hyacinth Macaw is a very large, long-tailed parrot, dressed almost entirely in deep blue. Bright yellow skin beside the eyes and at the base of the lower bill makes it unmistakable. Look for it in palm-rich savanna, open woodland and forest edges in central and eastern South America. It is often encountered in pairs, sometimes in small groups.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Hyacinth Macaw |
| Scientific name | *Anodorhynchus hyacinthinus* |
