# How to identify the Golden-crowned Sparrow (*Zonotrichia atricapilla*)

This large sparrow has a long tail and a yellow patch down the middle of its crown, edged by dark markings. The crown pattern stands out most clearly in breeding adults; younger birds look browner but usually retain some yellow. It occurs in western North America, nesting in Alaska and western Canada and moving south for winter, when it may turn up in scrub, forest edges, or gardens.
