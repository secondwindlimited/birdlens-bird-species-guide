# How to identify the Golden-fronted Leafbird (*Chloropsis aurifrons*)

A bulbul-sized green leafbird with a striking orange-gold patch above the bill, a dark face and throat, and a fine blue line beside the bill. The sexes look much alike. Listen for a lively, scratch-edged warble. It lives in forest and scrub across the Indian subcontinent and parts of Southeast Asia, and is often seen high in the canopy near fruit or flowers.

## What kind of bird is it?
