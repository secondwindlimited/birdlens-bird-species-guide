# How to identify the Western Tanager (*Piranga ludoviciana*)

Look for a pale, sturdy bill and two light bars across the wings. In breeding males, a red face stands out against a yellow body and dark back; females and young birds are much duller. Western Tanagers are often high in trees in western forests, especially during the breeding season, and migrate south for winter.
