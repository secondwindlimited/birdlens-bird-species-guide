# How to identify the Marbled Murrelet (*Brachyramphus marmoratus*)

The Marbled Murrelet is a squat, small auk with a narrow dark bill and pointed wings. In winter, its black, white and gray pattern is conspicuous; in summer, it becomes mottled brown. Watch for it in small groups on Pacific coastal water, where it dives for fish. It also depends on mature forest for nesting, so birds may occur well inland from the shore.
