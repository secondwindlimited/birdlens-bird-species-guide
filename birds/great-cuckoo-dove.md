# How to identify the Great Cuckoo-Dove (*Reinwardtoena reinwardti*)

The Great Cuckoo-Dove is a notably large pigeon with an exceptionally long tail. Its rich chestnut upperparts set off a much paler head and underside; the pale areas are white on Biak and some Moluccan islands, and grey in other regions. Look for it in forest beneath the canopy, often alone, though fruiting trees may draw several birds together.
