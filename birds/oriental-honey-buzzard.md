# How to identify the Oriental Honey-buzzard (*Pernis ptilorhynchus*)

A large forest raptor, the Oriental Honey-buzzard is recognizable by its slim, small-looking head, long neck and barred tail. In flight, look for dark bands beneath the wings and rounded wingtips. It lives in tropical forests across much of its range; birds from Siberia and Japan travel south for winter. Migrating birds may gather in huge flocks.
