# How to identify the Red-billed Streamertail (*Trochilus polytmus*)

Look for a male with a shining green body, dark cap and long, narrow tail streamers; its bill is red with a dark tip. Females have shorter tails and pale underparts. The Red-billed Streamertail occurs across most of Jamaica, from lowland woods to mountain forest, and often visits garden feeders. It is absent from the island’s far east, where the Black-billed Streamertail occurs.
