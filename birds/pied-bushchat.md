# How to identify the Pied Bushchat (*Saxicola caprata*)

A small, upright chat of open country across southern Asia, the Pied Bushchat is easiest to recognise when a male is in view: his mostly black plumage is marked with white on the wing, rump and lower body. Females are brown and less conspicuous. Look for it on exposed scrub, grassland, field edges and cultivated ground.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Pied Bushchat |
| Scientific name | *Saxicola caprata* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Muscicapidae |
| Genus | *Saxicola* |
