# How to identify the Pacific Loon (*Gavia pacifica*)

Look for a medium-sized, dark loon with a compact, straight bill and rounded head. In summer, adults have a gray head, black throat, fine white neck stripes, and a black-and-white patterned back. In winter, the throat is white against darker upperparts. It breeds on tundra ponds and lakes, then spends the colder months on Pacific waters from North America to East Asia.
