# How to identify the Tumbes Hummingbird (*Thaumasius baeri*)

A small, subdued hummingbird with green upperparts, a pale gray belly, a white mark behind the eye and a sturdy, nearly straight bill. It lives in dry country on the Pacific side of the Andes, from southwestern Ecuador into northwestern Peru. Look for it in scrub or along the margins of deciduous forest; it stays in its range year-round.
