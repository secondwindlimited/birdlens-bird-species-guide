# How to identify the Crested Auklet (*Aethia cristatella*)

A small, stocky, dark auklet with pale eyes, white feathers sweeping back from the eyes, and a conspicuous orange bill. In breeding plumage, look for its curved black crest. It spends much of its life at sea, but in late spring and summer gathers on rocky islands and coastal cliffs around the Bering Sea and northern Pacific.
