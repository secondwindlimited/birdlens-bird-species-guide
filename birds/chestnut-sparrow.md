# How to identify the Chestnut Sparrow (*Passer eminibey*)

The Chestnut Sparrow is a very small, sturdy sparrow. Breeding males are deep chestnut, with a darker face and blackish wings and tail; females have gray heads, brown backs, and pale underparts. Look for it in dry East African country, including open savanna, fields, and village edges. It often gathers in flocks, sometimes with other bird species.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
