# How to identify the Olive-sided Flycatcher (*Contopus cooperi*)

Look for a stout, upright bird on an exposed high branch: its dark grey breast sides frame a clean white throat and central belly, making a vest-like pattern. It is larger and heavier-billed than the pewees it resembles. In North America it breeds in open forest and bog country, then travels south for the non-breeding season.
