# How to identify the Gray-headed Canary-Flycatcher (*Culicicapa ceylonensis*)

Look for a small, lively woodland bird with a slate-gray head, a fine pale ring around each eye, an olive-toned back, and vivid yellow underparts. It often perches upright, then darts out to catch insects. Listen for squeaky whistles or quick, bouncing notes. It occurs in wooded parts of southern and southeastern Asia, from low country to mountain forests.
