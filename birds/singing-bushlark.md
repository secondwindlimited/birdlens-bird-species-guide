# How to identify the Singing Bushlark (*Mirafra javanica*)

The Singing Bushlark is a compact, pale brown lark with a prominent buff eyebrow and little streaking across the centre of its breast. Look for it feeding on the ground in open grassland, dry plains or farmland, where its subdued plumage can make it difficult to spot. When disturbed, it often gives a sharp call; its elaborate song may come from a perch or during flight.
