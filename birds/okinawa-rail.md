# How to identify the Okinawa Rail (*Gallirallus okinawae*)

The Okinawa Rail is recognised by its scarlet bill, curved white stripe behind the eye and finely barred dark underside. It occurs only in Yanbaru, the forested north of Okinawa Island, where it searches the ground for food. Look for it crossing a road or clearing. Its loud squeals and clattering calls are most often heard near dawn, dusk or at night.
