# How to identify the Elf Owl (*Micrathene whitneyi*)

At roughly sparrow scale, the Elf Owl is recognized by its tiny build, yellow eyes and pale eyebrow marks against mottled gray, rusty red and white feathers. It is active after dark, but its thin, chuckling or plaintive calls often reveal it near sunset. Look for it in desert-edge woodland and scrub in the southwestern United States during warmer months; it spends winter farther south in Mexico.
