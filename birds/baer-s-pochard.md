# How to identify the Baer's Pochard (*Aythya baeri*)

Baer’s Pochard is a medium-sized diving duck with striking chestnut-and-white flanks. Males have a dark, often green-sheened head and pale eyes; females are brown-headed and dark-eyed. It breeds in parts of Siberia and China, and is most likely to be encountered in winter, alone or in small groups among more numerous ducks on open water.
