# How to identify the Ring Ouzel (*Turdus torquatus*)

Look for a dark thrush with a clear white curve across the chest, especially in a male. Females and young birds are browner, and their chest marking is less distinct. Ring Ouzels breed on upland moors and mountain slopes across parts of Europe. During migration, they may also appear in farmland; in winter, many head to southern Europe or north-west Africa.
