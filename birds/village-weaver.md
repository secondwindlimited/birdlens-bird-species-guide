# How to identify the Village Weaver (*Ploceus cucullatus*)

Look for a sturdy weaver with a thick, pointed bill and dark reddish eyes. Breeding males are bright yellow with black and chestnut markings; females are duller and streaked. The bird is often seen in noisy groups around farmland, weedy ground and settlements across much of sub-Saharan Africa. Introduced populations also live on Caribbean and Indian Ocean islands.
