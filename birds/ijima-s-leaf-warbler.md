# How to identify the Ijima's Leaf Warbler (*Phylloscopus ijimae*)

A small, olive-green leaf warbler with pale underparts, a fine bill and pinkish-brown legs. Look for its faint head markings and yellow lower bill, which help separate it from the larger Eastern Crowned Leaf Warbler. It breeds on Japanese islands, especially in mature coastal woodland, and is most often encountered away from its breeding sites during migration.
