# How to identify the Salvin's Albatross (*Thalassarche salvini*)

A large, grey-headed albatross with white underparts and a narrow dark border along the front of the wing. Adults have a pale yellowish bill marked with a dark spot near its end; young birds have darker bills and more grey on the body. It breeds on islands south of New Zealand and is often seen at sea off Chile and Peru.
