# How to identify the American Wigeon (*Mareca americana*)

Look for a compact, round-headed dabbling duck with a stubby neck and fine bill. A breeding male is especially distinctive: a pale forehead and crown meet a green band behind the eye. Females are brown, with rusty flanks. The species is most often seen in North America around lakes and marshes, sometimes feeding in fields beside water.
