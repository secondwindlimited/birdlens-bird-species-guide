# How to identify the American Bittern (*Botaurus lentiginosus*)

The American Bittern is a bulky, brown heron with a streaked throat and a dark mark down each side of its neck. Look for it standing among reeds in marshes and bogs; it often stays hidden until dawn or dusk, when it may fly low across a wetland. In spring and summer, its deep, hollow call can reveal a bird that is hard to see.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
