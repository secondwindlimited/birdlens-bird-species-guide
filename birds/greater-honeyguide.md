# How to identify the Greater Honeyguide (*Indicator indicator*)

Look for a compact, brown honeyguide that stands upright and shows bright white markings near the tail edges in flight. Males have a pink bill, black throat and large white patches beside the head; females are duller, with a dark bill and pale throat. It is usually found alone or in pairs in woodland, cultivated country and gardens, though it can be hard to spot.
