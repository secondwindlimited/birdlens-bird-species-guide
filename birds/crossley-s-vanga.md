# How to identify the Crossley's Vanga (*Mystacornis crossleyi*)

Small and secretive, Crossley’s Vanga is easiest to pick out by its long, olive-grey bill and the male’s dark face and throat. The female has pale underparts instead. It spends much of its time close to the ground, moving through dense growth and leaf litter in Madagascar’s eastern forests, where it may be heard before it is seen.
