# How to identify the Cassia Crossbill (*Loxia sinesciuris*)

Look for a compact, big-headed finch with a heavy bill whose tips cross. Adult males are muted red to orange, while females are olive-yellow; young birds show stronger streaking. Its dry, low-pitched “dip” calls in flight help distinguish it from Red Crossbills. It occurs year-round in mature lodgepole pine woods of Idaho’s South Hills and Albion Mountains.
