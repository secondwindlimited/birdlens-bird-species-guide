# How to identify the Hoatzin (*Opisthocomus hoazin*)

The Hoatzin is a bulky, long-tailed bird with a small head, bare blue face and bristling orange-red crest. Its brown plumage and awkward movements make it distinctive along tropical waterways. Look for it in pairs or groups in waterside trees and shrubs, where it often gives rough, breathy calls.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Hoatzin |
| Scientific name | *Opisthocomus hoazin* |
| Order | Opisthocomiformes |
| Family | Opisthocomidae |
| Genus | *Opisthocomus* |
