# How to identify the Red-billed Tropicbird (*Phaethon aethereus*)

Look for a mostly white seabird with a vivid red bill and two slender white plumes trailing from its tail. Dark marks edge the wings and cross the back. Red-billed Tropicbirds spend much of their time over tropical seas, so they are seldom seen from shore except near nesting islands. There, pairs may circle high above cliffs, calling sharply.
