# How to identify the Pompadour Cotinga (*Xipholena punicea*)

Look for a plump, medium-sized bird high above the forest floor. A male is easy to pick out in flight: its brilliant white wings stand out against a dark, wine-red body. Females are more easily overlooked, with plain grey plumage and a stocky outline. Pompadour Cotingas live in the rainforest canopy and may gather near fruiting trees.

| Common name | Pompadour Cotinga |
| Scientific name | *Xipholena punicea* |

| Family | Cotingidae |
| Genus | *Xipholena* |
