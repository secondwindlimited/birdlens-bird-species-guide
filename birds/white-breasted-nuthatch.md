# How to identify the White-breasted Nuthatch (*Sitta carolinensis*)

A white face, dark crown and blue-gray back make the White-breasted Nuthatch easy to pick out. Watch it shuffle headfirst or upside-down along trunks and branches in mature woodland. It is also at home in parks, gardens and orchards, and may come to backyard feeders. Its insistent, nasal yammering calls often give away a nearby bird.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
