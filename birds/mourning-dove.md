# How to identify the Mourning Dove (*Zenaida macroura*)

Look for a slim, softly brown dove with a long, narrowing tail and dark marks across its wings. In flight, pale outer tail feathers show against darker inner ones, and the wings often whistle as it takes off. Mourning Doves occur across much of North America and are especially familiar around farms, open neighborhoods, and backyard feeders.
