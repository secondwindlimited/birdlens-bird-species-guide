# How to identify the Giant Nuthatch (*Sitta magna*)

The Giant Nuthatch is a large, sturdy bird with blue-grey upperparts, pale underparts and a bold black stripe through each eye. Look for cinnamon tones on the belly and rusty feathers beneath the tail. It lives in mountain forests from south-central China to north-western Thailand, but its need for mature pine stands makes it uncommon across much of that range.
