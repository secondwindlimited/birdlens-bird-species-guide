# How to identify the Himalayan Cuckoo (*Cuculus saturatus*)

Look for a medium-sized cuckoo with grey upperparts, a dark, pale-spotted tail and black bars across the white belly. Its buff-toned feathers beneath the tail help separate it from Common and Lesser Cuckoos. It is most likely to be encountered in forested hills during the breeding season, and in Southeast Asian habitats when it migrates south.

|---|---|
