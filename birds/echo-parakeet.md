# How to identify the Echo Parakeet (*Psittacula eques*)

The Echo Parakeet is a long-tailed green bird found only on Mauritius. Look for males’ red bills and pink-and-black neck markings; females have dark bills and less vivid plumage. Its nasal, horn-like calls often come in rapid bursts and sound lower than the sharp cries of the Rose-ringed Parakeet. It is most likely to be seen in native forest, especially in the southwest.
