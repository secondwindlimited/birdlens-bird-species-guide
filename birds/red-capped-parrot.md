# How to identify the Red-capped Parrot (*Purpureicephalus spurius*)

Recognise this medium-sized parrot by its red crown, vivid green cheeks, purple underparts and long, pale bill. It lives in southwestern Australia, especially in eucalypt woodland with Marri trees. Watch for birds using their strong bills to open seeds, or listen for rough chatter and squawks. In drier parts of its range, it may move locally as conditions change.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Red-capped Parrot |
| Scientific name | *Purpureicephalus spurius* |
| Order | Psittaciformes |
| Family | Psittaculidae |
| Genus | *Purpureicephalus* |
