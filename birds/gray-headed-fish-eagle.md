# How to identify the Gray-headed Fish-Eagle (*Icthyophaga ichthyaetus*)

Look for a bulky eagle with a gray head, brown upperparts, white underside and a white tail crossed near its end by a dark band. It lives around waterways across the Indian subcontinent and much of Southeast Asia, where its coarse, yelping calls may reveal it from nearby.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Gray-headed Fish-Eagle |
| Scientific name | *Icthyophaga ichthyaetus* |
| Order | Accipitriformes |
| Family | Accipitridae |
| Genus | *Icthyophaga* |
