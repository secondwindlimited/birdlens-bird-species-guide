# How to identify the Mallee Emuwren (*Stipiturus mallee*)

Look for a very small, warm-brown wren moving among dense spinifex in mallee country. Its exceptionally fine tail, formed from six hairlike feathers, extends far beyond its body and is the clearest clue. Adult males have a blue throat and upper breast; females are brown and lack blue. The bird is found only in far western Victoria and nearby South Australia.
