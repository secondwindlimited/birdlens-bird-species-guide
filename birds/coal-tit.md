# How to identify the Coal Tit (*Periparus ater*)

A compact, restless tit, the Coal Tit is easiest to recognise by the pale patch at the back of its dark head and two light bars across each folded wing. It moves briskly through conifers and mixed trees, often in winter flocks, and may also visit garden feeders. Its thin, high contact calls can reveal it before it comes into view.
