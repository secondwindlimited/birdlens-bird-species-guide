# How to identify the Trumpeter Swan (*Cygnus buccinator*)

Look for an enormous white swan with a long, upright neck and an entirely black bill. There is no yellow at the base of the bill, even on a distant bird. Trumpeter Swans are most likely around freshwater wetlands and ponds in Alaska, western Canada and the north-central United States during the breeding season. In winter, they use open waters farther south.
