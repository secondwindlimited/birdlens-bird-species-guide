# How to identify the Sarus Crane (*Antigone antigone*)

Look for a very large, pale-grey crane with long pink legs and a vivid patch of bare red skin that runs from the head onto the upper neck. It wades in shallow wetlands and also feeds in farmed fields. Across much of Asia it is distinctive; in north-eastern Australia, compare it carefully with the Brolga. Its loud, far-reaching bugles are another useful clue.
