# How to identify the Ash-throated Flycatcher (*Myiarchus cinerascens*)

A slim, long-tailed flycatcher with a small crest, gray chest and unusually light yellow wash below. Its pale throat and low, coarse calls help separate it from similar Myiarchus flycatchers. Look for it in dry, brushy country and open woodland across the southwestern United States and Mexico; in winter it moves south to Central America.

## What kind of bird is it?

| Common name | Ash-throated Flycatcher |
