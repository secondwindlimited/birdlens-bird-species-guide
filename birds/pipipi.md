# How to identify the Pipipi (*Mohoua novaeseelandiae*)

The Pipipi is a small, active forest bird of New Zealand’s South and Stewart Islands. Look for its brown back, smoky-grey face and throat, and pale buff-brown belly. It often moves quickly through the treetops in chattering groups, so its sharp calls may reveal it before it comes into view. It remains in its range year-round, though upland birds move to lower ground in winter.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Pipipi |
| Scientific name | *Mohoua novaeseelandiae* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Mohouidae |
| Genus | *Mohoua* |
