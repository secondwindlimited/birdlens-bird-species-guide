# How to identify the Black-legged Kittiwake (*Rissa tridactyla*)

Look for a small gull with a white body, grey back and wings, and sharply black wing ends. Adults also show a bright yellow bill and dark legs. Black-legged Kittiwakes breed on northern sea cliffs and spend the colder months far out over the ocean, though migrating or feeding flocks may sometimes be seen from shore.
