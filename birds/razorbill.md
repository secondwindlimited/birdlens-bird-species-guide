# How to identify the Razorbill (*Alca torda*)

The Razorbill is a sturdy black-and-white seabird with a deep, blunt bill and a narrow white mark running from the eye toward the bill. It breeds on rocky islands and coastal cliffs around the North Atlantic, then spends the colder months at sea. Look for it offshore, sometimes in groups, or near shore after winter storms.
