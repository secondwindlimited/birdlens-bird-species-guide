# How to identify the Upland Goose (*Chloephaga picta*)

Look for a large goose in the grasslands and wetlands of southern South America or the Falkland Islands. Males have a white head and neck, with either a white breast or dark bars across it; females are brown-headed and more heavily marked. Both sexes show a bold black-and-white pattern on the wings in flight. They are often seen in pairs or flocks beside roads and lakes.
