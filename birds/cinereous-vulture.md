# How to identify the Cinereous Vulture (*Aegypius monachus*)

Look for an enormous, nearly uniform brown vulture with broad, fingered wings and a jagged-looking rear edge. Adults have a paler, finely downy head; young birds lack this contrast. It circles above remote mountains and open uplands from southern Europe across Asia, where it may be seen searching for carrion or gathering at a carcass.
