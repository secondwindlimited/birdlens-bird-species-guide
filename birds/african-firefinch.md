# How to identify the African Firefinch (*Lagonosticta rubricata*)

A small finch with reddish underparts, brown wings and a blue-grey bill. Many birds have a grey head, and all show a dark patch beneath the tail that helps separate them from similar firefinches. Look for them in pairs or small parties around scrub, wooded edges and cultivated land across much of sub-Saharan Africa. They stay in the same region year-round.

## What kind of bird is it?
