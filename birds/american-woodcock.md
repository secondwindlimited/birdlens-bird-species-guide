# How to identify the American Woodcock (*Scolopax minor*)

Look for a compact, rounded shorebird with a very long straight bill, a large eye set high on its head and warm buff underparts. Its mottled brown-and-gray back blends into cover. American Woodcocks are most often found on the ground in damp young woods, brushy patches and field edges across eastern North America, where they can be difficult to see in daylight.
