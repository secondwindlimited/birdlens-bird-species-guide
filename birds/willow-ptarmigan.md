# How to identify the Willow Ptarmigan (*Lagopus lagopus*)

A heavy-bodied grouse of northern open country, the Willow Ptarmigan is easiest to identify by its changing plumage. In winter it is white except for dark feathers at the sides of the tail; in summer males turn brown and reddish, while females are patterned gold-brown. Listen for low chuckles and clucks. Look for it in tundra, willow scrub, bogs and open woodland.
