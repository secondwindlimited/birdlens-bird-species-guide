# How to identify the Ring-necked Pheasant (*Phasianus colchicus*)

The male Ring-necked Pheasant is a large, long-tailed bird with a glossy blue-green head, red bare skin around the eyes and richly coloured body. A pale neck band is clear on some males but weak or absent on others. Females are mottled brown and much less conspicuous. Look for them in fields, brushy ground and woodland margins, including in many introduced populations.
