# How to identify the Graylag Goose (*Anser anser*)

A large, heavy-bodied goose with brownish-gray plumage, a broad pink-orange bill and pink legs is usually identifiable as a Graylag Goose. In flight, look for a pale gray area along the front of the upper wing and a wide white tip to the tail. It is encountered around lakes, marshes and other wetlands; in parts of Europe it also uses city parks.
