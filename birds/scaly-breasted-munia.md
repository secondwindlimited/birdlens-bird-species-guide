# How to identify the Scaly-breasted Munia (*Lonchura punctulata*)

Look for a compact, sparrow-sized munia with a dark head, warm brown back and white belly patterned like overlapping scales. Young birds are plainer and buff below. It often feeds in small groups in grassy places, gardens and crop fields. The species is native to South and Southeast Asia, and can also be found in places where escaped cage birds have settled.
