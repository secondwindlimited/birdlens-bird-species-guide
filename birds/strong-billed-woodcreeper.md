# How to identify the Strong-billed Woodcreeper (*Xiphocolaptes promeropirhynchus*)

Look for a very large, bulky woodcreeper climbing thick trunks, with an oversized gray-to-dark bill and delicate pale marks mostly on its head and upper chest. It can be difficult to spot despite its size. Search highland pine-oak and evergreen woods, where it forages among middle and upper branches and may travel with feeding flocks that include jays or orioles.
