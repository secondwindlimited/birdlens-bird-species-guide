# How to identify the Star-throated Antwren (*Rhopias gularis*)

A tiny, short-tailed antwren with a cinnamon-brown back, gray face and belly, and white markings on its throat. Males have a dark throat; females show larger white spots there. It lives low in evergreen forest in southeastern Brazil, especially among thick plants beside streams or in shaded foothill gullies. It stays in its range year-round.
