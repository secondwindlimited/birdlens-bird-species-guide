# How to identify the Asian Fairy-bluebird (*Irena puella*)

Look for a sturdy, red-eyed bird in fruiting trees. Males show brilliant blue across the back and wings, set against black underparts; females are darker blue-green with dusky wing tips. The species lives mainly in evergreen forest from lowlands to foothills, but may also visit gardens and plantations. Its paired whistles and sharp flight calls can reveal it in the canopy.
