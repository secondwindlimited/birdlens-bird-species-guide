# How to identify the Ashy-faced Owl (*Tyto glaucops*)

This large, night-active owl is easiest to pick out by its gray facial disc and warm buff-toned belly. Barn Owls have a similar outline, but their faces are white and their lower plumage looks lighter. Ashy-faced Owls hunt above open ground after dark, then shelter by day in trees or buildings. It occurs on Hispaniola and several islands in the Lesser Antilles.
