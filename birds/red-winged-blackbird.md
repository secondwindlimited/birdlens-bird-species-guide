# How to identify the Red-winged Blackbird (*Agelaius phoeniceus*)

Adult males are glossy black, with bright red patches on the wings that may be hidden when the bird is perched. Females are brown and heavily streaked, with a pointed bill that helps separate them from many sparrows. Look for Red-winged Blackbirds around marshes and wet fields, or in winter flocks; they may also visit backyard feeders.
