# How to identify the Short-eared Owl (*Asio flammeus*)

Look for a tawny-brown owl with a pale underside and conspicuous light panels near the ends of its wings. It often hunts in daylight, especially around sunrise and sunset, gliding low over open ground with loose, uneven wingbeats. Short-eared Owls are most likely to turn up in broad fields, marshes and other treeless country, rather than deep woodland.
