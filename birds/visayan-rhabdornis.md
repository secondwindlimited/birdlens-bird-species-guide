# How to identify the Visayan Rhabdornis (*Rhabdornis rabori*)

A medium-sized forest bird with a gray crown, black face mask, brown back and white belly. Its pale brown flanks have bold white streaks. Look for it in the canopy or sitting on a bare branch in the hills of Negros and Panay in the Philippines. High, thin chips and squeals may reveal it before it comes into view.
