# How to identify the Black-billed Cuckoo (*Coccyzus erythropthalmus*)

Look for a slim, brown-and-white cuckoo with a long, stepped tail and a dark bill. It can be hard to pick out among leaves, so its low, repeated “cu-cu-cu” calls are often the first clue. In the breeding season it occurs in wooded parts of eastern North America, especially along leafy woodland margins and in dense young growth.
