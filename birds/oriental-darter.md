# How to identify the Oriental Darter (*Anhinga melanogaster*)

Look for its narrow head and long neck rising above the water while the rest of the bird stays submerged. Adults are dark, with pale streaks on the wings and a light line along each side of the neck. Oriental Darters occur on lakes, rivers and other inland or coastal waters across South and Southeast Asia.

## What kind of bird is it?
