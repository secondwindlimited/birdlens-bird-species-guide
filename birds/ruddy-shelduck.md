# How to identify the Ruddy Shelduck (*Tadorna ferruginea*)

The Ruddy Shelduck is a large, warm orange-brown waterfowl with a much paler head; adult males show a slim dark collar. Look for it on open lakes, reservoirs and farmland, especially in South Asia during winter. In flight, broad white areas on the wings stand out against dark flight feathers.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Ruddy Shelduck |
| Scientific name | *Tadorna ferruginea* |
| Order | Anseriformes |
| Family | Anatidae |
| Genus | *Tadorna* |
