# How to identify the White Cockatoo (*Cacatua alba*)

A white cockatoo with a tall, fan-shaped crest and yellow flashes visible beneath its wings. It is often encountered in pairs, giving loud, harsh calls. Its natural home is the lowland forests of North Maluku, Indonesia; introduced birds also occur in Taiwan and on Oahu in Hawaii.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | White Cockatoo |
| Scientific name | *Cacatua alba* |
| Order | Psittaciformes |
| Family | Cacatuidae |
| Genus | *Cacatua* |
