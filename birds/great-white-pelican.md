# How to identify the Great White Pelican (*Pelecanus onocrotalus*)

Look for an exceptionally large, mostly white pelican with a long pink-and-yellow bill, a pale yellow pouch and pink skin around the eye. Black wing tips stand out when it flies. It feeds on lakes and other waters, sometimes with a group that drives fish together before scooping them up. It occurs across parts of Europe, Asia and sub-Saharan Africa.
