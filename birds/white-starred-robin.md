# How to identify the White-starred Robin (*Pogonocichla stellata*)

A small forest bird with a slate-grey head, bright yellow underparts and a dark tail marked with yellow at the sides. Tiny white spots near the eyes and on the throat can be hard to see. It keeps to dense cover and is often heard before it is found, in wooded mountain areas of eastern and southern Africa.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | White-starred Robin |
| Scientific name | *Pogonocichla stellata* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Muscicapidae |
| Genus | *Pogonocichla* |
