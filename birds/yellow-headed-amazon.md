# How to identify the Yellow-headed Amazon (*Amazona oratrix*)

A large, green parrot with a sturdy body and a conspicuous yellow head. The amount of yellow varies by region, and young birds show less than adults. Look for it in pairs or small groups in lowland areas with scattered trees, forest edges or open woodland in Mexico, Belize, Guatemala and Honduras. It is resident, but scarce in much of its range.
