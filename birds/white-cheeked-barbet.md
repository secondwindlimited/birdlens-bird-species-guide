# How to identify the White-cheeked Barbet (*Psilopogon viridis*)

Look for a compact green bird sitting upright and motionless in a tree, often noticed first by its repeated, hollow-sounding call. Its dark brown head contrasts with a white throat, pale eyebrow and conspicuous white area beside the ear. It occurs in southern India, where it uses wooded places ranging from gardens and small urban parks to forest.
