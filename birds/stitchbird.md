# How to identify the Stitchbird (*Notiomystis cincta*)

The male Stitchbird is easy to pick out by his black head, white tufts beside the face and bright yellow band at the neck. Both sexes have white markings on the wings and often hold the tail upright. Look for this New Zealand endemic on predator-free offshore islands and at protected mainland sites in the North Island; it may come close, but moves quickly.

## What kind of bird is it?
