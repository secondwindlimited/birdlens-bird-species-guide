# How to identify the Gray-faced Buzzard (*Butastur indicus*)

Look for a slim, long-winged hawk with a white throat, dark lines down its sides and a pale eyebrow. Adults show brown upperparts and a pale, dark-marked belly; young birds have more white on the head and underside. It is often seen over forested hills during migration, when groups may circle high together.
