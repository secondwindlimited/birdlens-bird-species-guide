# How to identify the Great Hornbill (*Buceros bicornis*)

A very large forest bird, the Great Hornbill is easy to pick out by its enormous yellow bill and curved casque. Its dark head and wings contrast with a pale neck, underside and tail. Listen for a run of deep, loud grunts. It lives in dense forest across parts of South and Southeast Asia, where it stays throughout the year.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Great Hornbill |
| Scientific name | *Buceros bicornis* |
| Order | Bucerotiformes |
| Family | Bucerotidae |
| Genus | *Buceros* |
