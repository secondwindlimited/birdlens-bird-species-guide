# How to identify the Asian House-Martin (*Delichon dasypus*)

Look for a small, stocky swallow with glossy blue-black upperparts, a striking white patch above the tail and pale underparts shaded grey. It is most likely to be seen near cliffs or mountain valleys, but also nests on buildings. Asian House-Martins often gather in noisy colonies, where their compact shape and pale rump stand out as they fly.
