# How to identify the Orange-bellied Parrot (*Neophema chrysogaster*)

A small, green parrot with blue wings and a vivid orange spot low on its belly. Look for a narrow blue band across the forehead and listen for a thin, buzzing call. It breeds in southwest Tasmania, then crosses to coastal South Australia and Victoria for winter. It may be seen feeding on the ground in grassy or saltmarsh habitat.

## What kind of bird is it?

| Common name | Orange-bellied Parrot |
| Scientific name | *Neophema chrysogaster* |

| Genus | *Neophema* |
