# How to identify the Southern Giant-Petrel (*Macronectes giganteus*)

A very large, long-winged seabird with a massive pale bill tipped green. Its plumage ranges from dark brown to almost entirely white, so colour alone is not enough for identification. Look for it over the Southern Ocean, around breeding islands and coasts, or following ships. In Tierra del Fuego, it is often seen from shore and ferries.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Southern Giant-Petrel |
| Scientific name | *Macronectes giganteus* |
| Order | Procellariiformes |
| Family | Procellariidae |
| Genus | *Macronectes* |
