# How to identify the Double-banded Plover (*Anarhynchus bicinctus*)

A small plover with a grey-brown back and white underparts. In breeding dress, look for a narrow dark stripe across the upper chest and a broader rusty band below it. Outside the breeding season, those markings fade, but a pale collar behind the head and buff-toned face can help. It breeds in New Zealand and is often seen on riverbeds or, seasonally, on Australian shores.
