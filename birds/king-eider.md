# How to identify the King Eider (*Somateria spectabilis*)

The King Eider is a sizeable sea duck. Breeding males are striking, with a bright orange knob above the bill, a pale blue-grey head and raised black back feathers. Females are brown and less conspicuous. It breeds around Arctic shores and spends winter on northern seas, often alone or in small groups with other eiders and sea ducks.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | King Eider |
| Scientific name | *Somateria spectabilis* |
| Order | Anseriformes |
| Family | Anatidae |
| Genus | *Somateria* |
