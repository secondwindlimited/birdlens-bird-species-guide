# How to identify the Rufous-crested Coquette (*Lophornis delattrei*)

This tiny hummingbird is easiest to pick out when a male’s bright, pointed orange crest catches the eye. Both sexes have a clear white mark over the rump; females are plainer, with a rusty forehead and throat. Look for Rufous-crested Coquettes around flowering shrubs at forest edges, in brushy clearings, and along roadsides within their Central and South American range.
