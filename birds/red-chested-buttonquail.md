# How to identify the Red-chested Buttonquail (*Turnix pyrrhothorax*)

A small, secretive Australian buttonquail with a pale eye and a fairly even grey-brown back. Females show a vivid rusty chest; males are duller and have a white chin. It keeps low in thick grass and grassy woodland, often revealing itself only when it bursts from cover and flies close to the ground.
