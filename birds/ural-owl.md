# How to identify the Ural Owl (*Strix uralensis*)

A large, pale-to-brown owl with a rounded, tuftless head, dark eyes and a conspicuously long tail is distinctive. Look for dark streaks on the light breast and a yellow bill. It is most likely to be encountered after dark in northern coniferous or mixed woodland, especially near a clearing or forest margin. Pairs can be heard calling to one another.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Ural Owl |
| Scientific name | *Strix uralensis* |

| Family | Strigidae |
| Genus | *Strix* |
