# How to identify the Atlantic Puffin (*Fratercula arctica*)

A small, stocky seabird of the North Atlantic, black above and white below, with a pale grey face and bright orange legs. In summer the large triangular bill is banded in red, yellow and blue-grey. Puffins come ashore only to breed, nesting in burrows on grassy islands and cliff tops, and spend the rest of the year far out at sea.

|---|---|
