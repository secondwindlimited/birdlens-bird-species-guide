# How to identify the Flammulated Flycatcher (*Ramphotrigon flammulatum*)

A compact, rather quiet flycatcher of western Mexico’s dry lowlands and foothills, the Flammulated Flycatcher is often heard before it is seen. Look for a dusky-streaked pale breast, yellowish belly, light markings around the eye and cinnamon edges on the wings. It usually sings from inside thorny scrub or woodland, and can be mistaken for a Myiarchus flycatcher.
