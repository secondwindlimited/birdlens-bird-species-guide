# How to identify the Sharp-tailed Ibis (*Cercibis oxycerca*)

A large, dark ibis with a strikingly extended tail, the Sharp-tailed Ibis is easiest to identify when it stands or flies low over open ground. Its red bill and legs contrast with glossy black feathers, and bare red skin shows around the face. Listen for a loud, trumpet-like call, often answered by a second bird. It lives in wetlands and flooded grasslands of northern South America.
