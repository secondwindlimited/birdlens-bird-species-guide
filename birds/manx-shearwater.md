# How to identify the Manx Shearwater (*Puffinus puffinus*)

A small black-and-white shearwater, the Manx is best picked out by its dark, smudged face and white cheek area that curves behind it. It skims close above the waves on quick, stiff-winged strokes, often showing a mostly white underside. Look for it over cool Atlantic waters; it is rarely seen from shore and may travel alone or in a small group.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Manx Shearwater |
| Scientific name | *Puffinus puffinus* |

| Family | Procellariidae |
| Genus | *Puffinus* |
