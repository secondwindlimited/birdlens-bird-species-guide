# How to identify the Steller's Sea-Eagle (*Haliaeetus pelagicus*)

Look for an enormous eagle with a huge orange bill and broad wings. Adults are dark brown, sharply contrasted by white on the belly, shoulders and tail. It lives along coasts and large rivers in northeastern Asia, often near fishing grounds. Many move south for winter to Japan, Korea and China, where they may gather with White-tailed Eagles.
