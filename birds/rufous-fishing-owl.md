# How to identify the Rufous Fishing-Owl (*Scotopelia ussheri*)

Look for a medium-sized, rounded-headed owl in tawny cinnamon tones, with fine dark streaks below and a dark bill whose base is yellow. It keeps close to rivers in West African lowland forest and is often difficult to see, resting by day in dense growth. After dark, its deep, mellow, dove-like hoot may reveal its presence.

| Common name | Rufous Fishing-Owl |
