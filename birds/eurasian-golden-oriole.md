# How to identify the Eurasian Golden Oriole (*Oriolus oriolus*)

The adult male is bright yellow with dark wings and a dark tail, while females and young birds are greener and more streaked. Look for the stout pinkish-red bill in every plumage. Eurasian Golden Orioles live among leafy trees in woodland, parks and gardens, but often stay concealed in the canopy. Their clear, flowing song may reveal one before it is seen.
