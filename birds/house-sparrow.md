# How to identify the House Sparrow (*Passer domesticus*)

A stocky, thick-billed sparrow that lives alongside people almost everywhere. Males have a grey crown, chestnut sides to the head, pale cheeks and a black bib that grows larger in the breeding season. Females and young birds are plain buff-brown with a pale stripe behind the eye and a streaked back. Usually seen in noisy groups around buildings, hedges and outdoor tables.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | House Sparrow |
| Scientific name | *Passer domesticus* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Passeridae |
| Genus | *Passer* |
