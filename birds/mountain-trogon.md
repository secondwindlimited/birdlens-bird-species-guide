# How to identify the Mountain Trogon (*Trogon mexicanus*)

Look high in montane pine or pine-oak woodland for a fairly large trogon, often sitting quietly among the upper branches. The male is easiest to identify by the broad white panels beneath its tail, set against black. Females are brown above and have a broken white eye-ring and bold black-and-white tail markings. The species lives in the highlands of Mexico, Guatemala and Honduras.
