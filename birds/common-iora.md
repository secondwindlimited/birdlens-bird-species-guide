# How to identify the Common Iora (*Aegithina tiphia*)

The Common Iora is a small, yellow bird with a slim, pointed bill and two pale bars across the wing. Breeding males stand out through their black crown and back; females and nonbreeding birds look greener above. Listen for a clear, repeated whistle. It is usually found in wooded country, scrub and farmland across South and Southeast Asia.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Common Iora |
| Scientific name | *Aegithina tiphia* |

| Genus | *Aegithina* |
