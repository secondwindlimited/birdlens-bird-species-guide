# How to identify the Eastern Paradise-Whydah (*Vidua paradisaea*)

Look for a breeding male with a chestnut breast, black upperparts and an extraordinary trailing tail of narrow feathers. Females and males outside the breeding season are much plainer-bodied but show a bold black-and-white face, dark bill and curved marking below the ear. This resident bird uses open country in eastern Africa, including savanna and grassland.
