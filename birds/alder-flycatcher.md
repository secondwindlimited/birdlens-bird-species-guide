# How to identify the Alder Flycatcher (*Empidonax alnorum*)

Alder Flycatchers are small, olive-backed birds with pale underparts, a fine white eye-ring and two noticeable white bars across each wing. They are most likely to be found in wet, bushy growth during the breeding season, especially where alders are present. Their rough, rolling song is a more dependable clue than appearance when distinguishing them from similar flycatchers.
