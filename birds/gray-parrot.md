# How to identify the Gray Parrot (*Psittacus erithacus*)

A large, mostly grey parrot with a pale face, black bill and red tail. The tail is bright red across most of its range, but more brick-coloured in the west. Gray Parrots live in equatorial Africa and are usually seen in pairs or small groups, in forest or at its edges. They are resident rather than migratory.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Gray Parrot |
| Scientific name | *Psittacus erithacus* |
| Order | Psittaciformes |
| Family | Psittacidae |
| Genus | *Psittacus* |
