# How to identify the Rufous-capped Antthrush (*Formicarius colma*)

The Rufous-capped Antthrush is a small, secretive bird of shaded South American forests. Look for a vivid rusty-orange crown above dark, subdued plumage and a short-tailed, ground-walking shape. It slips through leaf litter with its tail held up, so it is often heard before seen. Its clear, sustained trill grows louder toward the finish.

## What kind of bird is it?
