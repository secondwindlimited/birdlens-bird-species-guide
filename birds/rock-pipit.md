# How to identify the Rock Pipit (*Anthus petrosus*)

Look for a dark, subdued pipit on rocky shores, often among stones or on nearby short grass. Its upperparts are grey-brown, while the pale underside has conspicuous, soft-edged dark streaks. Dark legs help separate it from the pink-legged Meadow Pipit, which may feed in nearby coastal grassland. It walks with purpose and may give a slight tail pump.
