# How to identify the Large-tailed Antshrike (*Mackenziaena leachii*)

Look for a dark, long-tailed antbird with a small head and stout, hooked bill. Males are black with fine white marks; females show warm brown tones and pale spots and bars. It is usually encountered in pairs, moving through thick forest growth, often near bamboo. Its clear, rising-and-falling song may reveal a bird that stays difficult to see. The species is resident, though its population is declining.
