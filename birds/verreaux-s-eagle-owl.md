# How to identify the Verreaux's Eagle-Owl (*Ketupa lactea*)

A very large, grey owl with dark markings around its face, a pale bill and striking pink eyelids. Its deep, rough grunts can reveal it after dark. Look for it in tree-dotted savanna, thorn scrub and open woodland across much of sub-Saharan Africa, especially in the east and south. It stays in its range year-round.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Verreaux's Eagle-Owl |
| Scientific name | *Ketupa lactea* |
| Order | Strigiformes |
| Family | Strigidae |
| Genus | *Ketupa* |
