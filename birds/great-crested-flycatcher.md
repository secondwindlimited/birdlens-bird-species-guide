# How to identify the Great Crested Flycatcher (*Myiarchus crinitus*)

A fairly large, warm-brown flycatcher with a vivid yellow underside, gray throat and chest, and rusty tail. Its raised crown and broad shape help, but its loud, upward-slurred “queeep” is often the surest clue. Look for it in leafy woods across eastern North America in the breeding season; it spends the colder months from Mexico southward.
