# How to identify the Golden Eagle (*Aquila chrysaetos*)

Look for a very large, dark eagle with broad wings and a warm golden sheen on the back of its head and neck. That colour can be difficult to see when the bird is high overhead. Golden Eagles hunt across open country, often in remote areas and mountain landscapes. Young birds show white at the base of the tail and in the wings.
