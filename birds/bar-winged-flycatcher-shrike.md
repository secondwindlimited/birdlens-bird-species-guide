# How to identify the Bar-winged Flycatcher-shrike (*Hemipus picatus*)

A small, neat bird with a dark head and back, pale underparts, and a conspicuous white mark across each wing. White on the rump and outer tail also shows as it moves between branches. Look for it in forest, often among mixed-species flocks, where it catches insects around the middle levels of the canopy or in brief flights.
