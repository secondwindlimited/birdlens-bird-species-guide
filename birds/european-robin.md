# How to identify the European Robin (*Erithacus rubecula*)

A small, round bird with an orange-red face and breast edged in soft blue-grey, olive-brown upperparts and a whitish belly. Males and females look alike. Robins in Britain and Ireland are famously tame around gardeners, and both sexes sing through much of the winter to defend their territories.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | European Robin |
| Scientific name | *Erithacus rubecula* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Muscicapidae |
| Genus | *Erithacus* |
