# How to identify the Flame Robin (*Petroica phoenicea*)

The Flame Robin is a slender Australian robin, about 12–14 cm long. Adult males are easy to recognise by their orange-red breast and belly, slate-grey upperparts and broad white wing panels. Females are brown and less distinctive; pale wing marks help separate them from female Dusky Robins. Look for the species in open country and clearings in southeastern Australia and Tasmania.
