# How to identify the Bornean Bristlehead (*Pityriasis gymnocephala*)

Look for a stocky, mostly black bird moving through the forest canopy, with a large hooked bill and a striking bare yellow crown edged with short bristles. Red colours the head, neck, throat and thighs, while grey patches sit beside the eyes. It occurs only on Borneo, where it wanders in small groups through lowland and foothill forests.

| Common name | Bornean Bristlehead |
