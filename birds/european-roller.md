# How to identify the European Roller (*Coracias garrulus*)

The European Roller is a stocky, jay-sized bird with vivid blue plumage, an orange-brown back and dark flight feathers. It is easiest to spot sitting on a wire or bare branch in open country, or flying low with its bright colours flashing. It breeds across parts of Europe, North Africa and western Asia, then travels to Africa south of the Sahara for winter.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | European Roller |
| Scientific name | *Coracias garrulus* |
| Order | Coraciiformes |
| Family | Coraciidae |
| Genus | *Coracias* |
