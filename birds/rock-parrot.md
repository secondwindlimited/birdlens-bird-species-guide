# How to identify the Rock Parrot (*Neophema petrophila*)

The Rock Parrot is a small, lightly built bird with subdued olive-brown plumage, a yellow-washed belly and blue markings around the forehead and folded wing. Look for it close to the southern Australian shore, where it walks over bare ground or low scrub. Its sharp, repeated “zit” notes can reveal birds feeding out of view.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Rock Parrot |
| Scientific name | *Neophema petrophila* |
| Order | Psittaciformes |
| Family | Psittaculidae |
| Genus | *Neophema* |
