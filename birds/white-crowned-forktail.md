# How to identify the White-crowned Forktail (*Enicurus leschenaulti*)

Look for a large black-and-white forktail beside a shaded forest stream. Its white crown, plain black back, white rump and deeply notched tail create a distinctive pattern. It often stands on rocks or low branches beside rushing water, and may be heard before it is seen. Listen for high, ringing whistles or a rougher call. It occurs from the Indian subcontinent through mainland and island Southeast Asia.
