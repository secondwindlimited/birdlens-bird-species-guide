# How to identify the Yellow-breasted Chat (*Icteria virens*)

A long-tailed bird with a heavy bill, olive upperparts, yellow underparts and conspicuous white markings around the eyes. It often stays hidden in thick shrubs, but singing males may perch in the open during the breeding season. Look for it in brushy places across much of the United States and Mexico; in winter, it moves south into Mexico and Central America.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Yellow-breasted Chat |
| Scientific name | *Icteria virens* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Icteriidae |
| Genus | *Icteria* |
