# How to identify the Ivory Gull (*Pagophila eburnea*)

An adult Ivory Gull is white from head to tail, with black legs and a blue-green bill tipped orange-red. Its compact build and short legs give it a pigeon-like outline. Look for it around sea ice in the high Arctic, often alone or in loose groups; feeding birds may gather at a seal carcass. It rarely appears far south.
