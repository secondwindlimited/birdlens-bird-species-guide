# How to identify the Amazonian Barred-Woodcreeper (*Dendrocolaptes certhia*)

The Amazonian Barred-Woodcreeper is a tall, slender climber with a long tail and warm brown plumage, usually marked by faint dark crossbars. Its cinnamon-toned wings and tail, pale throat and brown-to-red bill help separate it from similar woodcreepers. Look for it in lowland Amazonian rainforest, often alone or in pairs near army-ant columns.
