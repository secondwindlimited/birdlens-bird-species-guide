# How to identify the Olive Ibis (*Bostrychia olivacea*)

The Olive Ibis is a dark, fairly small forest ibis with a ragged crest, blackish bare skin around the face and bright green tones on the shoulders. It is usually difficult to see because it is shy and scarce. Look for it in damp, thick rainforest, and listen for a short, two-part honking call. It is resident across much of tropical Africa.

## What kind of bird is it?
