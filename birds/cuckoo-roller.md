# How to identify the Cuckoo-roller (*Leptosomus discolor*)

The Cuckoo-roller is a large forest bird with a broad head, a small dark bill and short legs. Males have a grey-and-white underside and a glossy green-purple back; females are brown and spotted below. It is usually found in the trees of Madagascar and the Comoros, but may also be seen circling above the canopy while giving a loud, wavering call.
