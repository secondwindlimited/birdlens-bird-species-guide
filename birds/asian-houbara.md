# How to identify the Asian Houbara (*Chlamydotis macqueenii*)

Look for a large, pale, sandy-brown bustard with a white neck marked by dark stripes. In flight, its long wings show bold dark areas and a pale patch near the tips. It lives in dry open country, including steppe and semi-desert, and is most likely to be encountered in its Asian range or on migration through parts of Pakistan and western India.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Asian Houbara |
| Scientific name | *Chlamydotis macqueenii* |

| Genus | *Chlamydotis* |
