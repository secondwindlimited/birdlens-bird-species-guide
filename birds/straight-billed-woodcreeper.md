# How to identify the Straight-billed Woodcreeper (*Dendroplex picus*)

Look for a slim, medium-sized brown woodcreeper with a strikingly straight, pale bill and a finely marked head and chest. Its back is largely plain, while the rump and tail are reddish. It lives across northern South America, especially in open woodland, scrub and trees beside waterways. Watch for it alone or with a mate, climbing trunks and limbs.
