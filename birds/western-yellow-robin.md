# How to identify the Western Yellow Robin (*Eopsaltria griseogularis*)

A compact robin of southwestern Australia, the Western Yellow Robin is recognised by its grey head and back, pale throat, grey breast and vivid yellow belly. It often waits on a low branch or tree trunk before dropping to the ground for prey. Look for its yellow rump and pale wing marking as it flies; it is a resident of forests and woodlands.
