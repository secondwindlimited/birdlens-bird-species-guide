# How to identify the Magellanic Diving-Petrel (*Pelecanoides magellani*)

A tiny, compact seabird with dark upperparts, pale underparts and a conspicuous white curve beside its black-capped head. It flies just above the waves on quick, steady wingbeats. Look for it in southern Chilean fjords, channels and the Strait of Magellan, where it may be seen from shore or a ferry; it is much less often found far offshore.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Magellanic Diving-Petrel |
| Scientific name | *Pelecanoides magellani* |

| Genus | *Pelecanoides* |
