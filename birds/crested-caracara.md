# How to identify the Crested Caracara (*Caracara plancus*)

A large, long-legged falcon with a dark cap and body, pale neck, and bare facial skin that ranges from orange-red to pink. In flight, look for pale patches in the wings and tail. Crested Caracaras often walk across the ground or sit in view on posts and poles. They live in open country from the southern United States through Central and South America.
