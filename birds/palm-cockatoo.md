# How to identify the Palm Cockatoo (*Probosciger aterrimus*)

A very large, black cockatoo with a towering crest, an outsized bill and bare red skin on its cheeks. It is found in rainforest and woodland in New Guinea and on Queensland’s Cape York Peninsula. Listen for short, sharp whistles or squeaks. Males may also make a loud display by striking a stick against a tree.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Palm Cockatoo |
| Scientific name | *Probosciger aterrimus* |
| Order | Psittaciformes |
| Family | Cacatuidae |
| Genus | *Probosciger* |
