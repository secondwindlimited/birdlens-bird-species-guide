# How to identify the Crested Satinbird (*Cnemophilus macgregorii*)

A plump, colourful bird about 24 cm long, the Crested Satinbird is found in high forests in eastern and southeastern New Guinea. Males have a blue eye and dark underparts; their backs are vivid yellow in the southeast and scarlet elsewhere. Females are olive-toned, with orange-brown wings and tail. Look for it in thick mountain-forest vegetation.
