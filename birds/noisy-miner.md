# How to identify the Noisy Miner (*Manorina melanocephala*)

Look for a sturdy grey honeyeater with a dark cap, bright yellow bill and a bare yellow patch behind the eye. Noisy Miners often call loudly and gather in groups that chase other birds from their territory. They are found across much of eastern Australia, including open eucalypt country, farms, parks and suburban gardens.
