# How to identify the Madagascar Plover (*Anarhynchus thoracicus*)

The Madagascar Plover is a small shorebird with a dark band across its upper breast and striking black-and-white markings on its head. It occurs only in Madagascar, mainly beside the island’s western and southern shores. Look for it on very short grass near coastal wetlands, where it runs over the ground and picks up food. Its population is declining.

| Common name | Madagascar Plover |
