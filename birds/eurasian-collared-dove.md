# How to identify the Eurasian Collared-Dove (*Streptopelia decaocto*)

A pale, slender dove with a slim black mark curving around the back of its neck. Its long, square-ended tail and distinctive pattern underneath help separate it from other doves. Listen for its repeated, three-part coo. It is often seen in pairs around farms and suburbs, and occurs widely across Eurasia and northern Africa, as well as in introduced populations in the Americas.
