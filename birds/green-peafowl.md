# How to identify the Green Peafowl (*Pavo muticus*)

The Green Peafowl is a very large, long-legged bird with a shimmering green neck and an upright crest in both sexes. Males carry a long, eye-spotted train, while females have a much shorter tail. It lives in parts of Southeast Asia, often in forest near water, and may also be seen in open country along forest edges.
