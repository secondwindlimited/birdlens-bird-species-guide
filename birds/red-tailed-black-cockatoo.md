# How to identify the Red-tailed Black-Cockatoo (*Calyptorhynchus banksii*)

A large, dark cockatoo with a rounded crest and red or orange markings in its tail. Males are mostly black, while females show yellow-orange markings on the body and tail. Look for it across much of Australia, especially in the drier north, where birds may gather in noisy flocks and feed on the ground.

## What kind of bird is it?
