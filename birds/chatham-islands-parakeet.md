# How to identify the Chatham Islands Parakeet (*Cyanoramphus forbesi*)

Look for a vivid green, long-tailed parakeet with a yellow patch at the front of its crown and a narrow red forehead stripe. Forbes’ parakeet lives only on two small islands in the Chatham group, where it feeds in woodland and on flowering flax. It may also be seen walking and scratching at the ground, sometimes in small seasonal flocks.
