# How to identify the Black Rail (*Laterallus jamaicensis*)

The Black Rail is a tiny, sparrow-sized marsh bird with dark slate plumage, a bright red eye and fine white marks on its wings. It is usually heard before it is seen: its ringing, rattling song is often given after dark. The species occurs in scattered wetlands across the Americas and stays low among dense marsh plants.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Black Rail |
| Scientific name | *Laterallus jamaicensis* |
| Order | Gruiformes |
| Family | Rallidae |
| Genus | *Laterallus* |
