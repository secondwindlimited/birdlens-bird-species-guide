# How to identify the Whitehead's Broadbill (*Calyptomena whiteheadi*)

Whitehead's broadbill is a bulky, vivid-green bird of Borneo’s northern and central mountains. Look for dark marks across the back and wings, a heavy black throat patch in males, and a rounded feather crest that partly hides the bill. Females are less boldly marked. It is usually encountered alone in mature upland forest, though several birds may gather where trees are bearing fruit.
