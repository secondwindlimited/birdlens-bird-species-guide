# How to identify the Amazonian Antshrike (*Thamnophilus amazonicus*)

A medium-sized Amazonian antbird with a striking difference between the sexes: males are mainly gray and black, while females show warm rusty or cinnamon tones on the head and breast. Look for it moving slowly through the lower and middle layers of rainforest, especially near rivers or thick vines. Its quick, trogon-like song can help separate it from other antshrikes.
