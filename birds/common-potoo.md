# How to identify the Common Potoo (*Nyctibius griseus*)

Look for a broad-headed, big-eyed bird whose mottled grey and brown plumage resembles weathered bark. By day, a Common Potoo may sit upright at the tip of a broken trunk, where it is easy to overlook. It hunts flying insects after dark, usually from a perch in open woodland, a forest margin or a riverside stand of trees.

## What kind of bird is it?
