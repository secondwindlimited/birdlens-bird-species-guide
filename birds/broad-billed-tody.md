# How to identify the Broad-billed Tody (*Todus subulatus*)

On Hispaniola, look for a tiny, rounded bird with a broad bill, green back and wings, and a red throat. Dark eyes help separate it from the similar Narrow-billed Tody, which has pale eyes. Broad-billed Todys are usually found at lower elevations, in places such as dry scrub, coffee plantations, pine woods, and mangroves. Their rough, repeated calls are another useful clue.
