# How to identify the Variable Oystercatcher (*Haematopus unicolor*)

A large, solidly built shorebird with a long red bill and pink legs is a good first clue. Adults may be entirely dark or show varying amounts of white. Look for it around New Zealand’s coasts, where it stays year-round; it also feeds in coastal grassland and fields. Pied birds are bigger than South Island Pied Oystercatchers and have less sharply divided black-and-white plumage.
