# How to identify the Pink-necked Green-Pigeon (*Treron vernans*)

The male is easy to pick out by his pink neck, grey-blue head and orange breast. Females are mostly green, with yellow-green underparts. Both sexes show black and yellow in the wings. Look for this tree-dwelling pigeon in lowland coastal woodland, mangroves, parks and gardens across Southeast Asia and nearby islands. It often feeds or roosts in groups.
