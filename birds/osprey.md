# How to identify the Osprey (*Pandion haliaetus*)

Look for a large fishing raptor with a white head and belly, dark upperparts and a strong stripe through each eye. Its long wings bend at the wrists, making a shallow M as it glides above water. Ospreys are often seen around lakes, rivers and coasts, where they may hover or drop feet-first to seize fish. Their bulky stick nests are built on raised structures near water.
