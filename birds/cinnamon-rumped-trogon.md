# How to identify the Cinnamon-rumped Trogon (*Harpactes orrhophaeus*)

The Cinnamon-rumped Trogon is a shy forest bird, often noticed only when it gives a short run of falling notes. A male has a black head, warm brown upperparts and bright red underparts; the rump is muted brown-orange rather than scarlet. Females are duller, with yellowish underparts. Look for it in the lower growth of forests in Thailand, Peninsular Malaysia and Borneo.
