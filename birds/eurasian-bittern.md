# How to identify the Eurasian Bittern (*Botaurus stellaris*)

A large, secretive heron of reed-filled wetlands, the Eurasian Bittern is mottled buff and brown, with a dark crown and a tawny face and neck. Its deep, booming call may give away a bird hidden from view. Watch for one flying low over reeds, often around dawn or dusk; most of the time it stays concealed in dense vegetation.
