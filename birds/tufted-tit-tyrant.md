# How to identify the Tufted Tit-Tyrant (*Anairetes parulus*)

Look for a tiny, restless gray-brown bird with a split-looking, upswept black crest and striking pale eyes. It often keeps its tail raised while moving through bushes, usually with a partner. Tufted Tit-Tyrants use scrub and open woodland across the Andes, from Colombia south to Chile and Argentina; in Chile, they also occur close to sea level.

|---|---|
