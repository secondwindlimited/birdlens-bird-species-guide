# How to identify the Common Kingfisher (*Alcedo atthis*)

A small, short-tailed waterside bird with a large head and a long, dagger-like bill. The upperparts are brilliant blue-green with a streak of paler electric blue down the back, and the underparts are orange. It is most often seen as a blue flash flying low and fast over a river, accompanied by a sharp, high whistle.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Common Kingfisher |
| Scientific name | *Alcedo atthis* |
| Order | Coraciiformes |
| Family | Alcedinidae |
| Genus | *Alcedo* |
