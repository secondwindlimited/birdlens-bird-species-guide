# How to identify the Black-bellied Whistling-Duck (*Dendrocygna autumnalis*)

A tall, slim duck with a long neck and pink legs, the Black-bellied Whistling-Duck is easy to pick out when adults show their chestnut body, dark underside and bright bill. In flight, a wide white panel stands out on each wing. Look for flocks around marshes, lakes, flooded fields and swamps across much of the Americas; in the United States it is most often found in the south.
