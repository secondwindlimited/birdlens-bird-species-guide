# How to identify the Black Stork (*Ciconia nigra*)

A large, dark stork with a white underside, red bill and red legs. In good light, its upper plumage shows a greenish or purple gloss. It is usually found around wooded wetlands during the breeding season, but may appear in open fields or beside lakes and rivers while travelling or in its winter range. It is generally shyer than the familiar White Stork.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Black Stork |
| Scientific name | *Ciconia nigra* |
| Order | Ciconiiformes |
| Family | Ciconiidae |
| Genus | *Ciconia* |
