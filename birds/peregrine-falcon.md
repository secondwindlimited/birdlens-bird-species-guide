# How to identify the Peregrine Falcon (*Falco peregrinus*)

A powerful, crow-sized falcon with long pointed wings and a fairly short tail. Adults are blue-grey above and finely barred below, with a dark hood and a broad dark 'moustache' on each side of a white throat. It hunts other birds in the air and is the fastest animal known, exceeding 300 km/h in a hunting dive.
