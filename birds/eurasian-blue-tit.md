# How to identify the Eurasian Blue Tit (*Cyanistes caeruleus*)

A small, lively tit with a blue crown, white cheeks, a dark eye-stripe and yellow belly is easy to pick out. It is smaller and more restless than a Great Tit, though the two often share sites. Look for it in leafy woods, parks, gardens and farmland hedges; it also regularly comes to feeders and may use nest boxes.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
