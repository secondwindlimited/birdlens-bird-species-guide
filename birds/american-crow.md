# How to identify the American Crow (*Corvus brachyrhynchos*)

Look for a large bird with glossy black plumage, a black bill and legs, and a familiar repeated caw. American Crows are at home in open country, wetlands, open woods, parks, and towns across much of the United States and Canada. They often gather in groups, with especially large flocks forming at communal night roosts outside the nesting season.
