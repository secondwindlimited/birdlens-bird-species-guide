# How to identify the Eurasian Wryneck (*Jynx torquilla*)

A small, slim woodpecker with finely mottled brown and grey plumage and a dark stripe running along the crown and back. It is often hard to spot among its surroundings. Look for it in open farmland, orchards, gardens or parkland with scattered trees. It often feeds on the ground, searching for ants, and makes deliberate, jerky hops.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Eurasian Wryneck |
| Scientific name | *Jynx torquilla* |
