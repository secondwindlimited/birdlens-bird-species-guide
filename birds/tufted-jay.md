# How to identify the Tufted Jay (*Cyanocorax dickeyi*)

The Tufted Jay is a large, boldly marked jay with a tall black crest, yellow eyes and a broad white end to its tail. Its black-and-white face stands out against blue-violet wings and back. It is usually encountered in small, moving parties high in pine-oak forest in the Sierra Madre Occidental of north-western Mexico, sometimes among Steller’s Jays.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Tufted Jay |
| Scientific name | *Cyanocorax dickeyi* |

| Genus | *Cyanocorax* |
