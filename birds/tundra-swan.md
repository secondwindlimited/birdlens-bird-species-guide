# How to identify the Tundra Swan (*Cygnus columbianus*)

A very large, white swan with a dark bill and black feet, the Tundra Swan is best separated from other swans by the yellow markings on its bill. North American birds usually have a small yellow area beside the eye; Eurasian birds show more yellow overall. Look for it on Arctic breeding waters or in winter flocks on wetlands, coasts and harvested fields.
