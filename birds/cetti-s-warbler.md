# How to identify the Cetti's Warbler (*Cettia cetti*)

A compact, warm-brown warbler with a broad tail, pale underparts and a fine light line above each dark eye. It is usually heard before it is seen: its loud, abrupt song comes from deep cover, often near water. Look for it in dense waterside growth such as marsh scrub, though in winter it also uses thick vegetation in drier places.
