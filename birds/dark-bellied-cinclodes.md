# How to identify the Dark-bellied Cinclodes (*Cinclodes patagonicus*)

A dark, fairly large cinclodes with a long, curved bill, clear white stripes over and below the eye, and fine pale marks across the breast. Look for it near water in southern South America: on rocky shores, beside rivers and lakes, or in nearby fields and lawns. It stays in its range throughout the year and may raise a wing while singing.
