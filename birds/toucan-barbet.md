# How to identify the Toucan Barbet (*Semnornis ramphastinus*)

A compact, powerful-looking bird with a dark crown, pale mark behind the eye, slate-gray throat and vivid red lower chest is unmistakable in its range. Its heavy yellow-and-green bill has a dark tip. Look for it high in humid Andean forests in northwestern Ecuador and southwestern Colombia, often alongside its mate. Paired birds call together with repeated, nasal notes.
