# How to identify the White-bellied Storm-Petrel (*Fregetta grallaria*)

A small black-and-white seabird, the White-bellied Storm-Petrel has dark upperparts and a pale belly and rump. In flight, its relatively short legs usually reach about to the tail tip, and it lacks the dark stripe down the belly seen on a Black-bellied Storm-Petrel. Look for it over the open Southern Hemisphere oceans, where it feeds at the water’s surface.
