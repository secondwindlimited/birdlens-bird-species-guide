# How to identify the Crested Treeswift (*Hemiprocne coronata*)

The Crested Treeswift is a slender, grey bird with swept-back wings, a crest and a deeply notched tail. Look for the male’s rusty-orange face or the female’s dark face markings. It spends much of its time flying in loose circles above wooded country, but may sit upright on a high, bare branch. It is a resident of South and Southeast Asia.

## What kind of bird is it?
