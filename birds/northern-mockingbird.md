# How to identify the Northern Mockingbird (*Mimus polyglottos*)

Look for a gray bird with a slim body, a long tail and conspicuous white panels on its wings, especially when it flies. Northern Mockingbirds are often seen in parks, gardens and other open places with shrubs or small trees. Their varied, repeated songs may include imitations of other birds and even familiar mechanical sounds.
