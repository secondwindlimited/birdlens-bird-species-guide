# How to identify the Black-legged Seriema (*Chunga burmeisteri*)

The Black-legged Seriema is a tall, ash-grey ground bird with long legs, a dark bill and dark feet. Its shaggy-looking feathers and loud, barking calls help identify it. It lives in the dry Chaco region of South America, usually among thorny scrub or woodland. Although large, it can be hard to spot in dense cover.

| Common name | Black-legged Seriema |
| Scientific name | *Chunga burmeisteri* |

| Family | Cariamidae |
| Genus | *Chunga* |
