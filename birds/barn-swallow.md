# How to identify the Barn Swallow (*Hirundo rustica*)

A slender, graceful swallow with glossy blue-black upperparts, a rusty-red forehead and throat, and a long, deeply forked tail with wire-like outer feathers. It hunts flying insects low over fields and water and builds a cup of mud pellets inside barns, sheds and under bridges. It is the most widespread swallow in the world.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Barn Swallow |
| Scientific name | *Hirundo rustica* |
| Order | Passeriformes |
| Family | Hirundinidae |
| Genus | *Hirundo* |
