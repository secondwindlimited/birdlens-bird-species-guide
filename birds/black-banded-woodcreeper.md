# How to identify the Black-banded Woodcreeper (*Dendrocolaptes picumnus*)

A large, slender woodcreeper with a strong straight bill, pale lines on the head and breast, and bold dark bars across the lower body. Its chestnut wings and long tail stand out as it climbs through tree trunks. It is found from southern Mexico through Central America and much of South America, usually in mature forest, where it is often seen alone or in pairs.
