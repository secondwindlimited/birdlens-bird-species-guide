# How to identify the Northern Bald Ibis (*Geronticus eremita*)

Look for a large, dark ibis with a bare red face, a long curved red bill and loose neck plumes. Its glossy feathers may flash bronze or violet in good light. The surviving wild colonies are in southwestern Morocco, where the birds nest on cliffs and feed in open, dry country nearby.

## What kind of bird is it?

|---|---|
| Common name | Northern Bald Ibis |
| Scientific name | *Geronticus eremita* |
| Order | Pelecaniformes |

| Genus | *Geronticus* |
