# How to identify the Noisy Pitta (*Pitta versicolor*)

The Noisy Pitta is a compact, brightly coloured bird with a chestnut crown, green wings, yellow underparts and a blue patch at the shoulder. It is often easier to hear than to spot as it searches the forest floor for food. Look for it in rainforest along eastern Australia, or in coastal forest and mangroves during the non-breeding season.

## What kind of bird is it?

| | |
|---|---|
| Common name | Noisy Pitta |
| Scientific name | *Pitta versicolor* |
