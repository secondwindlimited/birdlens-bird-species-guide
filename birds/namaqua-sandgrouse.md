# How to identify the Namaqua Sandgrouse (*Pterocles namaqua*)

Look for a compact, ground-dwelling sandgrouse with a pointed tail, narrow wings and quick, straight flight. Males show a warm buff head and two contrasting chest stripes; females are more heavily flecked in brown and white. It occurs in dry country across southern Africa, especially the Kalahari, and may gather at water around dawn or near sunset.
