# How to identify the White-fronted Tern (*Sterna striata*)

Look for a medium-sized tern with a pale grey back, white body and a deeply forked tail. Adults have a dark cap and slender bill, with a narrow white gap between them. White-fronted Terns are most often seen along New Zealand’s coasts, sometimes gathered in large flocks; some young birds move to southeastern Australia in winter.
