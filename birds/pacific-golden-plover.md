# How to identify the Pacific Golden-Plover (*Pluvialis fulva*)

Look for a slim, long-legged plover with gold-flecked upperparts and a small dark bill. In breeding dress, black covers the face and underparts, edged by a white line along the neck; outside that season it is browner and paler below. It is most often encountered on Asian and Pacific wintering grounds, including beaches and mudflats, though it also uses open inland sites.
